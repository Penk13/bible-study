Romans · Message 38

Life and Death in Romans Five through Eight

The two trees of Genesis run as two lines through Romans 5–8. God's economy is not about good or evil but life or death, and we stay on the line of life by the new habit of living in the spirit.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Trees, Two Lines: The tree of knowledge issues in death; its seed ends in the lake of fire, and the tree of life ends in the New Jerusalem.
  • Through Romans 5–8: Reign of death and reign in life; newness of life; "this death"; a fourfold life in chapter 8.
  • Life, Not Good: Good and evil belong to the same tree; one can be good, even submissive, and still dead.
  • Life in Spirit, Death in Flesh: Both trees are now inside us; we switch to one or the other.
  • A New Habit: Drop the old habit of living by ourselves and stay in the spirit ().

The Two Lines

The tree of death is subtle: it is named for knowledge, good, and evil, three "sisters" that together bring forth death (). The seeds sown in Genesis are harvested in Revelation: death is cast into the lake of fire (), and life flows as a river in the city (). In between, both lines run through Romans 5–8.

Living, Not Merely Right

Neither Genesis 2, Revelation, nor Romans 5–8 speaks of husbands loving or wives submitting; they speak of life and death. A woman in the cemetery never argues, yet God does not want a dead submission. The mind set on the spirit is not called "submission" but "life and peace" (). A noisy kindergarten is better than an orderly graveyard.

Setting the Mind

Minding the flesh is not only worldly entertainment; resolving to love your wife, or worrying over tomorrow, sets the mind on the hopeless self. Instead, say "Lord, I can do nothing apart from You" and rest in Him today.

A New Habit

After electricity was installed, the young Witness Lee kept lighting the oil lamp out of habit. Christ, the heavenly electricity, is installed in our spirit, yet we still live by ourselves. Life and death are as near as a switch: we do not lose our temper and then lose the spirit; a thin insulation cuts us off first, and then we fail. In the spirit we are spontaneously dead to sin and alive to God ().

🪔

The Old Habit

Living by ourselves, resolving to do good, falling into death.

💡

The New Habit

Switching on the spirit; by the Spirit putting to death the practices of the body ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does the Bible put good on the same tree as evil?Three sisters.

One Source: Knowledge, good, and evil come from the tree whose issue is death ().

God's Concern: His economy asks whether we are living, not whether we are right.

Can a submissive wife or a loving husband still be dead?The cemetery.

Yes: Good behavior produced by self-effort belongs to the tree of knowledge.

Life and Peace: What God wants is the living expression that comes from a mind set on the spirit ().

What is the fourfold life of Romans 8?From Spirit to body.

Four Places: The divine Spirit, our human spirit (), our mind (8:6), and our mortal body ().

Still Death: Death remains in us until Revelation 20, so we must choose life daily.

I lost my temper. Where did it really start?Insulation.

Cut Off First: Some insulation already separated us from the spirit; the temper only revealed it.

The Remedy: Not a resolution but the habit of staying in the spirit, where life swallows death ().
