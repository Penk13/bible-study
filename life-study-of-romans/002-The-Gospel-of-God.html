The Gospel of God

Romans is the fifth gospel—unveiling the subjective Christ as the indwelling Spirit of life. Christ was designated the Son of God out of resurrection as the Prototype, and through the obedience of faith, God is legally bound by His righteousness to save us to the uttermost.

The Fifth Gospel: The Objective Christ vs. The Subjective Christ

Why do we need the book of Romans when we already have Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John? The four Gospels reveal an objective Savior walking on earth before His death. Romans reveals the subjective Savior who has become the indwelling Spirit of life in resurrection (; ).

The Four Gospels

Christ in the Flesh (Objective)

  • Realm: After incarnation, before resurrection.
  • Location: Among the disciples, outside of their being.
  • Focus: Earthly walk, teachings, and redemption by His blood on the cross (; ).
Romans: The Fifth Gospel

Christ as the Spirit (Subjective)

  • Realm: After death and glorious resurrection.
  • Location: Within our spirit as the Spirit of life ().
  • Focus: Imparting divine life, sanctifying, transforming, and building up the one Body!

The Two Steps of Christ's Process: The Carnation Seed Analogy

In , Paul unveils the two successive steps through which Jesus Christ was processed to become the Son of God with both divinity and humanity:

🌱

The Seed Sown (Jesus in the Flesh)

Before it is sown, a tiny carnation seed looks plain and ordinary. Divinity was hidden within the lowly shell of Jesus of Nazareth for 33½ years.

🌸

The Blossom (Resurrection Designation)

When sown into the earth and blossoming, everyone recognizes: “This is a carnation!” Resurrection is Christ's blossoming—His humanity was uplifted, sanctified, and designated as the Son of God!

Romans 1:3–4

The Prototype: Jesus Christ

Jesus had human flesh without and the Spirit of holiness within. Through death and resurrection, His humanity was designated into the divine sonship.

Romans 8:29–30

The Mass Production: The Many Sons

We also have human flesh without and the Spirit of the Son within ()! We are undergoing the same process to be fully designated in glory as sons of God!

Preaching and Receiving the Gospel

How is the gospel of God effectively communicated and received according to Paul’s standard in Romans?

🔥

1. In Spirit ()

Paul refers to our human spirit 11 times in Romans! Preaching requires exercising our regenerated spirit to touch men's conscience—not using psychological manipulation or worldly gimmicks.

🙏

2. By Prayer ()

Prayer is far more vital than human effort. Without unceasing intercession for souls, gospel preaching is completely powerless and fruitless.

⚡

3. With Eagerness ()

If the gospel does not burn in you, it will never kindle another heart. Fervency in spirit () and genuine tears of love produce real repentance in sinners.

The Righteousness of God: The Mighty Legal Bond

Why is the gospel the power of God unto salvation ()? Because in it the righteousness of God is revealed (). Consider the crucial legal distinction between Love/Grace and Righteousness:

Divine Attribute Scripture Basis Legal Relation to God's Law The Believer's Assurance
God’s Love Not legally compulsory; no law forces anyone to love. The motive of salvation.
God’s Grace A free gift; not legally mandated by a judicial court. The means and enjoyment of salvation.
God’s Righteousness ; Strictly legal! Christ has fulfilled all the righteous requirements of the law by shedding His blood! Unshakable foundation! God is legally BOUND by His own righteousness to forgive and save us!

📖 The Extract of Romans: Habakkuk 2:4 Quoted

“The just shall have life and live by faith” (; ):
1. Being Just: Justification in God's way (Chapters 1—5).
2. Having Life: Impartation of the Spirit of life (Chapters 5—8).
3. Living: Practicing the Body life in the local churches (Chapters 12—16).

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Paul designate Romans as a "gospel" (the fifth gospel), and how does the subjective Christ in Romans contrast with the objective Christ of the four Gospels?Contrast Christ in the flesh among the disciples before resurrection with Christ as the indwelling Spirit of life in resurrection (; ).

The Contrast: In Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, Christ lived in the flesh before His death and resurrection. He was a wonderful, objective Savior who walked among the disciples, healed the sick, and taught them, but He was not yet in them. If we only possess the four Gospels, our Christian experience remains outward and objective.

The Fifth Gospel: Romans is the gospel of the resurrected Christ as the Spirit of life (). He is no longer confined to Palestine; through resurrection He has become the indwelling Spirit living in our spirit (). Romans provides the deep, subjective reality of Christ living in His believers.

Explain the analogy of the carnation seed. How was Jesus designated the Son of God out of resurrection, and how is He our Prototype?Reflect on and . Why did the Son of God need to be designated if He was already divine in eternity?

The Carnation Seed Analogy: A small carnation seed sown into the earth looks dry and indistinct. But when it grows and blossoms, its true nature is unveiled—the blossom is its designation. During His 33½ years on earth, Jesus possessed divine glory concealed within mortal flesh. Through death and resurrection, He "blossomed," uplifting and sanctifying humanity into the divine sonship (; ).

The Prototype & Mass Production: Before incarnation, God had only one divine Son. In resurrection, Christ became the Firstborn Son possessing both divinity and glorified humanity. He is the Prototype (). We, having human flesh without and the Spirit within (), are the mass production being conformed to His image to be designated sons of God in glory ()!

Why does Paul emphasize serving God "in my spirit in the gospel" (Romans 1:9)? How does this eliminate human gimmicks in Christian service?Consider the contrast between dead religious traditions and the exercise of our regenerated human spirit (; ).

Serving in the Spirit: In Romans, Paul argues against empty religious forms, dead letters, and physical rituals (; ). God can only be touched, served, and expressed in our regenerated human spirit indwelt by the Holy Spirit. Paul references the human spirit 11 times in this epistle.

No Gimmicks: Worldly techniques, psychological tricks, entertainment, and marketing gimmicks cannot impart divine life. Effective gospel preaching requires that we exercise our spirit, bathe our labor in fervent prayer, and speak with heartfelt tears and eagerness (). Only a spirit on fire can kindle fire in others.

Why is God legally bound by His righteousness to save and forgive us, and how does this provide absolute assurance of salvation?Contrast God's love () and grace () with God's righteousness (; ).

The Legal Distinction: Love is an inward emotion; grace is an unmerited favor. Neither love nor grace is bound by law—no judge can force a person to love or grant a gift. But righteousness is strictly legal, bound to the court of divine law.

The Mighty Bond: Because the Lord Jesus Christ went to the cross and shed His blood, He paid our entire debt and completely fulfilled every righteous demand of God's holy law (). Therefore, when we confess our sins and believe into Christ, God is legally obligated by His own righteousness to forgive us! As declares: “He is faithful and just [righteous] to forgive us our sins.” God would be unrighteous if He refused to save a believer whose debt has been fully settled by Christ's blood!
