Romans · Message 45

Saved in Life from Self-Likeness (2)

Christ was designated the Son of God as the divine essence of holiness saturated His humanity through death and resurrection. The same essence is now in us, and through all things God is saturating us, sanctifying the many sons and bringing them into glory.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Designated: Christ's glory was hidden in His flesh until resurrection marked Him out as the Son of God ().
  • Spirit of Holiness: Not the Person of the Holy Spirit here, but Christ's divine essence, the fullness of the Godhead ().
  • Concealed Again: That essence now hides in our humanity; God works all things to process us ().
  • Organic, Not Ethical: Like digestion, saturation happens whether we sense it or not.
  • Many Sons into Glory: The Sanctifier and the sanctified are all of one ().

The Prototype Designated

No one could see through Jesus' flesh to His divinity. Holiness filled His spirit but had not yet permeated His body. Death and resurrection saturated His whole humanity with the holy essence, and He became the firstborn Son.

🌿

According to the Flesh

The seed of David, His human essence ().

👑

According to the Spirit of Holiness

Designated the Son of God in power out of resurrection ().

Same Principle, Many Sons

Romans 8:29 begins with "because," joining it to 8:28: all things work together so that the many sons may be designated as the One was. In the church we are part of the processing of one another, and this needs His mercy and grace.

Organic Transformation

Outward correction is an ethical, religious concept. God's way is spiritual metabolism: permeation and saturation with Christ Himself. Desperate prayer may aid our "digestion," but the saturation goes on; simply stay in the church life and become precious stone.

📏

Outward Adjustment

Teachings, regulations, and forms that correct behavior.

🧬

Inward Saturation

The holy essence permeating us layer by layer, part by part.

Bringing Many Sons into Glory

The Father is leading many sons into glory through the Sanctifier (). Born of the same Father in resurrection (), He and we share one life and nature, so He is not ashamed to call us brothers ().

An Indelible Injection

Like an injection received in a clinic, the holy essence cannot be removed once it has saturated us. Even a backslider remains somewhat saturated; no one can undo what God has wrought.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does "designated" mean for Christ, and for us?Hidden, then manifested.

For Christ: Resurrection manifested the Son hidden in His flesh ().

For Us: The Son is hidden in our humanity; saturation will manifest us as sons conformed to His image.

Why is outward improvement not God's way to deal with self-likeness?Ethical vs. organic.

Ethical Change: It only adjusts the surface; the self remains.

Organic Change: Christ as the holy essence replaces the self's likeness from within, like food being assimilated.

How are the saints in the church part of my processing?Processing one another.

All Things: The brothers and sisters are among the "all things" God uses ().

Mutual: I am part of their process too; bearing one another needs grace.

What comfort is there in the holy essence being indelible?He finishes His work.

Assurance: What God has wrought in us stays, even through failure.

Destination: The Sanctifier is bringing the many sons into glory ().
