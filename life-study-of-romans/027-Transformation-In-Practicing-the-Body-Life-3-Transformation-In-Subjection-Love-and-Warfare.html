Romans · Message 27

Transformation in Practicing the Body Life (3) — Transformation in Subjection, Love, and Warfare

The Body life rests on a normal Christian life (12:9–13:14): toward others, God, ourselves, and our enemies, and in subjection, love, and the warfare against the flesh by putting on Christ.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Normal Life: Without a normal human living, the church life lacks its base. Romans 12:9–21 is the Bible's finest portrait of it.
  • Whole Being: A presented body, a renewed mind, and a burning spirit ().
  • Subjection: Our natural character rebels; submitting to God-appointed authority shows transformation ().
  • Love: Love fulfills the law, but it is the expression of an inward life, not mere behavior ().
  • Warfare: Christ Himself is the weapons of light. To put Him on is to live by Him and live Him out against the lusts of the flesh.

The Living of a Normal Life

After the presenting of the body, the renewing of the mind, and the exercising of gifts comes a fourth point: daily living. The church needs living stones full of affection, not stone faces; we must learn to rejoice and weep with others (). Many of these verses are nearly proverbs, worth memorizing.

Toward Others

Love & Honor

Unfeigned love, being first to show honor, hospitality, and being led away to lowly things ().

Toward God

Burning in Spirit

Not slothful, burning in spirit, serving the Lord as a slave who has no freedom of his own.

Toward Ourselves

Rejoicing in Hope

Enduring tribulation, persevering in prayer, abhorring evil and cleaving to good ().

Toward Enemies

Overcoming Evil

Bless, do not avenge, feed the hungry enemy, and live in peace if possible ().

Subjection and Love

By birth our response to authority is "No." To be subject to the authorities, out of conscience and not merely for fear of wrath, rendering taxes, fear, and honor, requires growth in life. Likewise, the commandment to love sums up all the others, yet our natural life is not one of God's love. Trying to love without the supply of life does not work; we need God's nature of love wrought into us.

Putting On Christ

The night is far spent (). The salvation now nearer is its last stage, the transfiguration of our body at the Lord's return (). So we wake up, cast off the works of darkness, and put on the weapons of light. Placing verses 12 and 14 together shows that these weapons are the Lord Jesus Christ. This battle is not against the powers in the air but against our lusts, as the Spirit against the flesh ().

Romans 7:23

Law Against Law

The law of sin in the flesh wars against the law of the mind, without the Spirit, and we are defeated.

Romans 13:14

Christ Against the Flesh

We live by Christ and live Him out, and He becomes our weapon. We make no provision to feed the flesh.

Stay Away from the Well

Walking near a deep well may not end in a fall today, but likely will someday. Do not trust your strength; keep away from what feeds the flesh.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does it mean to put on Christ when we are already in Him?Baptized into Him, yet still to put Him on.

Position: Through baptism we are already in Christ.

Practice: To put Him on is to live by Him and express Him in daily life; that living is our weapon against the lusts ().

How is love by transformation different from love by effort?Expression, not behavior.

Effort: Love attempted from our natural life soon fails, for that life does not have God's nature of love.

Life: As the Spirit transforms us, love is expressed spontaneously and the law is fulfilled ().

How can a person be in the church bodily yet still not practice the Body life?Body, mind, spirit.

Unrenewed Mind: One may come with the body but bring old concepts, traditions, and natural cleverness that trouble the church.

Cold Spirit: Even with a presented body and a renewed mind, a cold spirit will not work. We must be burning in spirit ().

What does it mean today to make no provision for the flesh?The flesh is still hungry.

Reality: However spiritual we become, the flesh can revive. Media and advertising readily supply it with food.

Practice: We keep away from such provisions, and whatever we do we do one with the Lord in our spirit, conquering evil with good ().
