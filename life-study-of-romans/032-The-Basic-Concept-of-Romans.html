Romans · Message 32

The Basic Concept of Romans

Romans is not merely about justification by faith. Its underlying thought is that God is making sons out of sinners to form a Body that expresses Christ.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Basic Thought: God works Himself as life into sinners, making them His sons, and builds them into one Body to express Christ.
  • Two Sections: Chapters 1–8 cover personal salvation (many brothers); chapters 12–16 cover corporate function (the Body and the churches).
  • Our Condition: Sinful in our deeds, constituted sinners in our being, and short of God's glory ().
  • God's Three Answers: His righteousness put upon us, His holiness wrought into us, and His glory to bring us in.
  • God's Heart: Behind it all is His eternal love, our stay and safeguard ().

Sons, a Body, the Churches

In chapter eight the believers are the many brothers of the Firstborn, a matter of life: whoever has the Son has life (). In chapter twelve the same brothers are members of the Body, a matter of function. The Body is then expressed practically in local churches, which chapter sixteen shows through Paul's greetings (), a window into the churches of the first century.

Chapters 1–8

Personal Salvation

Sinners become sons by the divine life; the many brothers of Christ ().

Chapters 12–16

Corporate Function

The sons become members functioning as the Body, expressed in the local churches.

God is in Christ, Christ is in the Body, the Body is in the churches.

What God Must Deal With

Paul first exposes our deeds, then, from , our being. We sin because we are sinners, as an apple tree bears apples because it is an apple tree; we were constituted sinners in Adam before we were born (). And we fall short of God's glory, which is God Himself expressed: made to express Him, we express sin and self instead.

Preaching Tip

In the gospel, expose what people do before telling them what they are. Deeds are what convict first.

Righteousness, Holiness, Glory

God makes sons out of sinners by three things. Through Christ's redeeming death His righteousness is put upon us like the best robe on the returning prodigal (). Through Christ living in us His holiness, His nature, is being wrought into us; though our holiness may seem to vanish like a rainbow, the saturating goes on. One day we shall be glorified and enter the freedom of the glory of God ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is "justification by faith" not an adequate summary of Romans?What is God after?

Too Small: Justification is a step; God's goal is sons who form a Body to express Christ.

Short-Sighted: Without this view, chapters 12–16 seem like an appendix instead of the goal.

What is the difference between being a brother and being a member?Life and function.

Brothers: A matter of life; by having the Son we are sons with Him ().

Members: A matter of function; the sons must function together as the Body in the local churches.

What does it mean to be short of God's glory?Made to express Him.

Glory Defined: God expressed is glory; man was made in His image for that.

Our Lack: Even a "good" person expresses self rather than God ().

How should I view my holiness when it seems so temporary?The infusion goes on.

Not Disappointed: Holiness is God's nature being infused; the process is real even when unfinished.

Assurance: God loves us and will finish what He began; we may cause Him hardship but cannot frustrate Him ().
