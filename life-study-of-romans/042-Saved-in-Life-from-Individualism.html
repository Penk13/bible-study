Romans · Message 42

Saved in Life from Individualism

We ourselves, with our strong mind, emotion, and will, are the greatest hindrance to the building of the Body. Exposed in married life and church life, we open to the Lord so that Christ can live in us and save us from individualism.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Past and Future Tense: Reconciliation is accomplished; being saved in His life is still in process ().
  • Seven Negative Things: Sin, worldliness, naturalness, individualism, divisiveness, self-likeness, and the natural body.
  • The Goal Is Building: Justification leads to life, and life leads to the building; the Lord returns for a corporate Bride.
  • The Real Problem: Not our heart or motive, but our mind, emotion, and will.
  • Exposed to Be Saved: Marriage and the church expose us so that Christ may live in us ().

Seven Things to Be Saved From

Each is subjective, part of our constitution. Individualism shows itself plainly at home: a wife does not want to depend on her husband, nor a husband on his wife. But God is after a Body, not a collection of individual believers.

The Greatest Hindrance

Paul does not mention the Body until chapter 12, because the worldly and natural cannot be built with others. Neither justification nor life is the goal; the goal is the church as Christ's Body and God's house, and without it the Lord has no way to return for His Bride ().

Mind, Emotion, and Will

Many have consecrated themselves to Christ and the church, yet their soul is still the problem. Disharmony in the service usually comes from differing concepts, feelings, or a will that wants control; these are foreign elements in the Body.

Mind

Peculiar thoughts, concepts, and imaginations.

Emotion

Even harder to control; easily stirred and offended.

Will

Stubborn and set, wanting everything under its control.

On the Altar

We cannot fix ourselves. Open to the Lord, lay yourself on the altar (), and let Him deal with your mind, emotion, and will for His building.

Exposed to Be Saved

The "I" crucified with Christ lives in our mind, emotion, and will (). When it prevails, the Lord is dethroned and "saved in His life" stays a doctrine. Marriage shatters our self-made holiness, and the church life, more intense still, exposes us even further.

👉

Blaming

The spouse, the church, even the Lord; dreaming of a "divorce" from an imperfect church.

🙏

Thanking

"Lord, I love the church because it exposes me." Exposure gives Him room to live in us and save us.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Paul wait until chapter 12 to speak of the Body?Materials first.

Prepared Materials: Only those justified, sanctified, and transformed can be built together.

The Goal: Everything before chapter 12 serves the building of the Body ().

I have consecrated myself. Why am I still hard to build with?Heart vs. soul.

Not the Motive: The heart may be right while the mind, emotion, and will remain strong.

Practical Consecration: Ask the Lord to keep you on the altar and open, so He can deal with each part ().

How can knowing "I have been crucified" remain only a doctrine?Who is on the throne?

The "I" Prevails: When the mind is strong, the emotion stirred, or the will set, Christ has no room to live.

Christ Lives: His saving life works only as He is given the opportunity to live in us ().

What should I do when my marriage or church life exposes me?Thank, don't blame.

Ordained Exposure: The Lord's arrangement is never wrong; we would not know ourselves otherwise.

Surrender: Thank Him, open your inner being, and let His life save you from individualism into the Body.
