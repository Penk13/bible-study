Life-Study / Romans 043

Romans · Message 43

Saved in Life from Divisiveness

Deep in our natural being is an element that wants others to be like us and forms its own group. Seeing that foods and days are shadows of Christ, we receive all who believe in Him, and by growing in His life we are saved from divisiveness.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Fifth Item: After sin, worldliness, naturalness, and individualism, Christ's life must save us from divisiveness ().
  • Worse Than Individualism: The individualistic want to be left alone; the divisive actively draw others away to form a party.
  • Receive All Believers: Whatever they eat or whatever days they keep, all who believe in Christ are to be received ().
  • Shadows and the Body: Foods and days are shadows; the reality is Christ ().
  • Growth in Life: The greater our measure of Christ, the less divisive we become.

A Hidden Element

Before we were saved, few of us knew that our natural life has a tendency to divide. Romans traces the saving life step by step: freedom from the law of sin (ch. 8), transformation from naturalness and building against individualism (ch. 12), and in chapters 14 and 15, dealing with our divisive nature.

Individualism

Leave Me Alone

Passive; does not want others to interfere, simply wants to stay as it is.

Divisiveness

Form a Party

Aggressive; contacts the saints, even travels, with the aim of dividing them ().

Eating and Days

Even in Paul's day some ate only vegetables and some ate everything; some kept special days and some esteemed every day alike. These differences concern what we take in and what outward rituals we keep. God cares not for the shadows but for Christ, the body that casts them.

ShadowReality in Christ
Clean foods (Lev. 11)The many aspects of the unsearchably rich Christ; like the tree of life, good for food ()
SabbathChrist as the completion of God's work, our rest and satisfaction
New moonChrist as our new beginning
The bodyChrist Himself; to God every day is the same ()

Care for the Weak

Seeing the reality frees us from these matters, yet many genuine believers are still weak in faith here. The strong must stay open to them and not judge their reasonings (); otherwise we ourselves are divisive.

Our Standing

Our natural tendency is to insist that every believer be the same as we are. Requiring people to drop or adopt certain practices, a particular baptism, or a way of praising, before receiving them is divisive. The basis of receiving is nothing more and nothing less than Christ ().

Mrs. Jones

We do not claim to be the only church. But a wife married to Mr. Jones who calls herself Mrs. Smith cannot be offended when another is called Mrs. Jones. Our standing is simply to be one with all genuine believers in the locality.

The Need for Growth

Teaching about oneness is not enough. In 1957 leaders of independent groups in Taipei agreed believers should be one, yet still chose to remain separate. Only the Lord's mercy and the growth of Christ's life within can keep us one; we must stay watchful, since the old nature remains.

❓

Questioning

A young believer probes others on baptism, the rapture, and other doctrines.

🌱

Appreciating

A growing believer values the measure of Christ in each saint and ignores outward things.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is divisiveness worse than individualism?Passive vs. active.

Individualism: Keeps to itself and does not want to be bothered.

Divisiveness: Works on others, drawing them away to form a group of its own ().

How does seeing Christ as the body end disputes over foods and days?Shadow and substance.

Shadows: Diets and holy days only portrayed Christ; God never cared for them in themselves.

The Substance: When we hold Christ, these become secondary and no reason to separate ().

If I see the truth clearly, why must I still be careful with the weak?Strong bear with weak.

Genuine Believers: Those who still keep days or diets belong to the Lord just as we do.

Receiving: Despising them would make us the divisive ones; we receive them without judging ().

What practical sign shows I am being saved from divisiveness?What do I look for in others?

Less Probing: I stop testing others on doctrines and practices.

More Christ: I appreciate the Christ in them and receive them as Christ received me (), while asking the Lord for real growth.
