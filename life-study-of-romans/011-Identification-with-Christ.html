Life-Study / Romans 011

Romans · Message 11

Identification with Christ

Born in Adam, we have been baptized into Christ, identified with His death and resurrection. We must see this fact in a vision, reckon on it by faith, and cooperate by rejecting sin and presenting ourselves to God, so that the divine life can sanctify us unto eternal life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Man Himself Exposed: Romans 1–5:11 dealt with man's deeds and position. From 5:12 to chapter 8 Paul diagnoses man himself: sinful in nature, under death.
  • The Heavenly Ledger: Adam, transgression, sin, judgment, condemnation total death. Christ, obedience, grace, the gift of righteousness, justification total life (; ).
  • Baptized into Christ: Not a ritual but a transfer out of Adam into Christ and His death (). We have grown together with Him like a seed sown ().
  • See, Then Reckon: Knowing is seeing the fact in a vision (). Then we reckon ourselves dead to sin and alive to God (). Reckoning as a technique fails.
  • Reject and Present: Neither passive nor self-active. Reject sin (), present our members as weapons (), ourselves as slaves to God (). The result: sanctification unto eternal life ().

The Universal Account

Paul, a heavenly accountant, uses "reckon" (put to account) repeatedly (; ). Lay out the ledger:

Debit · AdamCredit · Christ
AdamChrist, far surpassing him ()
Transgression, offense, disobedienceObedience, the righteous act

Total: DeathTotal: Life — zoē, the divine life ()

The gift in Romans 5 is not tongues or miracles but God's righteousness. As long as we have it, judgment is impossible.

Identified Through Baptism

Born in Adam, how can we be in Christ? By being baptized into Him (). Never baptize people ritually; we are putting them into Christ.

Likeness of His Death

Grown Together

The same word describes thorns growing up with wheat. Like a seed sown, we were buried and began to grow (). NKJV: "united together."

Likeness of His Resurrection

Newness of Life

We have grown, and we are growing with Him in His resurrection life. So we walk in newness of life ().

The Order of Experience

Doctrinal understanding of Romans 6 is not enough. Many have practiced "reckoning" as a technique and failed.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does baptism actually accomplish, if not a ritual?Read Romans 6:3 with the word "into" in mind.

A Transfer: Baptism puts members of Adam into death and into Christ (). We were born in one man; we are transferred into the other.

Beyond Arguments: Disputes over water and method miss the point. What matters is the realization that people are being put into Christ and His death.

Why does "reckoning ourselves dead" so often fail in practice?Notice the order: "knowing this" (6:6) comes before "reckon" (6:11).

Technique Without Vision: Reckoning based on doctrinal understanding is self-effort. It always ends in failure.

Seeing First: When we see in a vision that we were crucified and raised with Christ (), faith rises spontaneously, and reckoning () is simply recording what we have seen.

Why does Witness Lee prefer "weapons" to "instruments" in Romans 6:13?Compare 2 Corinthians 6:7 and Romans 13:12.

A Battle Within: The same Greek word is "armor" in , and Paul speaks of "the armor of light" (). A war rages between righteousness and unrighteousness ().

On the Alert: Every member of our body is a weapon. Presenting them to God means taking sides with the indwelling Christ in the next battle.

What is the balance between being passive and being active in sanctification?What should we do, and what should we not do?

Not Our Own Efforts: Do not try to love your spouse or be humble by yourself; that is self-activity. Yet do not become passive either, or you will be deceived.

Reject and Cooperate: Say to sin, "Depart from me" (), and to the Lord, "I am Your slave; I will cooperate with You" (). Under grace, sin has no right to rule ().
