Romans · Message 13

The Bondage of the Law in Our Flesh (2)

Romans 7:7–25 reveals three laws: the law of God demanding from outside, the law of good in our mind responding, and the law of sin in our members warring and taking us captive. This is Paul's experience before salvation, recorded to prove we should not try to keep the law. The war in Galatians 5 is a different war: flesh against Spirit.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Law Helps Sin: Sin takes occasion through the commandment (), revives, deceives, and kills (). The law itself is holy, just, and good ().
  • Three Laws: God's law above us; the law of good in our mind (); the law of sin in our members (), always waiting to capture us.
  • Something Good Remains: "In my flesh nothing good dwells" (), but there is a will to do good. Man has two natures: the God-created good, and Satan's evil injected into the body.
  • Before Salvation: "Wretched man" () vs. "now no condemnation" (): Romans 7 is Paul's past, written to keep us from the law. Many Christians repeat it after salvation by resolving to do good.
  • A Different War: Romans 7 is good law vs. evil law. Galatians 5 is flesh vs. Spirit (). The flesh is never improved; we walk by the Spirit ().

Three Laws

"Husbands, love your wives." The good law in the mind says, "I will!" The evil law in the flesh answers, "Don't you know I am here?" And the result is defeat. This is not doctrine but our life history.

Outside · Above

The Law of God

Holy, just, good, and spiritual (, ). It makes demands that expose fallen man.

In the Mind

The Law of Good

Delights in God's law and quickly responds, "Amen, I will do it" (). It represents our own strength.

In the Members

The Law of Sin

Dormant until the good law responds, then wars and makes us captive (). It is Satan's principle, stronger than we are.

The Body of Sin

Active, capable, never tired in sinning. Since the old man was crucified, it is "jobless."

The Body of This Death

Weak as a corpse in keeping the law. Stop dragging it; live as the new man with the new Husband.

When Did Romans 7 Happen?

Romans 7:24 says, "O wretched man that I am!" Romans 8:1 says, "There is therefore now no condemnation." Witness Lee concludes the experience of Romans 7 was Paul's before he was saved, when he zealously tried to keep the law.

The Flesh Is Never Improved

Some teach that once we are spiritual, lust disappears from the flesh. That is dangerous. "That which is born of the flesh is flesh" (). Even genuine believers can fulfill its lust, so we must walk by the Spirit.

Is Man Good or Evil?

Both schools are partly right. Man was created good, and that goodness remains, like metal thrown in the dirt that is still metal (). But at the fall Satan's nature entered the body.

🧑

Men

The Israelites bitten in the wilderness had human life and appearance: God-created life with a law of good.

🐍

And Serpents

The serpent's poison permeated them, so a bronze serpent was lifted up for them. Satan's life in the flesh brings the law of sin.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How do the three laws in Romans 7 interact?Trace what happens when God's law says, "Honor your parents."

Demand and Response: God's law makes a demand; the law of good in the mind delights in it and promises to obey ().

Ambush: The law of sin in the members is roused and wars against the law of the mind, making us captive (). Our strength cannot defeat Satan's power.

If Romans 7 describes Paul before salvation, why do so many Christians live it after salvation?Consider what a new believer does the night they are saved.

Resolutions: Many were careless before salvation. After believing they resolve to be good, and so for the first time they meet the war between the good law and the evil law.

Not Necessary: Romans 8 continues Romans 6. Romans 7 exposes us so that we stop trying and say, "Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ" ().

What is the difference between the war in Romans 7 and the war in Galatians 5?Who are the combatants in each?

Romans 7: Our good law against the evil law. It has nothing to do with the Spirit and is common to all who try to be good.

Galatians 5: The flesh against the Spirit (). Here the believer walks by the mingled spirit and continually puts the flesh to death (; ).

Why should we never accept the teaching that the flesh improves when we become spiritual?Read Galatians 5:16 and John 3:6.

Flesh Is Flesh: Nothing changes its nature (). Paul warns genuine believers that they can still fulfill its lust ().

Stay Alert: Believing the flesh is safe leads to carelessness and moral failure. Our safeguard is not a better flesh but walking by the Spirit.
