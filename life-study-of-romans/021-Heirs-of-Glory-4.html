Life-Study / Romans 021

Romans · Message 21

Heirs of Glory (4)

Within, the Spirit intercedes; without, the Father causes all things to work together to conform us to the Son. In God's eyes our glorification is already accomplished, and our security is the love of God in Christ Jesus.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • All Things Work Together: The Father answers the Spirit's groaning by arranging all matters and all persons for our conformation (). While we grow, He molds.
  • Already Glorified: Every verb in verse 30 is past tense. With the eternal God there is no clock; our glorification is accomplished and insured ().
  • One Intercessor: Christ intercedes at God's right hand () and the Spirit within us; they are one, as one electricity is in the plant and in the home ().
  • Revelation of the Sons: We are sons under a veil. Glorification is the removing of that veil, which sets creation free ().
  • Inseparable from His Love: Nothing can separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus; this love is our eternal security ().

Outwardly, All Things

The Greek for "all things" covers every matter and every person. God knows the number of our hairs, the children we need, the spouse who suits our growth. It is as if He sacrifices everyone around us for us. If God is for us, who can be against us ()? He is for us in His way, not in ours.

Do Not

Pray for Sufferings

Do not be so "spiritual" that you ask for hardship. Ask the Father to keep you from trouble and temptation.

But Do

Accept What Comes

When affliction comes anyway, do not complain: "If possible, take this away; nevertheless, not my will." Everything is in His hands.

Glorification

On the mount the Lord Jesus was unveiled and His glory shone (). We too are sons of God under a veil. Creation, held in vanity and the slavery of corruption, watches eagerly for our revelation. Our hope is wholly unseen, and it is Christ Himself, the hope of glory (). We boast in it now (), and when He appears we will appear with Him in glory ().

Our Insurance

John saw the New Jerusalem nineteen centuries ago, and Revelation speaks of future things as done. Predestinated, called, justified, glorified: in God it is all secured.

Into the Heart of God

Paul built the earlier sections of Romans on God's righteousness, holiness, and glory, but he ends in God's love. Love came before all the rest; it is the source of salvation and its security. He commended this love in chapter five () but waited until now to unveil it fully.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How do the Spirit's intercession and the Father's arrangement work together?Verses 26–28.

Within: The Spirit groans in us according to God (), with conformation to the Son as His purpose.

Without: The Father knows that purpose and answers by causing all things, matters, and persons to work together for good ().

Why does Paul say "glorified" in the past tense?There is no clock in heaven.

Our View: We are bound by time; to us, glorification lies ahead and many are not yet called or justified.

God's View: The eternal God sees it as accomplished (). That is why verse 31 follows: what more can we say, if God is for us?

What will our glorification mean for creation?The veil removed.

Revelation: The sons of God now veiled will be revealed, as Christ was on the mount ().

Freedom: A kingdom of glory will replace vanity and corruption, and creation will enter the freedom of the glory of the children of God ().

What is your real eternal security?Not in us, but in God's heart.

The Source: God loved us before He predestinated, called, justified, or glorified us. His love did not start with us and does not depend on us.

The Security: Because it is His love in Christ Jesus, nothing in death, life, or any creature can separate us (; ). Grace, love, and fellowship are with us all ().
