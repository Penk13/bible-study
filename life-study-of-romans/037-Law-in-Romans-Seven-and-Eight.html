Romans · Message 37

Law in Romans Seven and Eight

Three persons with three lives now live in us, and every life has its law. Willing to do good only presses the button of evil; living by the strongest law, the law of the Spirit of life, does everything spontaneously.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Laws: The law of God, the law of the mind, the law of sin and of death, and the law of the Spirit of life.
  • "The Law That": Whenever we will to do good, evil is present (); trying to do good pushes the wrong button.
  • Three Persons: Man in our soul, Satan in our flesh, and God in our spirit, each with its own life.
  • Every Life Has a Law: Birds fly and dogs bark without teaching; a life acts according to its law.
  • The Right Button: Set the mind on the spirit; the divine life, the strongest law, works spontaneously ().

The Law That

Besides the four laws there is a principle with no commandments: if we do not try to be humble, pride seems absent; resolve to be humble and pride shows up at once. Brother Lee was troubled by this for eight years, until he saw that willing to do good is like pushing a button that summons evil. We should pray, not for help to do good, but to be kept from pushing that button.

Stop Pushing the Button

"The good which I will, I do not" (). The more we try, the worse we become; even reckoning ourselves dead by effort makes us more alive.

Three Persons, Three Lives, Three Laws

Eden is now within us. Our human life from creation desires good; the evil life injected in the fall makes children lie untaught; regeneration brought in God Himself (). The law of good delights in God's law (), but the law in our members is stronger and takes it captive (). The divine law is strongest of all.

Living by the Right Law

Everything we do flows from one of these laws. Suppressing anger is only politics, and it cannot last; eventually the satanic law wins. No one commands an apple tree to bear oranges or teaches a carnation to blossom. Do not teach a brother to love his wife or a sister to submit; teach them to touch the right button, the Spirit witnessing with our spirit ().

Wrong button

Willing to Do Good

Evil is present; the stronger law of sin defeats the law of good.

Right button

Walking by the Spirit

The law of life fulfills God's requirements spontaneously ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does resolving to be patient make me angrier?"The law that."

A Principle: Whenever we will to do good, evil is present (). The resolution itself pushes the button.

The Prayer: Ask the Lord to keep us from trying, not to help us try harder.

Is a Christian simply someone with an old and a new nature?Count the persons.

Three Persons: The self in the soul, Satan in the flesh, and God in the spirit, each with a life.

Complicated: These three never harmonize; which one we live by decides what comes out.

What does a bird's flying teach about the Christian life?Life and law.

Natural Function: A life acts by its law without being taught; commanding a cat to fly is useless.

Applied: The divine life in us has its own law; we need not teach it, only let it live ().

How do I keep my finger on the right button?Where is the button?

The Button: It is the Spirit in our spirit (), the Lord Himself.

The Practice: Turn the mind and whole being to the spirit and stay there; negative things are put to death and the law of God is fulfilled.
