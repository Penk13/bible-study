Romans · Message 36

Being Freed from Death (2)

Death, based in the flesh, works inward to deaden mind and spirit. Life, in our spirit, works outward; we escape death not by fighting it but by taking refuge in the spirit, where life swallows it.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Wages: Death enters, reigns, and is the pay for the work of sin; more sinning, more pay ().
  • Self-Made Laws Kill: Sin uses every commandment, even our own resolutions, to deceive and kill us ().
  • Signs of Death: Darkness, uneasiness, dissatisfaction, weakness, and dryness follow a mind set on the flesh ().
  • Opposite Directions: Death works from the body inward; life works from the spirit outward.
  • The Last Enemy: God hates death even more than sin; staying in the spirit is the way to overcome it ().

How Death Kills Us

Before we sought the Lord, losing our temper did not seem to kill us. Once we vowed never to do it again, we made an "eleventh commandment," and sin took occasion through it (). Like a rubber ball pressed down, the suppressed temper springs higher; that is the law of sin and of death (). Afterward we cannot pray or testify; our words are hollow.

🌑

Darkness & Uneasiness

No light, no rest within.

🍂

Dissatisfaction & Dryness

The morning's satisfaction drains away; nothing waters us.

🪫

Weakness

Death is weakness carried to its end.

Two Directions

The unsaved are dead in body, soul, and spirit, like dry leaves drifting with the current, while those who have life swim upstream with purpose. When we called on the Lord, our spirit became life (). Yet the mind can remain dead: quick to recall every garment we own, slow to recall a chapter of the Bible. Christ must spread from the spirit into the mind and finally the body ().

Death

Circumference → Center

Based in the flesh, it deadens the mind and then the spirit.

Life

Center → Circumference

From Christ in our spirit, it enlivens mind, emotion, will, and body.

Refuge in the Spirit

Nothing drives darkness out; only light makes it vanish. Death in the flesh is not chased away by effort; life in the spirit swallows it. Sin offends God's righteousness, but death insults His very being; under the law, touching a dead body defiled longer than other uncleanness. So it is better to be livingly wrong than dead right.

Not Yes or No, but Life

In John the Lord never answered by right or wrong; true worship is in spirit (). Leave the tree of knowledge and stay in the spirit.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did my temper hurt me more after I vowed to stop losing it?The eleventh commandment.

A New Law: The vow became a commandment, and sin seized it as an occasion to kill ().

Fewer Laws: The more rules we make for ourselves, the more we are killed.

How can I tell that I am in death right now?Five symptoms.

The Signs: Darkness, uneasiness, dissatisfaction, weakness, and dryness, often after setting the mind on the flesh ().

Others Sense It: Those in the spirit detect death in our prayers, which water no one.

Why does God hate death even more than sin?The dirty boy and the coffin.

Against What He Is: Sin opposes what God does; death insults the living God Himself.

Our Concern: Many fear uncleanness but tolerate deadness. Being living matters more than being right.

What does it mean to take refuge in the spirit?Let the light in.

Not Fighting: Effort cannot drive death out, just as it cannot drive darkness out.

Fleeing Inward: Turn from the flesh, death's base, to the spirit where Christ is life, and let Him spread until death, the last enemy, is swallowed ().
