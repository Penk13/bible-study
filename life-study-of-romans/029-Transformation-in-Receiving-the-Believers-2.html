Romans · Message 29

Transformation in Receiving the Believers (2)

We receive the saints in the principle of love, for the kingdom life, and according to Christ. The all-inclusive Christ, root of Jesse and ruler of the nations, embraces all believers in one Body.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • In Love: Love, fresh in Paul from 1 Corinthians 13, governs receiving: no stumbling blocks, no grieving the one for whom Christ died.
  • The Kingdom Now: The church is the kingdom of God today; home for grace and life, kingdom for exercise and discipline.
  • Kingdom Life: Not eating and drinking, but righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit ().
  • According to Christ: Only one Christ, so being according to Him makes us likeminded; anything else divides ().
  • All-Embracing: Receive one another as Christ received us, whatever the nationality or background ().

The Church as Home and Kingdom

Some teach that the kingdom was suspended until the Lord returns, yet Paul says the kingdom of God is. In the Lord uses church and kingdom interchangeably: building the church establishes the kingdom. Like a head seen from the back and the front, the one church has two aspects. Our destiny is to reign, but birth is not enough; a king must be trained. God arranges spouse, children, saints, and circumstances to expose us, so we blame ourselves and not others.

Family

God's Home

Love, the supply of grace, the riches of life. "Praise the Lord, I am home!"

Government

God's Kingdom

Rule under Christ's headship: exercise in this age, or discipline in the next.

What the Kingdom Life Is

The saints are not received by our concepts of food and drink. Righteousness, peace, and joy are Christ expressed in us. Serving Christ this way pleases God and is approved by men. We pursue the things of peace, which keep the Body's unity, and the things that build, which minister life (). Satan uses doctrine and mental knowledge to divide; we must not break down God's work of grace in a brother over food.

Toward Ourselves

Righteousness

Strict and narrow with ourselves, making no excuses.

Toward Others

Peace

Pursued with everyone related to us, even at home.

With God

Joy

Daily praise in the Holy Spirit; no joy means defeat.

Receiving According to Christ

Paul opens this section with Jewish doctrinal concepts and closes it with Christ. The strong bear the weaknesses of the weak and please their neighbor for building, as Christ bore the reproaches meant for the Father (). The God of endurance and encouragement supplies the patience to receive. Christ's receiving is God's receiving.

For the Jews

Servant of the Circumcision

Confirming the promises to the fathers: God is truthful. As root of Jesse He is their source and supply.

For the Nations

Ruler Over the Gentiles

That they glorify God for His mercy; in Him the nations hope ().

Five Aspects of Receiving

According to God's receiving, in the light of the judgment seat, in the principle of love, for the kingdom life, and according to Christ. The result: the God of hope fills us with joy and peace in believing ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the church both a home and a kingdom?Two sides of one head.

Home: We enjoy love, grace, and the life supply as God's family.

Kingdom: We are ruled under Christ's headship and exercised, so that we may be trained to reign with Him rather than be disciplined in the coming age.

How is the kingdom life different from religious practices?Turtle or cabbage?

Not Outward: What we eat or which days we keep means nothing to the kingdom.

Christ Expressed: Righteousness toward ourselves, peace toward others, and joy with God in the Spirit are Christ lived out ().

Why does being according to Christ make us likeminded?One Christ, many teachings.

The Principle: Teachings, gifts, and practices are many, so following them divides. Christ is one, so following Him unites ().

The Cost: Receiving by concepts needs no endurance; receiving all by Christ needs the endurance and hope only God supplies.

What does it mean today to see my circumstances as kingdom exercise?A multi-dimensional photograph.

Exposure: Family, saints, and situations show us what we really are, which we could never see alone.

Response: Instead of blaming others, take the blame, thank the Lord for those who expose us, and accept His rule.
