Romans · Message 25

Transformation in Practicing the Body Life (1)

Sanctification is for the life process; transformation is for the life practice, and that practice is the Body life. We enter it by presenting our bodies as one living sacrifice and by being transformed through the renewing of the mind.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Paul's Final Word: Romans does not stop at chapter 8. Five chapters (12–16) are on the church; the life process is for the Body life.
  • Transformation: A metabolic change in nature and form as Christ's life is added to us, not outward correction ().
  • One Living Sacrifice: Through God's compassions we present our bodies; many bodies, yet one sacrifice ().
  • Not Conformed to the Age: The age is the current, practical form of the world system, which opposes the church life.
  • God's Will: The good, well-pleasing, and perfect will of is the Body life.

From Life Process to Life Practice

Chapters 1–8 bring us from sinners to conformed sons of God; chapters 9–11 show God's selection and economy. Now Paul turns to how this processed life is practiced. Many seeking Christians pursue Romans 8 yet treat chapters 12–16 as mere instructions on good behavior. But the main part of a believer's behavior is the church life; even our daily life is for it. Sanctification and spirituality are not for themselves but for the Body.

Outward

Coloring the Face

Paint gives a pale face color, but nothing has changed within.

Metabolic

Healthy Food Within

Organic elements absorbed day by day change the face from inside. So Christ's life changes our constitution.

The Body Life

The Body life is the practicality of the church life; without it "church" is only a term. It is corporate: a member cut off from the body loses its life and function. We, being many, are one Body and members of one another (). Many believers lack life and function simply because they are detached.

Present the Body, Renew the Mind

Paul begs us "through the compassions of God," many compassions shown in selecting, calling, and saving us. In chapter 6 we presented our members as weapons and servants (); here it is a sacrifice for God's satisfaction. It is living, not slain; holy, separate and of God's nature; and it is the most reasonable, priestly service. Since we are where our bodies are, being with the church "in spirit only" is not enough.

To be conformed to the age is to take on its fashions outwardly; to be transformed is to have Christ wrought in inwardly. With a renewed mind we can prove that God's will is the Body life. Housing, work, and marriage should all be decided in light of the church life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is transformation, intrinsically?Not paint, but food.

Definition: A change in both nature and form, produced as the organic life of Christ is added into our being by the Lord Spirit ().

Not Correction: Outward adjustment is like coloring a pale face. Real change comes from within, by metabolism.

How can spirituality itself become a veil?A Romans of eight chapters.

The Danger: One can pursue the experiences of Romans 8 for oneself and never come to the corporate life of Romans 12. Even the best things can veil us.

The Goal: Redemption, justification, sanctification, and conformation are all for the Body life ().

Why does Paul ask for our bodies, not just our hearts?Where our bodies are, we are.

Practical: Nothing about us is more real than the body. Being with the church only "in spirit" while staying away is a self-deception.

Reasonable: Moved by God's many compassions, offering ourselves as one living, holy sacrifice is our most reasonable service ().

What does it mean today to be transformed by the renewing of the mind?Not modernized.

Refusing the Age: Each age has its fashion, and it opposes the church life. We cannot follow it and still practice the Body life.

Renewal: As Christ spreads from our spirit into our mind, the soul is changed (), and we can prove that God's will is the church life.
