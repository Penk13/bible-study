Romans · Message 40

Saved in Life from Sin and Worldliness

Christ's death reconciled us to God; His life now saves us. The law of the Spirit of life frees us from the law of sin, and the same life sanctifies us inwardly from worldliness.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Sprout and Growth: Life sprouts in John and grows in Romans, where it can be not only known but experienced.
  • Justification of Life: Justification is not the end; it brings us back to the tree of life ().
  • Much More: Saved from hell by His death, we are still being saved in His life from temper, pride, jealousy, and more.
  • Two Laws: The law of sin works automatically; the law of the Spirit of life, the highest law, frees us as we set our mind on the spirit.
  • Sanctified by Life: Sanctification in Romans is dispositional, not merely positional, and it issues in more life.

Life Revealed Progressively

The life in view is not bios or psuche but zoe, the divine life. Its seed is sown in Genesis, it sprouts in John, it grows in Romans, and it is harvested in Revelation. Romans 8 adds what John does not mention: the law of the Spirit of life, the way to apply this life.

Justified for Life

The ultimate point of Romans 1 is not salvation, justification, or even faith, but life: the just shall have life and live by faith (). The fall closed the way to the tree of life (); Christ's death met God's righteousness and opened it again.

✝️

Reconciled by His Death

Redemption, justification, reconciliation: saved once for all from hell and judgment.

🌱

Saved in His Life

Much more, saved daily from sin, worldliness, naturalness, individualism, and divisiveness ().

The Law of the Spirit of Life

A ball falls without prayer; likewise we lose our temper, become proud, or lie without effort, because the law of sin "laws" us. Philosophers called it the war between principle and lust (), but none could win it. Every life has its law, and the highest life has the highest law.

Our Part

Do not suppress your temper. Turn your being to the mingled spirit, call on the Lord Jesus, and let the higher law overrule the lower one.

Sanctified in Life

Most books treat sanctification as a positional change by the blood, but Romans 6:22 links it to life. By nature we are common and worldly; we need our very disposition separated, not only our conduct.

Outward

By Rules

Choosing shoes or a hairstyle that merely avoids a worldly style, according to teachings and regulations.

Inward

By Life

Asking the Lord within and following the anointing, so that even our being is sanctified; this yields fruit unto sanctification and more life ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

If I am already saved, from what do I still need to be saved?Past tense, future tense.

Already: Christ's death has saved us once for all from God's judgment and from hell.

Still: His life must save us from temper, disposition, pride, jealousy, worldliness, and much more ().

Why does justification by faith point to life?Back to the tree.

Justification of Life: God justifies us so that we may enjoy His life ().

The Way Reopened: What sin closed in Genesis 3, Christ's righteousness opens again.

Why can't resolutions defeat my temper?Gravity.

A Law, Not an Act: Temper is the automatic working of the law of sin; willpower cannot repeal a law.

A Higher Law: Only the law of the Spirit of life frees us, and we cooperate by setting our mind on the spirit ().

How is buying shoes a matter of sanctification?Disposition.

Not the Shoes: The point is not the right style but contacting the Lord in the process.

The Being: Living by the inner life makes our very being uncommon, and such sanctification brings in more life.
