Life-Study / Romans 010

Romans · Message 10

The Gift in Christ Surpassing the Heritage in Adam

At 5:12 Romans turns from our position to our disposition. In God's eyes there are only two men, Adam and Christ, with two acts and two results. From Adam we inherit sin and death; in Christ we receive grace and life. Grace reigns through righteousness, and those who receive its abundance reign in life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Position to Disposition: Condemnation (1:18–3:20) and justification (3:21–5:11) deal with our position. From 5:12, "sins" become "sin": the nature within. "Abide in Me, and I in you" ().
  • Two Men: Adam, the first man, created without God's life; Christ, the second Man and last Adam, God mingled with man (, ). There is no third.
  • Two Acts: Adam left the tree of life for the tree of knowledge; Christ obeyed unto the cross (), ending the man of knowledge and restoring life.
  • Constituted: The many were constituted sinners, and through Christ are constituted righteous (), not painted on but injected into our being.
  • Four Reigning Things: Sin and death reign; grace reigns through righteousness unto eternal life (); believers who receive grace's abundance reign in life ().

Two Men, Two Acts, Two Results

We are nobodies; everything depends on which man we are in. "For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all shall be made alive" ().

Painted

Paint my skin green and my constitution is unchanged. That is an outward, positional change only.

🩸

Constituted

Inject green into my blood and my whole being is constituted with it. Sin was injected into us; now God as grace constitutes us righteous.

NKJV renders Romans 5:19 "made sinners" and "made righteous"; Witness Lee presses the sense of being constituted.

Four Reigning Things

Two kingdoms, four kings. Once sin was our king through death; now we are in the kingdom of life.

Sin

Satan Personified

Entered through Adam, dwells in the fallen body, making it flesh (). Its power is the law (); it reigns in death.

Death

The King Sin Inaugurated

Came through sin, its sting. Reigns over all through one man. We are all "living to die."

Grace

Christ Personified

God incarnated as our enjoyment (; ). Where sin abounded, grace abounded more (), reigning through righteousness.

The Believers

Reigning in Life

Receiving the abundance of grace (), grace upon grace (), we reign over every situation and walk in newness of life ().

Why Grace Needs Righteousness

We were constituted sinners. For God to come to us as grace, He needs a lawful means. Christ's redemption gives us God's righteousness, which gives us the position to claim God as our portion. Grace then reigns through righteousness unto eternal life ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Romans switch from "sins" to "sin" at 5:12, and why does it matter?Distinguish outward deeds from an inward nature.

Two Problems: Sins are outward deeds affecting our position; Christ's blood dealt with them. Sin is the evil nature within affecting our disposition (; ).

Two Salvations: Christ's death saved us objectively; His life within saves us subjectively (). Romans 5:12–8:30 is about the second.

What does it mean that in God's eyes there are only two men?Read 1 Corinthians 15:22, 45.

Included in One or the Other: Everyone is in Adam or in Christ. In Adam we inherited sin and death; in Christ we receive grace and life ().

No Third Man: Christ is the last Adam (). There is no self-improved third option; the only way out of Adam is into Christ.

Why should we stay away from the law in dealing with sin?Consider 1 Corinthians 15:56.

The Knife of Sin: The strength of sin is the law (). Trying to keep it hands sin the knife it uses to kill us.

A Stronger King: Grace is stronger than sin; where sin abounded, grace abounded more (). The answer to sin is not more effort but more grace.

How do we know whether we have "the abundance of grace," and how can we receive more?Consider how you respond to a frank, "salty" word (Colossians 4:6).

A Practical Test: If a situation is unbearable, or a frank word offends us, we have only a little grace. With abundant grace we can bear hard words and difficult people, as Paul bore his thorn ().

Grace upon Grace: Open to Christ and enlarge your capacity (). The more grace we receive, the more life issues, and we reign in life ().
