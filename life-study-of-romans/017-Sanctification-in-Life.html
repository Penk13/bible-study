Life-Study / Romans 017

Romans · Message 17

Sanctification in Life

Justification gives us God's righteousness outwardly; sanctification works His holy nature into us inwardly. In Romans sanctification is not positional by the blood but dispositional by life, so that God's born children may become His sons in reality.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Three Attributes, Three Sections: Justification matches God's righteousness (His acts), sanctification His holiness (His nature), and glorification His glory (God expressed).
  • A Processed God: Through incarnation, crucifixion, and resurrection God became the available Spirit of life (), as easy to receive as breath ().
  • Saturation: From our spirit He spreads into mind, emotion, and will, and even into our mortal body (). This saturation is sanctification.
  • Not by the Blood Here: Elsewhere the blood sanctifies (), but Romans speaks of subjective sanctification in life.
  • The Goal Is Sons: We were born children of God, but we must be sanctified to look like His sons ().

Three Stages, Three Parts of Our Being

Romans 1–8 expounds what 1 Corinthians 1:30 says in one sentence: Christ made to us righteousness, sanctification, and redemption ().

StageGod's AttributePart Dealt WithNature
JustificationRighteousnessSpirit made life ()Objective, like a roof covering us
SanctificationHolinessSoul, and somewhat the body ()Subjective, wrought into us
GlorificationGloryBody transfigured ()Brought fully into God as glory

Justification is for sanctification, and sanctification is for glorification.

Life Throughout Romans Is for Sanctification

The word life runs through Romans 1–8. It is not mainly for justification, which Christ's death already accomplished, but for the holy nature of God to be infused into us.

Romans 8:10

Christ Is in You

The initial stage: the spirit is life, though the body is still dead because of sin.

Romans 8:11

The Spirit Dwells in You

The further stage: making His home in us, He gives life even to our mortal bodies.

Position and Disposition

Hagios, hagiasmos, and their kin mean "separated to God." Sanctification has two aspects; Romans stresses the second.

Positional

A Change of Position

Gold sanctified by the temple, the gift by the altar; separated from the common to God.

Dispositional

A Change of Nature

Transformed from a natural to a spiritual disposition (), from regeneration to maturity at rapture ().

Crucified and Resurrected

The crucified Christ, the Redeemer in the flesh, was for our justification. The resurrected Christ, the life-giving Spirit in our spirit, is for our sanctification.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How do righteousness, holiness, and glory differ, and why does Romans follow that order?God's acts, God's nature, God expressed.

Definitions: Righteousness concerns God's ways and acts; holiness is His nature, as wood is the nature of a table; glory is God Himself expressed.

The Order: Justification enlivens our spirit, sanctification saturates our soul, and glorification transfigures our body. The three sections of Romans match the three stages of a complete salvation.

Why could God not sanctify us before He was processed?Creating outside us versus entering into us.

Outside, Not Inside: Before incarnation, crucifixion, and resurrection God could create the world but could not enter into His creature.

Now Available: As the Spirit of life () He comes in like breath () and spreads from our spirit to every part of our being.

What is the difference between Christ being in you and the Spirit dwelling in you?Compare Romans 8:10 and 8:11, and note "though," "yet," and "but."

Initial Stage: When we were justified, Christ came in; our spirit became life, though our body remained dead ().

Further Stage: If we let Him make His home in our mind, emotion, and will, He imparts life even to our mortal body (). Outwardly we cooperate by putting to death the practices of the body ().

Born a child of God, do you look like a son of God?Reality and practicality versus vain knowledge.

Not in Word Only: Many genuine Christians, though regenerated, still live in a worldly way. Birth makes us children; sanctification in life makes us mature sons ().

Our Need: Not another exposition, but His eternal life working His holy being into our nature, changing even our inward substance.
