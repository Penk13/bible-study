Romans · Message 19

Heirs of Glory (2)

To be children has no condition; to be heirs we must suffer with Him. While creation and we ourselves groan, we enjoy the Spirit as the firstfruit, and the Spirit joins in our manner to help and intercede, until full sonship: the redemption of our body.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Condition for Heirs: "If indeed we suffer with Him" (). Suffering is the incarnation of grace, and it intensifies glory, as star differs from star ().
  • Creation Waits on Us: Subjected to vanity () and the slavery of corruption, creation awaits the revelation of the sons of God ().
  • Groaning with a Foretaste: Worldly people only groan. We groan outwardly but rejoice inwardly, having the firstfruit of the Spirit ().
  • In Like Manner: The Spirit joins in to help us in our own manner, loud or soft, quick or slow (), as the Lord walked with the two to Emmaus.
  • Unutterable Groanings: The best prayer for growth in life, in which the Spirit intercedes according to God ().

Suffering and Glory

All the children will be glorified, but the degree of suffering decides the intensity of glory. The present sufferings are not worthy to be compared with the coming glory (). On that day no one will need to say "we are sons of God"; the glory will designate us.

Romans 8:20–22

Creation Groaning

Vanity, corruption, slavery: the whole creation groans and travails together until now ().

Romans 8:21

Freedom of Glory

When the children of God are brought into glory, a realm of freedom, creation is set free. If we mature slowly, creation must wait.

We are saved in hope, a hope never seen and therefore wonderful, and we wait for it with endurance (). We need not groan as much as we need to grow.

Children, Sons, Heirs

The blessings of sonship: the Spirit of sonship, the witnessing, the leading, the firstfruit, the helping, and the interceding of the Spirit. They lead to full sonship in three stages of the divine life.

Legally Declared Heirs

In ancient law an heir had to be of age and declared before claiming the inheritance. The redemption of our body is that declaration.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is there a condition for being heirs but none for being children?Notice the semicolon in verse 17.

Children: As long as the Spirit witnesses with our spirit, we are children. No condition.

Heirs: Joint-heirs "if indeed we suffer with Him" (). Suffering does not earn glory but intensifies it.

What is the difference between our groaning and the world's groaning?The firstfruit.

The World: Groaning only, seeking relief in entertainment.

Us: Groaning in a body still of the old creation, yet enjoying the Spirit as a foretaste of all that God is to us (), the presence of the Lord.

What does "in like manner" teach about how the Spirit helps, and how we should help others?The road to Emmaus.

The Spirit: He does not ask us to rise to His standard; He joins us in our manner ().

Us: Older saints can be too big and too high. Like an adult who becomes small to play with a child, we must help others in their manner.

When did you last pray with a burden you could not put into words?Clear about business, unclear about growth.

Our Lack: We have words for material needs but little understanding or utterance concerning growth in life.

His Intercession: When we groan from deep within, the indwelling Spirit groans in our groaning, interceding according to God () for our conformity to the image of God ().
