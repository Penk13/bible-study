Romans · Message 46

Reigning in Life by Grace

Beyond being saved in His life, we may reign in life today. Grace, God Himself as our portion, reigns when it fills and overflows us, and in that eternal life we reign as kings over sin, death, and Satan.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Unique Verse: Only Romans 5:17 speaks of reigning in life, and it concerns our daily life now, not only the millennium ().
  • Grace Reigns First: Grace reigns unto eternal life, and in that life we reign ().
  • Three Enemies: Sin, death, and Satan, not our spouse or children.
  • Grace Is God: God in Christ dispensed into our spirit as our portion, a living Person.
  • Receive to Reign: Open to be filled; grace abounds, overflows, and then reigns.

From John to Romans

Romans continues the Gospel of John. Both speak of life, grace, sin, death, slavery, and freedom, but only Romans speaks of grace and life reigning. Paul could write this because he himself was reigning.

Kings over Three Enemies

To reign is to subdue and rule, to have a kingdom. Toward our family and the saints we are servants; a husband is head, not king. Our temper and self are ugly because these enemies dwell in us; when they are subdued, even these become comely.

Sin

The law of sin in our flesh ().

Death

Reigned through the one offense ().

Satan

Soon crushed under our feet ().

Two Kings Within

God intends to give us nothing but Himself; all else is vanity. Grace is God in Christ for our enjoyment, and it is living, so it grows, abounds, and reigns. In Romans 5 both sin and grace are personified, and the two war within us.

⛓️

King Sin

Satan's evil nature embodied in our flesh, reigning in death.

👑

King Grace

God in Christ embodied in our spirit, reigning unto eternal life ().

Receiving the Abundance

Doctrine and exhortation do not help here; we neither labor nor pay. We come to the divine source and open from the depths, asking Him to remove every insulation, at work, at school, or driving.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How is reigning in life more than overcoming?Freed vs. ruling.

Overcoming: Being set free from sin and death.

Reigning: Ruling over them as kings, with a kingdom, today ().

Who are my real enemies, and who are not?Kings and servants.

Not Enemies: Spouse, children, and saints, toward whom we are servants.

The Enemies: Sin, death, and Satan (); when they are subdued, even our temper changes.

Why is grace a Person rather than a favor or a thing?God as our portion.

God Himself: Grace is God in Christ dispensed into us for enjoyment ().

Living: Because it is living, it grows, abounds, and reigns.

What practically lets grace reign in me?Open and be filled.

Not Effort: Teaching and striving do not produce it.

Openness: Stay open to the Lord everywhere, asking Him to remove every hindrance until grace overflows and reigns ().
