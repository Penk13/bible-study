Life-Study / Romans 004

Romans · Message 4

The Vanity of Religion and the Totality of Hopelessness

Even the best religion, built on God's own revelation, is vanity without the living Person in our spirit. And the whole world, religious or not, stands hopeless under God's righteous judgment. This is how Paul clears the way to minister Christ into us.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Lessons from Condemnation: The source of wickedness, the way of restriction, the vanity of religion, and the totality of hopelessness.
  • Religion Is Vanity: Judaism and Christianity both came from the Bible, yet God gave the Word to reveal Himself into us, not to form a religion. The stronger the religion, the more vanity.
  • No "-anity": Christ, Christians (), and the church are precious. Add "-anity" and it becomes vanity.
  • Six Marks of Vain Religion: An outward name, outward knowledge, the Scriptures held outwardly, outward forms, no inward reality in spirit, and the same evils as the unreligious (–29).
  • Totally Hopeless: None righteous, none good, none seeking God (). All the world is under God's judgment (), so all need Christ in the spirit ().

A Living Person, Not a Religion

Religion is the apex of human culture, and most people respect it. But we do not need something related to God; we need God Himself. The Jews formed a religion from 39 books; Christianity formed a stronger one from 66.

🏛️

Religion

A way to worship God, kept in names, letters, and forms. Christianity, "churchanity," Christmas: something added to Christ.

🔥

The Living Person

The Triune God coming into us through the Word. Christ, Christians, and the church, with nothing added.

Six Marks of Vain Religion

Romans 2:17–3:8 exposes religion that has everything outwardly and nothing within:

MarkVerseExample
An outward nameAttending a service once a year to keep the name of a believer.
Outward knowledge of GodKnowing God in letters, not in the spirit and through one's whole being.
The Scriptures held outwardly; A Bible under the pillow, a finger on a random verse, or teachings searched without coming to Christ for life.
Outward forms in letterEndless arguments over sprinkling, sea, river, or Jordan, while the reality is missed.
No inward reality in spiritEverything we are, do, and have must be in our spirit, where God dwells.
The same evils as the unreligiousTeaching others not to steal, yet stealing. No real difference in living.

In Spirit, or "Political"

Love that is not in the spirit is political love. A word not spoken in the spirit is political talk. In the spirit, our love and our words are with God; without Him, everything is vain.

The Totality of Hopelessness

Do not try to cure, correct, or improve the world; its condition is incurable. Paul gives proof after proof ():

Righteous

None

A righteous man pays what is owed. Not one person has done this before God.

Good

None

A good man gives grace beyond what is owed. Not one practices good.

Seeking God

None

No one understands or seeks God. All have turned aside and become useless.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

If Judaism and Christianity both come from God's revelation, how can they still be vanity?Distinguish God's intention in giving the Word from what man formed out of it.

God's Intention: God gave the Bible to reveal Himself into us, not to establish a religious form. Religion offers something related to God; we need God Himself as a living Person.

Stronger Form, More Vanity: More books gave Christianity a stronger religion than Judaism, but strength of form is not reality. Christ, Christians, and the church are precious; "-anity" added to them is vanity.

How can we have the Bible and still miss its reality?Read John 5:39–40 alongside Romans 3:2.

Superstition or Letters: Some treat the Bible as a charm or a lottery. More commonly, sincere believers care for its teachings but not for Christ, the living Person it testifies of.

Coming to Him: The Jews searched the Scriptures yet would not come to Christ for life (). The Word becomes life and reality only when it brings us to Him.

What does Romans 2:29 mean by doing everything "in spirit," and what does it look like in daily life?Consider baptism, love in a marriage, and the words we speak.

The Place Where God Dwells: The human spirit is where God can touch us and we can touch Him. Circumcision or baptism that is merely outward is unreal; it must be in spirit (–).

Daily Practice: Love a spouse in the spirit, not with "political" love. Speak in the spirit, so the word is with God. Do not hold to any form, even a correct one; pay full attention to the reality.

Why does Paul insist the world is totally hopeless before he speaks of salvation?Notice the difference between a righteous man and a good man, and where Paul is heading in Romans 8:10.

No Exceptions: Not one is righteous (paying what is owed) or good (giving beyond it) (). Every mouth is stopped and the whole world is under judgment (). We should not see ourselves as exceptions.

Paving the Way: Condemnation is not Paul's goal. Only those who see their hopelessness will receive Christ. His aim is "Christ is in you" (), met and enjoyed in our spirit.
