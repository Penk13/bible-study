Life-Study / Romans 041

Romans · Message 41

Saved in Life from Naturalness

God's goal is the building of the Body, and natural people cannot be built together. In Christ's life we are transformed by a metabolic change as our mind is renewed.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Redemption, Life, Building: Romans moves from what Christ's death solved to the goal reached by His life.
  • In His Life: Life is Christ's Person (); like Noah in the ark, we are saved in Him.
  • Metabolic Change: Transformation is not outward makeup but a new divine element added to our natural being.
  • For the Body: Nationality, gender, and disposition keep natural people from being built up together.
  • Renewing the Mind: Concepts are swallowed up by growth in life, not removed by argument ().

Saved in His Person

God's purpose is not merely to keep us out of hell but to build the Body as the corporate expression of His Son, and His unique way is life. We are saved not only by His life but in it (). Many who wait for heaven would feel out of place there; we still need daily, even hourly, saving from the natural man.

A Metabolic Change

Transformation adds a divine, holy, heavenly element to our natural element, producing an organic change from within. As our inward constitution changes, our outward expression changes too.

🎭

Makeup

Color painted on a pale face: an outward change only.

🍞

Nourishing Food

Taken in and assimilated, it changes the complexion from within: transformation.

Transformed for the Building

East and West, brothers and sisters, every natural disposition: none of these can be built together as they are. Some grow disappointed and think of quitting, but the train of the church life only goes forward. Our spouse and the saints around us are just what we need, for all things work together for good, the good of transformation ().

Under His Hand

Instead of resenting a slow brother or a mistake made under God's sovereignty, worship Him and give yourself to His transforming work.

The Renewing of the Mind

The source of the trouble is a mind filled with natural concepts, Eastern or Western, and even religious ones from our past. As life grows and saturates us, the mind is renewed and the concepts are swallowed up; then we are one according to the inner life ().

Knowledge

Arguing

Trying to convince a dissenting one only hardens the concept, like picking at a wound.

Life

Ministering Life

Praying and exercising the spirit to transfuse life; the life within heals the wound and renews the mind.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does it mean to be saved "in" His life rather than merely "by" it?The ark.

A Person: Christ Himself is the life ().

A Sphere: Noah's family was saved in the ark; as long as we remain in Christ, we are in the process of being saved.

How does transformation differ from behavior change?Makeup or food.

Outward Correction: Adjusting conduct is makeup; the natural element remains.

Organic Change: Transformation comes from the divine element being added within, metabolically changing what we are ().

Why is naturalness a problem for the church?Building materials.

Not Buildable: Natural differences of culture, gender, and disposition cannot be fitted together.

The Goal: God wants one Body, members one of another (), so we must be transformed.

A brother holds a concept I disagree with. What should I do?Leave the wound alone.

Do Not Argue: Pressure strengthens a dissenting concept.

Minister Life: Pray for him and supply life from your spirit; as he grows, the concept is swallowed up.
