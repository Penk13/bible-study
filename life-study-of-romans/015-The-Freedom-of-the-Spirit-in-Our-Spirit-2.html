Romans · Message 15

The Freedom of the Spirit in Our Spirit (2)

There are two condemnations: God's, removed by the blood, and our own self-condemnation, removed only by the law of the Spirit of life. God sent His Son in the likeness of the flesh of sin and condemned sin there, so that, as we walk by the mingled spirit with our mind set on it, the law's requirement is fulfilled in us spontaneously.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Condemnations: Objective, from God (), solved by the blood. Subjective, from ourselves (), growing the harder we try to be good.
  • No Condemnation in Christ: Not because of the blood, but because the law of the Spirit of life frees us from the law of sin and death (–), daily, as we live in the mingled spirit.
  • Up, Not Through: Surrounded by the enemy, do not fight through; say "Praise the Lord!" and rise above. The right medicine for self-condemnation is the Spirit, not more striving.
  • Sin Condemned in the Flesh: The law was weak through the flesh. God sent His Son in the likeness of the flesh of sin, like the bronze serpent (; ), and condemned sin there ().
  • The Mind as Wife: In Romans 7 the mind acts independently (); in Romans 8 it is set on the spirit, its "husband," and is life and peace ().

Two Kinds of Condemnation

Many believers, having solved God's condemnation, create for themselves the burden of self-condemnation. God would say, "You don't need this; you caused it yourself."

Fighting Through

A sister resolves to submit; that morning her husband is unusually difficult. The more she suppresses her irritation, the angrier she becomes.

🚁

Going Up

Instead of struggling, "Praise the Lord! Amen!" She is transcendent, and every enemy is under her feet. The law of the Spirit of life frees in a "super" way.

Sin Condemned in the Flesh

What the law could not do, being weak through the flesh, God did (). The body is weak toward God's law but strong toward sin.

The Mind Returning to the Spirit

The same mind can act two ways. Its position is that of a wife.

Romans 7:25

Independent Mind

"With the mind I myself serve the law of God": a wife acting as a husband, trying on her own and failing.

Romans 8:6

Mind Set on the Spirit

"Dear husband spirit, what should I do?" "You need do nothing; I will take care of it." Life for enjoyment, peace for rest.

Spontaneous Fulfillment

You may have no intention of loving your wife, yet find you love her; no awareness of submitting, yet you submit. The requirement of the law is fulfilled in us, not by us ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why doesn't applying the blood relieve self-condemnation?Distinguish the two kinds of condemnation.

Wrong Medicine: The blood removes God's objective condemnation. Self-condemnation comes from our own attempts to keep the law, and more effort, even more praying of the same kind, only deepens it.

Right Medicine: The law of the Spirit of life in our spirit frees us from the law of sin and death (). Turn to the spirit and praise; that is the release.

Why was the law of God "weak through the flesh"?Compare how energetic your body is for a prayer meeting versus for entertainment.

A Paralyzed Body: Sin dwells in the body, making it the flesh of sin (; ). Like a crippling disease, it leaves us weak toward God's law.

Strong Toward Sin: The same body is energetic for sinning. The problem is not the law but sin and its helper, the flesh.

What does the bronze serpent teach about Christ coming "in the likeness of the flesh of sin"?Read Numbers 21:9 and John 3:14.

Form Without Poison: The bronze serpent had a serpent's form but no venom. Christ took the likeness of sinful flesh without sin's nature ().

Judging Satan: Lifted up on the cross (), He was counted as the serpent, and the ruler of this world was cast out (). Sin and the flesh were dealt with together.

What does it mean practically to set the mind on the spirit?Think of the mind as a wife returning to her husband.

Not Acting Alone: The mind represents the person. When it acts independently, we act on our own and fail ().

Referring Back: When the mind returns to the spirit, we rely on Christ as the indwelling Spirit. The result is life and peace (), and the law is fulfilled without our striving.
