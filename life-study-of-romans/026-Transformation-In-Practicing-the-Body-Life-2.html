Romans · Message 26

Transformation in Practicing the Body Life (2)

The Body life is practiced as we exercise our gifts. The gifts of Romans 12 are not miraculous; they are gifts of grace in life, abilities that grow out of the divine life we have enjoyed and assimilated.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Sober-Minded: The high opinion we hold of ourselves must be torn down. Our measure of faith is the amount of God's element infused into us ().
  • Severally Members: "Severally" means differently, not separately. Each member does what others cannot, so we must coordinate.
  • Grace Is Life: Grace is God in Christ as our enjoyment; it reigns unto eternal life (). Gifts come out of the grace we have assimilated.
  • Seven Gifts: Prophecy, service, teaching, exhortation, giving, leading, and showing mercy: all that a local church needs for the Body life.
  • Not Miraculous: Tongues, healing, and miracles are absent from Romans 12. Miraculous gifts tend to divide; gifts of grace in life build up.

Thinking Soberly, Functioning Differently

Outwardly we may seem humble, yet inwardly most of us think highly of ourselves, and this troubles the church life. A mind that exalts itself is not sober; it needs to be renewed as Christ's life swallows up its negative elements. We are one Body with many members, and no two have the same function (). The eye cannot do what the nose does, nor the ear what the lips do. Realizing this, we respect others and coordinate with them instead of living as detached units.

Gifts of Grace in Life

When the divine element enters us as grace, it brings skills and abilities with it. The more of God's life we enjoy, the greater the gift; the less we enjoy, the more limited it is. Paul could say he labored more than all, yet not he but the grace of God with him (). These seven gifts together cover the practical needs of a local church.

Miraculous Gifts versus Growth

Romans was written from Corinth, about a year after Paul had to adjust the abuse of tongues and miracles there. Writing on the Body, he left those gifts out entirely. Tongues build up the speaker, but prophesying builds up the church (). The church is God's farm before it is God's building (): what grows on the farm becomes the material for the building.

🫏

Balaam's Donkey

It spoke a human language with no human life and no growth: a miraculous gift that does not depend on maturity.

👶

A Growing Child

Born with every organ, it walks and speaks only as it is fed and grows. So the gifts of Romans 12 emerge with the growth in life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is a gift of grace in life, intrinsically?An overflow, not a sudden endowment.

Definition: It is a skill or ability that proceeds out of the divine life we have enjoyed and assimilated. Grace is God as life for our enjoyment ().

Its Measure: Like the abilities of a mature human body, the gift is limited by the measure of life within.

How do the miraculous gifts compare with the gifts of grace in life?The donkey and the elder.

Miraculous: They come suddenly, regardless of growth, and they tend to build up the individual and divide the church.

In Life: No one becomes an elder overnight. Eldership requires growth day by day and year by year, and such gifts build up the Body ().

How can seeking gifts become an empty pursuit?Paul wrote Romans from Corinth.

The Danger: Prizing outward manifestations bypasses the growth in life, and Corinth shows how quickly that leads to confusion and division.

The Way: Feeding, planting, and watering produce gold, silver, and precious stones, transformed material for God's building.

What does thinking soberly look like in the church life today?"What I can do, you cannot."

Self-Estimate: We measure ourselves by how much of God has been infused into us, not by natural ability ().

Toward Others: Seeing that every member has a function we lack, we are happy when others function and we coordinate rather than compete.
