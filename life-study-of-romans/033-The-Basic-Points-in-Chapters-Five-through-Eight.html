Romans · Message 33

The Basic Points in Chapters Five through Eight

Romans 5 and 6 give two facts: we were in Adam and we are now in Christ. Romans 7 and 8 give two experiences: Adam is experienced in the flesh, and Christ is experienced in the spirit.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Titles: Chapter 5, in Adam; chapter 6, in Christ; chapter 7, in the flesh; chapter 8, in the spirit.
  • In Adam: Sin, the reign of death, and being constituted sinners under condemnation ().
  • In Christ: Grace with righteousness, reigning in life, and justification (); we entered by believing and being baptized into Him.
  • Don't Make Up Your Mind: Resolving to do good is going back to the law and lands us in Romans 7.
  • Set the Mind on the Spirit: Walk according to the spirit, and the indwelling Christ gives life to our whole being ().

Two Facts and Two Experiences

The fact of chapter five is lived out in the experience of chapter seven; the fact of chapter six can be enjoyed only through the experience of chapter eight. The spirit in chapter eight is the mingled spirit, the divine Spirit with our human spirit.

Transferred into Christ

We are put into Christ by believing into Him () and by being baptized into Him (); it is God who placed us there (). Like the eight in Noah's ark, whatever Christ passed through is ours: His death terminated our old man (), and we live with Him in resurrection. Reckoning on these facts, we present ourselves and our members to God (), and the divine life is free to sanctify us from within.

Believe and Reckon

"I died with Christ; I am alive to God in Him" (). These are facts to stand on, not feelings to produce.

From the Flesh to the Spirit

Having died, we are discharged from the law (). Yet every resolution to be humble or to love our spouse, even when prayed to God, is a self-made law, and it exposes us: the willing is present, but the doing is not (). The mind, meant to be a submissive wife, has presumed to be the husband ().

Romans 7

Making Up the Mind

Resolve to do good, pray for help, and fail more than ever ().

Romans 8

Setting the Mind

Walk according to the spirit and set the mind on the spirit (; ).

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How do the facts of chapters 5–6 relate to the experiences of chapters 7–8?Fact, then experience.

Adam: What we inherited in Adam becomes real to us whenever we live in the flesh.

Christ: What we have in Christ becomes real only when we are in the spirit ().

How do I know that I am in Christ?God put us there.

The Way: By believing into Him and being baptized into Him ().

The Result: As in the ark, all Christ passed through, His death and resurrection, is ours to reckon on.

Why is praying "Lord, help me to love my wife" a return to the law?The wrong source.

Self-Made Law: It is still the self resolving to do good, merely asking God to back it.

The Outcome: Romans 7: the good I will, I do not (). The self must be rejected, not reinforced.

What does it mean today to set my mind on the spirit?The mind as a wife.

Not Independent: The mind stops initiating its own projects and turns to the spirit.

Life Imparted: Then the indwelling Christ supplies life even to our mortal body (), doing more than the law could require.
