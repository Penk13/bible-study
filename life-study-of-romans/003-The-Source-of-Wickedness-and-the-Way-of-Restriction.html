Romans · Message 3

The Source of Wickedness and the Way of Restriction

All human evil flows from one source: refusing to hold God, the reality of the universe, in honor and knowledge. Yet God has built into man a way of restriction: His creation, His truth, our nature, our conscience, and our reasonings.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Condemnation in Four Aspects: On mankind in general, the self-righteous, the religious, and the whole world. Good or bad, religious or not, all are under God's condemnation.
  • Truth Is Reality: Truth is not mere doctrine; the prime reality is God Himself. Man held this truth down in unrighteousness ().
  • Four Elements of the Source: Suppressing the truth, refusing to hold God in full knowledge (), not glorifying or thanking Him (), and exchanging Him for idols (; ).
  • Give Up God, Be Given Up: God gives man up to uncleanness, dishonorable passions, and a disapproved mind (, , ), ending in fornication, which is confusion in order.
  • Five Restrictions: Know God by creation (), hold the truth in righteousness, live by our God-created nature, heed the conscience, and care for proper reasonings ().

Condemnation in Four Aspects

After the introduction, Paul brings every kind of person under condemnation. This message draws two lessons from the first two aspects: the source of evil and the way to restrict it.

The Source of Wickedness

These four elements are the origin of every kind of evil and sinfulness.

Holding Down the Truth

God is real, solid, and substantial. Mankind did not respect this reality but suppressed it unrighteously. This is the most significant aspect.

Disapproving to Know God

"We know there is a God, but we don't like to believe in Him." Having tested Him, man refused to hold God in full knowledge.

;

Not Glorifying or Worshipping

Not glorifying, thanking, worshipping, or serving God. All divorce and immorality come from this one source.

Exchanging God

Giving up God, the glory and reality, for idols, which are vanity. Today's idols are often one's future, position, degrees, and goals.

The Result of Giving Up God

When we let go of God, He is compelled to let go of us. A person given up by God never improves; he only descends lower.

The Way of Restriction

Although we are saved and living somewhere in Romans 5–8, we still need the five items that restrict us from evil:

Knowing God by creationThe human eye, teeth, and face all declare a Designer. Seeing Him, we worship.
Holding the truth in righteousness, , Keep God in full knowledge; glorify, thank, worship, and serve Him. The greatest blessing in human life is to be trained to worship God.
Obeying our natureOur God-created nature corresponds to His law. Even a thief's nature says, "Don't do this."

Worship: The Escape from a Bad Temper

Suppressing a bad disposition never succeeds. But when about to lose your temper, say, "God, I thank You that You are my God and my Creator. I worship You and serve You." Worship frees us at once.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is "the truth" in Romans 1:18, and what does it mean to hold it down in unrighteousness?Distinguish truth as doctrine from truth as reality.

Truth Is Reality: Truth is not mere knowledge; it is what is solid and genuine. The prime reality of the universe is God Himself. To deny Him is to speak vanity.

Suppression: Mankind knew God was real but did not care for that reality, holding it down instead of holding it rightly (). This, more than any outward sin, is the root of the evil that fills society.

Why does giving up God lead to being given up by God, and why is fornication called "confusion in order"?Trace Romans 1:24, 26, and 28, then consider God's ruling principle for marriage.

Forced to Let Go: God does not abandon man arbitrarily. When man lets go of God, God is compelled to let go of man, giving him up to uncleanness, dishonorable passions, and a disapproved mind (, , ).

Confusion in Order: One husband for one wife is God's governing principle. Fornication breaks that principle and throws human life into confusion, from which every other evil issues (1:29–32).

How can people today "exchange God" without bowing to any visible idol?Consider what Romans 1:23 and 1:25 mean when the idol is invisible.

Self-Made Idols: To exchange God is to give Him up for something else (). A future, a position, a degree, or a goal becomes an idol once we care for it instead of God.

Glory vs. Vanity: God is the glory and reality; every substitute is vanity and a lie (). The test is simple: what do we actually care for?

If we are saved and living in Romans 5–8, why do we still need our nature, conscience, and reasonings?Weigh the claim that "nothing natural can be good" against Romans 2:14–15.

Created Good: Our nature was poisoned by the fall, yet it was created good by God and still corresponds to His law, which is written in our hearts (). Condemning everything natural ignores what God built in.

Daily Protection: Nature, conscience, and reasonings are safeguards God placed in every person. Listening to them, while knowing God by creation and worshipping Him, keeps us from every kind of evil.
