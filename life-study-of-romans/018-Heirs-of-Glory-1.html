Life-Study / Romans 018

Romans · Message 18

Heirs of Glory (1)

Glorification is for the full sonship of God's children. We may be children without the growth of sons, and sons without the qualification of heirs. The Spirit's witness assures us we are children; His leading, by the inner sense of life, marks us out as sons.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Goal of Glorification: Condemnation requires justification, justification is for sanctification, sanctification is for glorification, and glorification is for full sonship.
  • All Are Sons: In spirit brothers and sisters alike are sons of God; in resurrection there is no male and female (). In the flesh today the distinction remains.
  • Abba, Father: The Spirit of the Son in our spirit makes it a spirit of sonship (; ). We cry, and He cries in our crying.
  • Witnessing With: The Spirit witnesses with our spirit, not merely in it (). If your spirit witnesses, He is witnessing too.
  • Leading by Life: Not signs or smooth circumstances, but the inner sense of life that comes from walking according to spirit and setting the mind on the spirit (, ).

Children, Sons, Heirs

The words children of God and sons of God first appear in Romans at 8:14. Paul turns from the sanctified ones to sonship, but his ultimate concept in this section is the heirs ().

The Spirit of Sonship

Romans 8:15 says we cry; Galatians 4:6 says the Spirit cries. Both are true, because the two spirits are mingled as one. Abba (Aramaic) and Father together are intimacy intensified, like a child's "Daddy," "Baba," or "Papa."

Romans 8:15

We Cry

Not a spirit of slavery to fear, but a spirit of sonship.

Galatians 4:6

He Cries in Our Crying

God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts.

The Leading of the Spirit

Verse 14 begins with For: the leading continues the walk according to spirit in the preceding verses. It is an issue of the inner life, continual like breathing, not an occasional event.

Mistaken Concept

Outward Signs

"If the store is open, it means the Lord wants me to go." Balaam's donkey was led to speak by a miraculous gift, yet had no human life.

Romans 8:14

The Inner Sense of Life

Strengthened, watered, and at peace, or empty, dry, and frustrated. Obeying this sense is being led by the Spirit.

A Mark of Sons

An inward forbidding in worldly talk or worldly fashion marks us out as different from the children of the evil one. Unbelievers lack this leading because they lack God's life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the difference between being a child of God and a son of God?Compare verse 16 with verse 14.

Children: The initial stage, concerning birth. The Spirit's witness with our spirit gives assurance ().

Sons: A more advanced stage, concerning growth. Living by the Spirit's leading gives the mark (). All real Christians are children; not all bear the mark of sons.

Why does Paul say the Spirit witnesses "with" our spirit rather than "in" it?What if you cannot feel the Holy Spirit?

Two Witnesses: "With" means both spirits witness together. When our spirit witnesses, the Spirit is witnessing too, for the two are mingled as one.

Assurance: You may not sense the Holy Spirit, but you cannot deny your spirit's witness. If calling "Abba, Father" is sweet within, you are a son of God.

How can good circumstances become a substitute for the Spirit's leading?Smooth outwardly, empty inwardly.

The Deviation: Money in hand, agreeable family, good weather, little traffic, even "all things work together for good" can justify what the inner life opposes.

The Test: Not what is outward, but whether there is anointing, watering, and peace within. The mind set on the spirit is life ().

Is the Spirit's leading in your daily life continual, like breathing, or an occasional event?Normal breathing is not an accident.

A Matter of Life: Since the leading comes from the divine life, it should be present even in small things: what we say, how we dress, where we go.

Practice: Turn the mind to the spirit and do not violate the inner consciousness. This is how children grow to be sons, and sons are matured to be heirs.
