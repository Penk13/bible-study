Life-Study / Romans 008

Romans · Message 8

The Subjective Experience of Justification (2)

Faith is not a human virtue but our reaction to God's transfusion. Abraham was no giant of faith; God appeared to him again and again until he believed. Through grace, not law, the reckoned righteousness becomes Isaac, and Isaac offered back produces the kingdom. All this is a return to the tree of life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Romans Follows Genesis: Creation (), the fall's stages, the ark's cover in chapter 3, and Abraham's full history in chapter 4. Without chapter 4 we would have the seed of justification without the kernel.
  • Transfusion → Infusion → Reaction: Like electricity through a fuse, God transfuses Himself into us, saturating us. Our reaction is faith, the "faith of Jesus" ().
  • No Giant of Faith: The God of glory appeared to Abraham () four times before Genesis 15. The fifth appearing brought the believing that was reckoned as righteousness ().
  • Sarah, Not Hagar: Hagar is law and yields Ishmael (); Sarah is grace and yields Isaac. Isaac's birth was God's coming (; ).
  • Back to the Tree of Life: The fall was turning to independence (). God called Abraham back to dependence. Offering Isaac on Moriah made Abraham a father of the kingdom.

Faith as Our Reaction to God

Witness Lee chose the word transfuse for the interaction between God and man. God is like heavenly electricity, or like radium: sit under Him and He penetrates you without your knowing.

"Something Got into Me"

At nineteen, hearing a young sister preach, Witness Lee was conquered without anyone arguing with him. Others have said, "I didn't want Jesus, but something got into me and I can't cast it away." That is Christ transfused.

From Reckoned Righteousness to Isaac

Righteousness was reckoned to Abraham, but he did not yet experience it concretely. The same is true of us after we first believe.

Hagar · The Law

Ishmael

Resolving to honor parents, love a spouse, or submit is marrying Hagar. "To will is present with me, but to do is not" ().

Sarah · Grace

Isaac

Joined to grace, we experience Christ as our righteousness. Isaac's birth was the coming of God: "At this set time next year I will return" (; ).

Offering Isaac for God's Satisfaction

We treasure our experience of Christ as our only begotten. Then God asks for it on the altar. Those who refuse keep their enjoyment but are deadened; God cannot go further with them.

The tree of life means dependence; the tree of knowledge means independence (). God did not tell Abraham where to go because He was bringing him back to Himself. Today the self must be terminated so we can say, "It is no longer I, but Christ" ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is it freeing to know Abraham was not a "giant of faith"?Count God's appearings before Genesis 15.

God Was the Giant: God appeared to Abraham in Mesopotamia (), twice in Genesis 12, and again in Genesis 13 before the promise of Genesis 15. Each time He stayed, and His element was infused.

Our Way Too: We do not need to manufacture faith. We need to stay in God's presence. Believing will rise as a reaction, and God will reckon it as righteousness ().

What is the difference between going to Hagar and going to Sarah in daily life?Read Galatians 4:24 and Romans 7:18.

Hagar: Making up our mind to do good is turning to the law (). The willing is there but not the doing (), and any success is only an Ishmael.

Sarah: Cooperating with grace means dropping our resolutions and opening to the Christ already within. The fruit is Isaac, a genuine experience of Christ as righteousness.

Why must even our precious experience of Christ be offered back to God?Consider what happened to Abraham on Mount Moriah.

Our Satisfaction or His: Our Isaac-experience satisfies us. God asks for it so that He may be satisfied. Refusing leaves us with our enjoyment but deadens our spiritual life.

Resurrection and Increase: Abraham received Isaac back in resurrection (), matured to be like the Father, and became father of a kingdom. Offering our individual Christ brings us into the church with many Isaacs.

How does justification bring us back to the tree of life?Contrast dependence and independence in Genesis 2:9.

The Real Fall: Man was made to God but turned to the tree of knowledge, living independently (). That is the meaning of the fall.

Every Hour Dependent: God called Abraham without telling him where to go so he would cleave to God moment by moment. Under His transfusion we learn to do nothing by ourselves: "It is no longer I, but Christ" ().
