Romans · Message 34

Being Freed from Sin, the Law, and the Flesh

As constituted sinners we face three problems. The crucified old man frees us from sin and from the law; walking according to the spirit frees us from the flesh.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Stations: Justification, sanctification, the Body, and the churches; many Christians stop early, and many only talk about the Body.
  • Past, Present, Future: Christ's death deals with what we did, His living in us with what we are, and His coming brings us into glory.
  • Facts, Not Feelings: Born in Adam, we have been put into Christ () whether we feel it or not.
  • Sin and Law: The old man, as the sinning person and as the self-assuming husband, was crucified; we are freed from both.
  • The Flesh: Freed only by setting the mind on the spirit and walking according to it, not by willing ().

Four Stations

God is turning sinners into sons to form the Body of Christ, and Romans traces the route. Some are content to be saved; some seek the inner life and reach Romans 8; some talk much of the Body yet multiply divisions. The Body becomes real only in the local churches.

Three Problems, Three Releases

We are sinners by constitution, not merely by conduct (). Our old man was crucified, so the body of sin is made of none effect: the instrument has lost its job because the sinning person is buried (; ). The law was given to expose man, but man tried to keep it; in Romans 7 the same old man is the husband who usurped his place, and his death releases us. The flesh, our fallen body, still wars against the law of the mind ().

Half Grace, Half Law

After offending a brother we confess and apply the blood; that is Romans 3 and 4, and it is right. But then we resolve never to do it again and ask the Lord to help us; that is Romans 7. The willing is present, the doing is not (), and the failure soon repeats.

The Key

Stop willing. When tempted to make up your mind, refuse it, and instead set your mind on the spirit and walk by it (). Live in Romans 8, not 5, 6, or 7.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can Body talk produce more division instead of the Body?Where is the Body?

Only Talk: A teaching about the Body, made into a banner, becomes one more group.

The Reality: The Body is realized only in the practical church life in localities.

I lost my temper an hour ago. Can I still say I am in Christ?Feelings may lie.

The Fact: God put us into Christ (); it does not change with our behavior or feelings.

The Response: Declare the fact, and our old man's crucification with it ().

What does "the body of sin made of none effect" mean?An unemployed instrument.

The Instrument: The fallen body is tireless for sin, but it is the tool, not the person.

Unemployed: With the sinning person crucified and buried, the tool has no one to work for; we are freed from sin ().

After I confess a failure, what should I do next?Not a resolution.

Grace: Confessing and applying the blood is right; stay there.

Not Law: Skip the vow to do better. Turn the mind to the spirit; we owe the flesh nothing ().
