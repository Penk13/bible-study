Romans · Message 35

Being Freed from Death (1)

Sin issues in the law, the flesh, and finally death, which deadens us today. The Spirit of life, making His home in us, spreads life from our spirit even into our mortal body.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Poison, Not a Mistake: In the fall Satan's nature entered man as sin; acts like lying are only its fruit ().
  • The Fourth Problem: Sin brings in the law, the flesh, and death; death is its ultimate issue.
  • Death Today: Death is active now, weakening us and silencing us in the meetings, not something waiting at the end of life.
  • One Body, Two Names: Toward sin the body is strong, the body of sin; toward God it is weak, the body of this death.
  • The Way Out: Turn to the spirit and give Christ ground to make His home in us; He gives life to our mortal body ().

Sin and Its Issues

A child who drinks poison has done wrong, but the mother worries about what is now inside him. Likewise in the fall an evil element entered man and constituted him a sinner (). Romans speaks of sin as a person: it enters, reigns, deceives, kills, and dwells in us (). Its issues are four problems, each with its own release.

ProblemFreed by
SinThe old man crucified; the body of sin unemployed
The LawThe old husband buried; married to another
The FleshWalking according to the spirit
DeathThe Spirit of life spreading from our spirit into our body

"This Death"

Paul could avoid stealing but not coveting, an inward desire he could not control. The more he strove to be perfect, the more he was being killed, until he cried for deliverance from "the body of this death" (). Sin and death are one law with two elements (). Death kills body, soul, and spirit; even careful deliberation before praising in a meeting, guarding our face, is its working.

Toward sin

The Body of Sin

Energetic and tireless for worldly things ().

Toward God

The Body of This Death

Suddenly tired and needing rest when it is time to meet or pray.

Giving Christ Ground

Because Christ is in us, the spirit is life even though the body is dead (). But verse 11 goes further: the Spirit must not only be in us but dwell in us, the same weighty thought as Christ making His home in our hearts (). As we give Him ground, life flows from the spirit into our deadened members, just as those fasting in the spirit are sustained by strength from within.

Which Element Will We Cook With?

Sin and death are in us, but so are the Spirit of life and the indwelling Christ. Do not believe the feeling of tiredness and do not make resolutions; turn to the spirit, call on the Lord, and let Him spread.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the fall more serious than a mistake?The poison bottle.

An Element Entered: Satan's nature was injected into man as sin, so we are sinners by constitution.

Its Fruit: Lying and stealing come out of that nature; the deepest result is death ().

I am too tired to go to the meeting tonight. Is that a fact or a symptom?Same body, two names.

A Symptom: The body that is lively for worldly things becomes the body of death toward God; the feeling is usually a lie.

The Remedy: Turn to the spirit and call on the Lord; the Spirit gives life even to the mortal body ().

What is the difference between Christ being in me and Christ dwelling in me?Romans 8:10 and 8:11.

In: Christ in our spirit makes the spirit life, while the body remains dead ().

Indwelling: To dwell is to make home and settle down (). When we give Him ground, He spreads into our members.

Why is carefulness in the meetings a sign of death?Whose face is protected?

Self-Glory: Weighing every word before praising usually protects our own face.

Release: A meeting is not a witness stand. Drop the consideration, release the spirit, and life will flow.
