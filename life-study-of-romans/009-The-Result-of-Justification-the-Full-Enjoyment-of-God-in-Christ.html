Romans · Message 9

The Result of Justification — the Full Enjoyment of God in Christ

Romans 5:1–11 concludes justification by showing its issue: six wonderful things (love, grace, peace, hope, life, glory) and the Triune God as our enjoyment. We stand in grace, walk in peace, boast in tribulation, hope for glory, and are being saved in His life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Six Things, Three Persons: Love (), grace and glory (), peace (), hope, life (); the Spirit, Christ, and God as our enjoyment ().
  • Love Poured Out: In trials we may doubt, but we cannot deny the love of God within. The Spirit confirms it; Christ died for us while we were weak ().
  • Stand in Grace, Walk in Peace: Out of grace? Return the way you entered: confess and apply the blood. No peace? Do not go ().
  • Tribulation Is Grace Incarnated: Rejecting tribulation is like loving God but not Jesus. Tribulation → endurance → approvedness → hope ().
  • Saved in His Life: Daily saved from temper, self, worldliness, naturalness, and individualism (), the main enjoyment we have in God.

Justified, Reconciled, and Brought into Grace

We were sinners and enemies. Through Christ's death God justified the sinners and reconciled the enemies (, ). Justification is the gate; beyond it lies a wide field of enjoyment.

Love Poured Out

"Why so many trials?" Still, the Spirit says, "Don't doubt; God loves you." He who gave His Son will not hurt us. "Lord, have Your way."

Grace for Our Stand

Grace is the realm we stand in. About to quarrel? Stop and return: "Lord, forgive me; cleanse me with the blood." Grace returns at once.

Peace for Our Walk

Witness Lee reads it as peace toward God: we are still on the way. Sinners never knew this way (); now, whatever we do, we do it in peace.

Boasting in Tribulation

The Greek for "boast" also means exult and glory. Our natural being must be transformed, so God works all things together for good (; ).

🌧️

Apparently Tribulation

As Jesus was apparently only a man, our environment appears to be only suffering. Madame Guyon kissed every cross.

🎁

Actually Grace

Tribulation is the incarnation of grace visiting us. "God gives me the cross, and the cross brings me God."

The Issue of Justification

Stand in grace, walk in peace, suffer in hope, and enjoy God in tribulation. While suffering and enjoying, we are being saved in His life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How does the love of God poured into our hearts answer our doubts in suffering?Read Romans 5:5–6 side by side.

An Inward Witness: The Spirit has poured out God's love within us (). Whatever we cannot explain outwardly, we cannot deny this love inwardly.

The Proof of the Cross: Christ died for us while we were weak and ungodly (). If God gave His Son, He will not harm us. One day we will thank Him for every trial.

What does it mean practically to "stand in grace and walk in peace"?Consider a moment when you are about to quarrel, or about to make a decision.

Standing: We can drift out of grace without sinning outwardly, simply by staying somewhere too long. Return the same way we entered: confess and apply the blood ().

Walking: Peace is the guide for every step (). Without peace, do not go; without grace to stay, do not stay.

Why is tribulation called "the incarnation of grace"?Compare rejecting tribulation with rejecting Jesus.

Hidden Visitation: God came to us hidden in the man Jesus; grace comes to us hidden in tribulation. To love God but reject Jesus is impossible; so is loving grace but rejecting tribulation.

Under Grace: Tribulation is not on grace's level but under it, serving it. It works endurance, approvedness, and hope (), all for our transformation ().

Why can a gifted believer still be a problem to others, and what is the remedy?Think of raw gold and the quality of approvedness.

Raw Gold: Light and gift may make someone productive at first, yet their rawness hurts others over time. They lack approvedness, a quality not given by birth.

The Fire: Approvedness comes through endured tribulation. Pray, "Lord, grant me approvedness," and accept the circumstances, even a spouse, He uses to produce it.
