Hebrews · Message 2

God Has Spoken

Our God is not silent like dumb idols—He speaks! In the Old Testament He spoke in many portions and ways in the prophets; today at the last of these days He has spoken to us in the Son.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Speaking God: Dumb idols cannot speak (). Our God creates, shines, and works by speaking: “He spoke, and it was done” (; ; ).
  • Threefold Transmission: When God speaks, His Word brings Light (revelation), Life (divine attributes & human virtues), and Power (the lasting, growing power of a seed).
  • The Two Stages:
    1) Of old to the fathers: In the prophets, in many portions (fragmentary), in many ways (; e.g. the burning bush in ).
    2) In these last days to us: In the Son ()—the person, embodiment, and complete expression of the Triune God.

The Nature of God’s Speaking

God’s speaking is not mere human lecturing. Whenever God speaks, three distinct divine realities are transmitted into us:

💡

1. Light (Shining)

Dispels all spiritual darkness, vanity, and confusion. We see the real universe and God's eternal economy.

🌱

2. Life (Enlivening)

Like a carnation seed, life holds all divine attributes and virtues—genuine love, humility, patience, and holiness.

⚡

3. Power (Growing)

Not emotional "mushrooming" excitement that vanishes in weeks, but the enduring, reproductive power of the divine life.

Hebrews 1:1 vs. 1:2 — The Two Stages

Compare the majestic opening words of the epistle:

To the Fathers in the Prophets

  • Instruments: Spoke in the prophets (external human vessels).
  • Portions: In many portions (fragmentary, bit-by-bit over 1,500 years).
  • Ways: In many ways (dreams, burning bush in , tabernacle types, allegories, shadows).
· Today

To Us in the Son

  • Person: Spoke in the Son—God Himself embodied in a Person!
  • Totality: One complete, final, and all-inclusive revelation.
  • Reality: No longer shadows or types, but the direct reality of God expressed and living in us.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does it mean practically that God works by speaking, and how does His Word bring light, life, and power?Contrast God's speaking with dumb idols (), and consider Genesis 1 and ("He spoke and it was done") versus human effort.

God's Way of Working: Dumb idols cannot speak (), but the living God works primarily through His speaking (; ; ). Without God's speaking, all human activity in church life is vain. When God speaks, creation and reality occur.

Light, Life, and Power: God's speaking shines divine light into our inward darkness. With that light comes the divine life, which organically contains every divine attribute (love, holiness, humility) and human virtue. Finally, this life possesses the genuine power to grow and reproduce.

Why is speaking "in the Son" fundamentally superior to speaking "in the prophets in many portions and in many ways"?Contrast external messengers giving piecemeal shadows () with the Son being the very Person and embodiment of God ().

The Prophets: The prophets were mortal men whom the Spirit came upon externally. They could only deliver fragments (portions) and types (burning bush in , tabernacle, rituals)—pieces of a puzzle over 1,500 years.

The Son: In the New Testament, God speaks not merely through a person, but in the Person of the Son (). The Son is the radiance of God's glory and the exact impress of His substance. When Jesus lived, walked, touched lepers, spoke, died, and rose, God was fully expressing Himself in a living Person.

Why does Witness Lee contrast the "growing power of a carnation seed" with "mushrooming Pentecostal excitement"? How does this apply to your personal spiritual life?Reflect on whether you seek quick emotional highs or the steady, enduring growth of the Word sown into your heart.

Mushrooming vs. Sowing: A mushroom sprouts overnight after rain, but withers just as quickly. Many spiritual movements rely on emotional sensationalism, drawing thousands temporarily who vanish after a few months.

The Power of Life: In contrast, the Word is a seed of divine life sown into the church. At first, nothing seems visible. But gradually a green sprout appears, roots grow deep, stems blossom, and seeds reproduce year after year. Genuine spiritual power is the enduring, reproductive power of life, not outward excitement.
