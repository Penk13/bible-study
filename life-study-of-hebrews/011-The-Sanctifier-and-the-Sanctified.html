Hebrews · Message 11

The Sanctifier and the Sanctified

Sanctification in Hebrews 2 is neither sinless perfection nor merely a change of position. It is dispositional: the Firstborn Son, who has both divinity and humanity, working the Father's holy nature into His many brothers, who come out of the same source.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Holiness Is Essential: Without it no one will see the Lord (). No book speaks of sanctification more than Hebrews.
  • Three Schools: Sinless perfection (unscriptural), positional change (true but incomplete), and dispositional change, which Hebrews 2 and Romans 6 reveal.
  • All Out of One: The Sanctifier and the sanctified are all out of one Father (). Same source, same life and nature, so He is not ashamed to call us brothers.
  • The Firstborn Sanctifies: The only begotten Son had only divinity. In resurrection His humanity was begotten (), and He became the Firstborn with two natures, like us.
  • Four Steps: Separated unto God (), transformed (), conformed (), glorified ().

Three Schools of Holiness

After the Reformation recovered justification, sanctification was recovered next, but not fully accurately:

Methodist · 18th century

Sinless Perfection

The Oxford students kept strict "methods" to control themselves and called that living holy. A human concept with no ground in Scripture.

Brethren · 19th century

Change of Position

Gold is sanctified by the temple, the gift by the altar (), food by prayer (). Correct, but not all.

Hebrews 2 · Romans 6

Change of Disposition

God's holy nature imparted into our being by Christ as the life-giving Spirit. Sanctification implies experience (, ).

Why "Brothers"?

No one calls a dog "brother": we are not of the same source. Christ calls us brothers without shame because He and we are both out of the Father, with the same life and nature (). That is a matter of nature, not position.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is sinless perfection not genuine holiness, and why is positional sanctification still not the whole story?Compare Matthew 23:17–19 with Hebrews 2:11 and Romans 6:22.

Not Behavior Control: Gold in the market may have no fault, yet it is common until it enters the temple (). Holiness is not the absence of sin but belonging to God.

More than Position: Hebrews 2:11 speaks of a common source, the Father. Sharing a source is a matter of life and nature. Sanctification in Romans 6 likewise bears fruit in experience (). God wants His holy nature wrought into us.

What is the difference between the only begotten Son and the Firstborn Son, and why does it matter for sanctification?Read Psalm 2:7 with Acts 13:33.

Humanity Begotten: The only begotten Son had only divinity. On earth His humanity was not yet born of God. In resurrection that humanity was begotten (), making Him the Firstborn with both natures.

One of Us: We too have divine life and human nature. Because the Firstborn shares both with us, He can sanctify us, and we are His brothers.

How do we know we are qualified to be sanctified, even when we do not feel holy?List what Hebrews 2 says has already been done for us.

Three Facts: God has been propitiated (), we have been released from the slavery of death (), and we have been born as sons of God ().

Word over Feelings: Feelings mean nothing here; the Word means everything. We are a freed people with no problem with God, qualified for the Firstborn's work.

What does metabolic transformation look like in daily life?Contrast outward adjustment with inward change by the element of life (2 Corinthians 3:18).

Not Adjustment: Methods and self-control adjust behavior from outside. Transformation is an organic change: a new element enters, replaces the old, and changes us from within ().

Beholding and Reflecting: As we behold the Lord with unveiled face, the Spirit transforms us into His image, toward the day we are conformed to the Firstborn ().
