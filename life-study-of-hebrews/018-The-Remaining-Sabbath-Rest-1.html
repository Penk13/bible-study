Life-Study / Hebrews 018

Hebrews · Message 18

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (1)

God rested on the seventh day not because His work was finished but because He had gained man to express and represent Him. That rest was lost at the fall. The remaining Sabbath rest is Christ as our rest in three stages, and today it is found in the church life, where God and man are mutually satisfied.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Not Merely the Millennium: Reading the Sabbath rest as only the seventh thousand years is partly correct but incomplete.
  • The First Sabbath: Other things were called into being; man was formed from dust after a divine counsel. Only then: "very good" (), and God rested ().
  • Rest Lost: "My Father has been working until now" (). The Jews kept the day but plotted murder; God had no rest because man was damaged.
  • Christ in Three Stages: Rest in our spirit now (); in fuller way in the kingdom (); fully in eternity. The first two are the remaining Sabbath, a prize for diligent seekers.
  • Today: The rest is "another day," "Today" (): the proper church life, where God is expressed, represented, and satisfied.

What Made God Rest

Why did God not rest on the fifth day? Not because the work was unfinished, but because man was not yet there.

Days 1–5

"Good"

Everything else was called into being by a word: "Light," and there was light.

Day 6 ·

"Very Good"

Man, made from dust in God's image to express Him and given authority to represent Him. God was satisfied and rested ().

The Meaning of the Sabbath

Whenever man expresses and represents God, that day is the Sabbath to God. When God rested, Adam rested too. It is a mutual rest.

Lost and Promised Again

The fall destroyed God's satisfaction. Since Genesis 3 the Father has been working ().

Christ as Our Rest in Three Stages

Hebrews warns believers not to go back to the old Sabbath but to be diligent to enter the remaining one.

Only the first two stages are the Sabbath rest of Hebrews 4. Many Israelites were saved through the Passover, yet few entered the land. Likewise, many genuine believers are not in the good land of the church life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did God rest on the seventh day?Compare "good" with "very good" in Genesis 1.

Not a Finished Job: God rested because He had attained His desire: man in His image to express Him and with His authority to represent Him. Only then did He say "very good" ().

Our Concepts: Reading Genesis with religious "colored glasses" makes the Sabbath a day off. Read purely, it is God's satisfaction in man.

What did the Lord mean by "My Father has been working until now"?Read John 5:16–17 with the healed man in view.

Rest Destroyed: The Jews kept the outward day while seeking to kill Jesus; they had no real rest. The man who once satisfied God had been damaged, so God had no rest either ().

Working for Rest: The Father and the Son work to recover man, so that God may again be expressed and represented and so rest.

Why does coming to Christ for rest sometimes "stop working"?Consider Matthew 11:28–29 and where Christ is found.

Not for Private Comfort: If we take Christ as rest only for individual relief, He may turn His face away. He is life and rest for the building of God's house.

Rest in the Church: Where God is expressed and represented, there is the Sabbath. Coming to Christ means coming where He is: the church ().

Why are the first two stages of rest a prize, while the third is everyone's portion?Compare the Israelites who left Egypt with those who entered Canaan.

Redeemed but Not Entered: All who kept the Passover were saved, yet few entered the land. Redemption is the portion of all; the land was the goal for those who pressed on.

The Kingdom Rest: Overcomers who enjoy Christ now will reign with Him () and enter their Lord's joy (). In eternity, all the redeemed will rest in full.
