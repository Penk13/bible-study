Hebrews · Message 12

The Many Sons, the Many Brothers, the Church

Hebrews 2:12 reveals the church at its root: a living composition of the brothers of the Firstborn Son, divine and human, in whom He declares the Father as the source of life and sings praise to the Father from within their singing.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Church in Hebrews: Mentioned only twice (; ), but 2:12 reveals its nature more basically than any other book.
  • Divine and Human: The Firstborn is divine then human; His brothers are human then divine. The church has two lives and two natures, mingled: an organism, not a building or a mere gathering.
  • The Many Sons: Predestinated unto sonship, not just salvation (), born of God in our spirit (), waiting for the redemption of our body ().
  • The Many Brothers: Brought forth in resurrection (), with the Firstborn as our elder Brother and the same life and nature ().
  • He Sings in Our Singing: He declares the Father as the source of life and praises Him in the church (). The more we praise the Father, the more we gain the Son.

What Is the Church?

Our understanding of the church has deepened step by step:

Every Church Is Wonderful

Because the church is organic, it grows and changes by life, like a tired body that is invigorated. Do not believe that a church is "not good." Our concept of the church rests on faith in what it is.

What the Firstborn Does in the Church

The church is a corporate partnership with Christ (). In it the Firstborn does two things ():

🌱

Declares the Father's Name

The Jews knew God as Creator, not as Father who begets. In resurrection He imparted the Father's life, nature, and being into His brothers, so we partake of the divine nature ().

🎶

Sings Praise in the Midst

Not once but throughout the centuries, He sings within His brothers' singing. When we open our spirit and praise the Father, He praises with us, and we enjoy and gain Him.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the church more than "a gathering of believers"?Compare gathered chairs with a living body, and consider the two natures of the Firstborn.

An Organism: A gathering of chairs can never be a body. A body has life, cells, and function. The church is the Body of Christ, living and growing.

Two Lives Mingled: As the brothers of the Firstborn (), we have both the divine life and an uplifted, resurrected human life. The church is the corporate expression of that mingling.

What difference does it make to be predestinated unto sonship rather than merely unto salvation?Read Ephesians 1:5 and Romans 8:29.

God's Goal: Traditional teaching speaks of being predestinated to be saved. Scripture says we were marked out for sonship (), to be conformed to the image of the Firstborn ().

Security and Direction: Even an "accident" that brought us to the Lord was God's arrangement. Our destiny is not to be servants like angels but sons growing toward full sonship ().

What does it mean that Christ "declared the Father's name" to His brothers?Contrast knowing God as Creator with knowing Him as Father.

More than a Title: The Father is the source of life and nature. Declaring His name was an impartation of the Father's life, nature, and being into the disciples on the day of resurrection.

Our Portion: That is why Peter could say we are partakers of the divine nature (). To us God is not only the creating God but the begetting Father.

How can we cooperate with the Firstborn who sings in the church?Think of your opening moments in a church meeting.

Open Your Mouth: When we come to a meeting and immediately praise the Father from our spirit, we give the indwelling Son His opportunity. His singing is in our singing ().

Gaining Christ: The more we praise the Father, the more He praises within us, and the more we enjoy and gain Him. Praise is the best way for Christ to work together with us.
