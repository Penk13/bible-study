Hebrews · Message 26

The Three Stages of Salvation

God's full salvation is a gradual process in three stages: out of Egypt, through the wilderness, into Canaan. For us it is salvation from the world, through the soul, and into the spirit, matching the outer court, the Holy Place, and the Holy of Holies, all for God's expression, kingdom, and rest.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • One-Third Saved: Leaving Egypt saved Israel from judgment and slavery, but there was not yet God's expression or dominion.
  • The Wilderness Is Not All Bad: It was the way to the land, with manna and living water; only the wandering of thirty-eight years made it bad.
  • All Redeemed, Two Rewarded: All ate the lamb and the manna, but only Joshua and Caleb gained the good land ().
  • Transformation Is Mingling: Like fine flour mingled with oil (), our humanity is saturated with divinity ().
  • Into the Spirit: The Hebrews were saved but staggering in the soul; they had to press into the spirit, the Holy of Holies ().

Three Stages, Three Pictures

The lamb, the manna, the living water, and the produce of the land all type the riches of Christ. Each stage brings a richer enjoyment, and the whole is for securing the land and building the temple, where God is expressed and His people rest ().

Transformation in the Soul

Much of Christianity offers justification without an exodus. Beyond leaving the world, including religion, the essence of Christ sown in our spirit must spread into our soul. This is not moral correction but the divine element wrought into us ().

🌾

Fine Flour

Our humanity, first cleansed by the blood.

🫒

Mingled with Oil

Saturated with the Spirit; both substances remain, yet become one entity. This is transformation.

The Hebrews' Situation

The Hebrew believers had passed the first stage but wandered in the second, wondering in their mind about Judaism and in danger of drifting back. To fall from the rest, which is Christ, is to be severed from Christ (). Paul, though fully redeemed, still pressed on to gain Christ as the prize ().

Breaking the Joints

As marrow is hidden in the joints, the spirit is hidden in the soul. Dividing them requires breaking the soul by the living word (), so we may enter the rest ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is being saved from Egypt only one-third of God's salvation?What about God's purpose?

What Was Missing: Israel was freed from judgment and slavery, but there was no tabernacle and no divine government yet.

The Goal: Full salvation is for God's expression and kingdom, which bring the Sabbath rest to God and His people.

How does "mingling" differ from moral improvement?Flour and oil.

Not Correction: Transformation is not a change of behavior but Christ's element saturating our soul ().

Mingling: The flour remains flour and the oil remains oil, yet they become one (). Humanity and divinity mingled is God's economy.

Which stage are you living in today?World, soul, or spirit?

The Test: Pursuing worldly pleasures is Egypt; murmuring and reasoning is the wilderness; enjoying Christ in the spirit is Canaan.

The Way On: Do not linger in a wandering soul. Deny it, turn to the spirit, and come forward to the Holy of Holies ().

Why did only Joshua and Caleb enter the land?Redemption versus prize.

Same Redemption: All shared the lamb, the manna, and the water. Redemption was not the issue.

The Prize: Only those who believed God's word and pressed on gained the land. Likewise, only believers who seek Christ diligently will enjoy Him as their rest in the church age and the kingdom.
