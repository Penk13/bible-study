Hebrews · Message 29

The Word of Righteousness and the Word of the Beginning of Christ

God's word is food, not mere knowledge. The word of the beginning of Christ is milk, the foundation of the Christian life; the word of righteousness, concerning God's governmental dealings with His people, is solid food for the mature.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Dull of Hearing: The Hebrews should have been teachers but still needed milk, so the word about Melchisedec was hard to explain ().
  • The Word Is Food: We live by every word from God's mouth (); even teaching is for nourishment.
  • Righteousness Means Government: Hebrews is built on God's righteous dealings with His people, the basis of all five warnings ().
  • Exercised Senses: The mature discern superior from inferior words, as one tells milk from solid food ().
  • Do Not Lay It Again: Six basic items form the foundation; we must build on it, not lay it again ().

Milk and Solid Food

The Bible divides God's word into milk for babes and solid food for the mature (). Milk is the rudiments, the "ABCs" of the oracles of God. It is not wrong, but many remain old babes, content with sweets such as being redeemed and going to heaven.

The Word of Righteousness

God loved Israel, yet He dealt with them governmentally: Miriam, Aaron, and even Moses did not enter the land (). The warnings about not entering the rest () are of this kind. Our God is a consuming fire (). Such words are hard bones that many throw away, but we remain under His government whether we accept them or not.

The Foundation: Three Pairs

The word of the beginning of Christ has six items in three pairs. In each pair the first brings us out of something negative, the second into something positive. The foundation is laid once; then it needs building up.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Do you come to the Bible mainly for knowledge or for food?Milk is drunk, not studied.

Not Mental: Even its teaching aims at spiritual realization and nourishment, not comprehension alone.

Daily Eating: As the Lord lived by every word of God (), we must eat the word daily and feed others with it.

Why is the word of righteousness harder to receive than the word of grace?Hard bones.

It Touches Government: It speaks of reward, loss, and discipline among God's own people, not of comfort.

Babes Reject It: Those who want only sweets call it heresy. The mature recognize that God's love and His righteous government both stand.

What do "good and evil" mean in Hebrews 5:14?Not morality.

Superior and Inferior: In context it is like discerning foods: Christ versus angels, Moses, and Aaron; the new covenant versus the old.

Exercised Senses: Both mind and spirit are trained by use to tell milk from solid food.

Are you ready for the Lord's coming as a time of righteousness?Reward, not curiosity.

He Brings a Reward: His coming will repay according to work (), with searching eyes of judgment.

Paul's Example: Fearing to be disqualified, he ran the race and looked to the crown of righteousness. We too must go on and not treat the Lord lightly.
