Hebrews · Message 49

The Author and Perfecter of Faith

The old saints were only witnesses of faith; Jesus alone is its Author and Perfecter. As we look away unto Him, He transfuses Himself into us as the believing element, so that our faith is really Christ believing in us.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Cloud: The people of faith are a cloud; the Lord is in it, so following the church is following His presence.
  • Author: On earth Jesus originated faith, cutting its way as Pioneer, Forerunner, and Captain.
  • Perfecter: On the throne in glory He finishes and completes the faith He began ().
  • Transfusion: We have no believing ability by nature; faith is a gift, Christ Himself imparted into us ().

Witnesses, and the One Source

Hebrews 11 lists many witnesses, but none of them was the source of faith. The cloud they form still leads, as the pillar of cloud led Israel (); yet the Originator and Completer is Jesus alone.

On Earth

The Author

Originator, Inaugurator, Pioneer, Forerunner. In His life and path He cut the way of faith like a builder of a highway; the same word as "Captain" in .

On the Throne

The Perfecter

Finisher and Completer. Seated in glory, He completes in us the faith He originated.

The Transfusion of Faith

Naturally we can only disbelieve. Saving faith is "not of yourselves" (). When we look unto Jesus, the life-giving Spirit () charges us with Himself, and believing rises spontaneously. We live by His faith ().

Charged and Charming

Gospel preaching and all ministry depend not on eloquence but on utterance, the release of the divine element. A preacher charged with God through prayer transfuses faith into hearers, sometimes against their own will.

Faith, the Firstfruit of the Law of Life

Hebrews 8 and Hebrews 11 seem unrelated, but in life one issues in the other. The law of life written within () is God's own expression; as it works in mind, emotion, and will, its first product is believing.

Hebrews 8

The Law of Life Works

The testimony of what God is, wrought into every part.

Hebrews 11

Faith Springs Up

Believing without strain; even unable to disbelieve.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the difference between a witness of faith and the Author of faith?Hebrews 11 vs. 12:2.

Witnesses: The old saints testified to faith and even died for it, but none originated it.

Author: Jesus is the source; He opened the way in His life and now completes it from the throne.

If faith is a gift, what is our part?Look away unto Jesus.

Not Producing: We cannot be giants of faith by trying; we have no believing ability of our own.

Looking: Turning away from everything to Him gives Him room to transfuse Himself into us as our faith.

What makes gospel preaching effective, according to this message?Utterance, not eloquence.

Charged First: The preacher must receive of the Lord through much prayer before speaking.

Transfusion: The divine element passes into hearers, and that infused element is faith.

Why is the most believing person the one in whom the law of life has worked the most?Hebrews 8 to 11.

Saturated: The more Christ is wrought into us, the more His believing becomes ours.

Spontaneous: Such faith comes without strife; it is the natural fruit of His life within.
