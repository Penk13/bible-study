Life-Study / Hebrews 038

Hebrews · Message 38

The Working of the Law of Life

Christ, the Firstborn Son with divinity and a glorified humanity, is the standard model. The law of life works Him into every part of our being until the many sons are reproduced in His image; this is perfection, which is glorification.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Standard Model: The Only Begotten, who embodied God (), became the Firstborn in resurrection with His humanity brought into sonship.
  • Perfection = Glorification: Not sinless perfection, but divinity mingled with a tested, resurrected, uplifted humanity.
  • Many Sons Produced: In His resurrection we were reborn; now He intercedes for our glorification, not merely our comfort.
  • The Working: The law of life renews, transforms, and conforms us to the Firstborn's image ().
  • Corporate Reproduction: Putting on the new man, the church, is the issue of this working ().

The Standard Model

Already in glory as the Only Begotten, Christ needed glorification for the humanity He put on (). Through death He made sin, Satan, and death a history; in resurrection His humanity was "sonized" according to the Spirit of holiness (), and He was begotten as the Firstborn ().

Two Kinds of Perfection

Overcoming every sin would leave us at best like a blank sheet of paper. The perfection Christ intercedes for () is having our whole being permeated with His glorified being, ending in the redemption of our body ().

In the Basement

Sinless Perfection

Not lying, not losing our temper, being humble and loving.

In the Holy of Holies

Glorification

Saturated with the Firstborn until we are His reproduction.

The Inner Sense and the Corporate Reprint

The heavenly intercession motivates the seed within; we know it by an inner sense that will not let us rest in worldly things or skip the meeting. God's goal is not scattered individuals but a mass reproduction of the model Son, the new man, which is the church ().

Elements of Our Perfection

Divinity, an uplifted and resurrected humanity, the terminating crucifixion, and every inward part saturated with Christ.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did Christ, already in glory, need to be glorified?John 17:1.

His Humanity: As God He was in glory, but the humanity He put on had to pass through a process into glory.

Our Pattern: His process is the way for our humanity to be brought into glory too.

What is wrong with a goal of simply overcoming sin?A blank sheet.

Too Low: It stays in the realm of behavior and leaves us empty of Christ.

The Real Goal: To be permeated with His glorified being until we are conformed to His image.

How do you sense the heavenly intercession in daily life?The inner "pum, pum."

An Inner Push: Something within will not let us rest when we drift toward the world or neglect the Lord.

Proof of the Model: That nothing but Christ satisfies us shows He is interceding for our glorification.

How is personal transformation tied to the church?The new man.

Renewed in Spirit: As the law of life renews the spirit of our mind, we put off the old man.

Putting On the Church: The new man is the practical church life, the corporate reprint of the Firstborn.
