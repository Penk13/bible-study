Hebrews · Message 25

An Evil Heart of Unbelief Deserting the Living God and the Living Word of God and the Parts of Man

Unbelief insults the living God more than any other sin and makes us depart from Him. The remedy is the living word, which pierces our being, divides the wandering soul from the spirit, and brings us into the spirit, where the church life and today's Sabbath are.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Worst Heart: No heart is more evil than one of unbelief. David's sin did not make God give him up; Israel's unbelief did ().
  • Acts Versus Ways: Israel saw God's acts; Moses knew His ways, the principles by which He acts ().
  • "Today": The window closed by the provocation was reopened when the church came into being ().
  • The Living Word: Living, operative, and sharp, it divides soul from spirit and discerns the thoughts and intents of the heart ().
  • Into the Spirit: The new covenant is heavenly and is in the spirit; staying in the mind keeps us wandering.

The Logic of Unbelief

An evil heart is hardened, goes astray, tests God, and is finally self-deceived. Unbelief reasons by the natural concept: "giants are there; we are grasshoppers." God's one promise to Moses should have been enough, more reliable than a signed check.

The Ten Spies

Reasoning by Sight

They mixed lies into their reasonings, provoked God, and fell in the wilderness. God could do nothing for them against His own principles.

Joshua & Caleb

Reasoning by God's Ways

"They are our bread" (). They honored God by believing, and He honored them.

The Living Word of God

Why does 4:12 suddenly speak of the word? Because Psalm 95 is that word. The Hebrews had read it often with no effect; now it must pierce the mixture in which their mind wondered and their soul wandered. If what we read is not living and dividing, we have touched only black-and-white letters.

The Parts of Man

The soul is our very self (). The spirit is the inward organ to contact God, where we are regenerated and the Holy Spirit witnesses (). The heart is not a fourth part but the soul plus the conscience. As God's temple (), we match its three sections.

Our Refuge

God's habitation is in our spirit (), and Christ, the heavenly ladder, is with our spirit (). Turning to the spirit is escaping the storm of thoughts and the germs of negative talk.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is unbelief worse than David's sin?Breaking a law versus insulting a Person.

An Insult: Every sin breaks God's law, but unbelief insults the living, faithful God Himself.

The Result: Once we turn away in unbelief, God cannot act for us against His own principles. Israel's carcasses fell in the wilderness.

Do you know God's acts or His ways?Manna every morning, yet still murmuring.

Acts: Israel saw a miracle daily, rejoiced briefly, then murmured again. We can praise at night and complain the next morning.

Ways: Knowing His principles, as Moses did (), lets us reason by His faithfulness instead of by our circumstances.

How can you tell you have touched the living word and not just print?Living, energizing, dividing.

The Test: The real word is living within, operating, and dividing our soul from our spirit ().

Discernment: It exposes which thoughts and intents are for the self and which are for God, something we cannot sort out by ourselves.

When problems with the church arise, where do they come from?The mind or the spirit?

The Mind: Criticism of elders and saints comes from our wondering mind; there no one is lovely but ourselves.

The Way Back: Let the word pierce and divide, then stand with the spirit. In the spirit we love the saints and remain in today's Sabbath. Releasing the spirit in praise can also turn us from the mind.
