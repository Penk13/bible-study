Hebrews · Message 40

The Experiences of Christ Portrayed by the Arrangement of the Furniture of the Tabernacle

The tabernacle is not only a picture of Christ but a map of our experiences of Him, moving from the outward to the inward to the innermost, and consummating in the inward law of life, the Spirit working in our spirit.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Experience, Not Typology: The recovery is not of teachings about the types but of experiencing Christ as they portray.
  • Three Sections: Outer court, Holy Place, and Holy of Holies match body, soul, and spirit, and outward, inner, and innermost experience.
  • The Incense Altar: It stands in the Holy Place but belongs to the Holy of Holies; prayer begins in the mind and ushers us into the spirit.
  • Within the Ark: The hidden manna, the budding rod, and the tables of the covenant ().
  • The Consummation: The inward law of life, the Spirit of the Triune God in our spirit, forms Christ in us ().

From the Altar to the Ark

Each step leads further in. In every group of three, the third item is the most important; we should not linger at the altar, which is only the start.

The Incense Altar Belongs to the Holy of Holies

Hebrews says the Holy of Holies had the golden altar, not that the altar was in it. There is no error: the veil only covered the ark and was destined to be removed (), and the altar's function was for the place of God's speaking. Praying with Christ as incense, we speak to God and He speaks back to us.

The Book's Emphasis

Press on from the soul, the Holy Place, into the spirit, the Holy of Holies.

Three Things Within the Ark

The inward law of life kills the Adamic element and adds the element of Christ, a metabolic transformation that makes us a corporate reproduction of the Firstborn. It is the Spirit witnessing with our spirit ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does the lampstand come after the showbread table?Life is light.

Light from Life: Light does not come from knowledge but from the Christ we eat and enjoy.

For God's Move: The seven lamps are for moving and acting in God's economy; the twelve loaves speak of an eternal supply.

How is the budding rod deeper than the incense altar?Acceptance plus authority.

Incense: Christ makes us acceptable to God.

Budding Rod: Christ in resurrection is also our God-given authority; elders and ministers of the Word need authority that comes from resurrection, not from the natural man.

What is wrong with remaining at the cross?The ABC's.

Precious but Initial: We treasure the cross and the blood, but they are the starting point.

Come Forward: The tabernacle calls us onward to the Spirit in our spirit, where Christ is wrought into us.

How does prayer usher you into the Holy of Holies?Mind to spirit.

Beginning in the Soul: We often start praying with the mind.

Ending in the Spirit: With Christ as incense, prayer brings us through the removed veil into fellowship at the throne of grace.
