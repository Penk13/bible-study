Hebrews · Message 13

A Merciful and Faithful High Priest

Hebrews 1 and 2 conclude with Christ as a High Priest: merciful because He is man, faithful because He is God. A priest's primary work is not serving God but ministering God into man, and this is how the Captain leads us into glory: by putting us on and carrying us through.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Words, Two Natures: Faithful matches His divinity: only the Almighty can always keep His word (; ). Merciful matches His humanity: He suffered as a man.
  • Fully Qualified: Incarnated, tried, put to death, propitiating, destroying the devil, releasing us, bringing forth brothers, crowned, and made Captain: all of Hebrews 1–2 qualifies Him.
  • What a Priest Is: Melchizedek, the first priest, came from God with bread and wine (). A priest primarily ministers God into man; serving God is secondary.
  • Captain by Being Priest: Like Moses needing Aaron, our Captain fulfills His leadership by ministering God to us. Ministered to, we become priests ministering God to others.
  • Succor: "He takes hold of the seed of Abraham" (): not a finger pulling us out but putting us on Himself and carrying us through ().

Merciful and Faithful

Aaron had only humanity: he could be merciful, but could he be truly faithful? Our High Priest is both God and man.

Son of God · Divine Nature

Faithful

Faithfulness needs virtue and ability. We often mean to keep our word and cannot. Nothing can frustrate God; He who promised is faithful (), and He cannot lie ().

Son of Man · Human Nature

Merciful

Made like His brothers (), tried as no one else (), misunderstood, attacked, and slandered by the religionists of His day. He knows how to sympathize.

What a Priest Really Is

The first mention of a thing sets its principle. Melchizedek did not go from man to God; he came from God to man ().

🕯️

The Low Concept

A priest serves God: burns incense, performs holy duties. Even heathen religions have such priests.

🍞

The High Concept

A priest ministers God into people: bread and wine to refresh, sustain, and nourish them with God's riches, until God is expressed in them.

Every Service Group a Priesthood

Cleaning the hall and arranging chairs is a help, but it is not yet the priesthood. The priesthood comes in when we minister God to one another as we serve.

The Captain Leads by Being the Priest

Moses needed Aaron; Joshua needed the high priest. In Christ the two offices are one person: the Captain of 2:10 is the High Priest of 2:17. Leading without ministering would leave us too exhausted to follow.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Hebrews call Christ both "merciful" and "faithful," and why could Aaron not fully be both?Match each word to one of Christ's two natures.

Ability to Keep His Word: Faithfulness requires not only a sincere heart but power to fulfill. Only the Almighty God, who cannot lie (), is fully faithful.

Experience of Suffering: Mercy requires having walked in our shoes. As a man tried in all things (), He knows. Aaron had humanity but not divinity; Christ has both.

What is the primary work of a priest, according to Melchizedek?Which direction did Melchizedek move, and what did he bring?

From God to Man: Melchizedek came out to Abraham with bread and wine (). Bread and wine signify God ministered as our enjoyment and nourishment.

Our Priesthood: God does not need our service as much as He wants us to minister Him to people. A priest who only goes to God and never goes to people is poor.

How does the Captain of salvation lead us into glory by being our High Priest?Think of Moses and Aaron, and of needing a meal after hard work.

Leadership Needs Supply: A captain who only fights ahead cannot bring weak followers through. Moses needed Aaron. Christ is both, leading on and ministering bread and wine within.

A Chain Reaction: As He ministers God into us we gain more of God, serve Him, and are sent to minister Him to others. This is how the Father leads many sons into glory ().

What is the difference between being comforted by Christ's sympathy and being succored by Him?Read Hebrews 2:16 carefully: "takes hold of."

Beyond Comfort: A low view of sympathy gives only comfort. To "take hold of" () means to come to us, put us on Himself, and carry us through, with the divine supply flowing in.

No More Threat: After experiencing this, difficulties no longer threaten us. The more difficulties, the more we enjoy God and are brought into glory ().
