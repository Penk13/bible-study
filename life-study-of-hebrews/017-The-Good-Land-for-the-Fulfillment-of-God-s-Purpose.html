Hebrews · Message 17

The Good Land for the Fulfillment of God's Purpose

In the Bible the land signifies God's proper people and is a figure of the resurrected Christ. The land, Christ, and humanity combined form God's expression and kingdom: miniatured in Canaan with the temple, foretasted in the church life, and fulfilled in the New Jerusalem on the new earth.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Land vs. Sea: The land signifies God's proper people; the sea, the world corrupted by Satan. In the new earth there is no more sea ().
  • Land as Christ: Dry land rose out of the death waters on the third day (), a type of the resurrected Christ, from whom all life comes.
  • Land, Christ, Humanity: Man was formed of that dust () in the image of God, which is Christ (), to express and represent God. These three combined are God's expression and kingdom.
  • Man and Land Are One: God judges both together, as in the flood. The ark rested on the seventeenth day of the seventh month (), the day Christ later rose in the reckoning of the sacred calendar.
  • Type, Foretaste, Fulfillment: Canaan with the temple (); the church life now; the New Jerusalem then. Milk and honey: Christ's rich life.

What the Land Signifies

Even the lowest interpretation, that the good land is heaven beyond the Jordan of death, treats it as a symbol. Scripture shows what it symbolizes.

The People

God's Proper People

God always counts the people and their land as one (Isa. 1:7–9). A corrupt people means a corrupt land.

The Person ·

The Resurrected Christ

Land out of the death waters () on the third day, the source of every kind of life.

The Soil ·

God's Chosen Ones

We are the soil in which God sows Himself, His field () to grow Christ.

An Elevated Land

Canaan is surrounded by the Mediterranean, the Dead Sea, and the Jordan: a land raised above the death waters. God called Abraham out of Chaldea, corrupted mankind one with its land, into this elevated land, signifying Christ with God's proper people.

The Good Land Through the Ages

God's eternal purpose is to express Himself through a corporate people. Each time Satan corrupted man and land, God raised up a new beginning.

Milk and Honey Today

Canaan flowed with milk and honey (): the produce of animal life and plant life.

🥛

Milk · Animal Life

Christ's life for redemption: shed blood, sacrifice, the Lamb.

🍯

Honey · Plant Life

Christ's life for regeneration: the grain of wheat that grows and multiplies.

In the proper church life we drink the living water and feed on Christ as the tree of life. Here is God's dwelling, rest, expression, and authority, a rest that Joshua could not give ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the land a type of the resurrected Christ?Read Genesis 1:2 and 1:9 together.

Out of Death Waters: The earth lay under the waters of judgment (). On the third day the dry land appeared (), as Christ rose out of death on the third day.

The Source of Life: Every kind of life, plant, animal, and human, came out of that land. Likewise all life comes out of the resurrected Christ.

How do the land, Christ, and humanity combine to fulfill God's purpose?Look at what Adam was made of and in whose image.

In Adam, a Miniature: Adam was dust from the land () in the image of God, which is Christ (), to express and represent God.

In the New Jerusalem, in Full: The new earth, Christ, and all God's glorified people together become His eternal expression and kingdom ().

Why does God always deal with the people and the land together?Consider the flood and Chaldea.

Judged as One: When man became flesh, God judged both the flesh and the earth by the flood. At Babel, corrupted mankind became one with Chaldea.

Called as One: So God called Abraham out of that land into an elevated land. Our location, spiritually, matters: whether we are in corrupt "Chaldea" or in the land that signifies Christ with His people.

In what sense is the church life a "foretaste" of the New Jerusalem?Compare a foretaste with a full taste.

Same Nature: A foretaste is the same in nature as the full taste, only smaller. What we enjoy in the church, the living water and the tree of life, is real, not a type.

Rest While Working: In the good land we find God's dwelling, expression, and authority. Even while serving we are resting, enjoying milk and honey (), Christ's rich life.
