Hebrews · Message 22

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (5)

God's purpose is to be expressed with His authority, and when He is, He rests. That purpose is not finished at the Lord's coming but at the end of the millennium, so the coming age is still a time for the Lord to deal with His believers: to reward, to chastise, and to bring the immature to ripeness.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Governing Principle: Man, earthly in substance yet bearing God's image (; ), expresses God and exercises His dominion. Then God rests. The New Jerusalem is the consummation of this.
  • Not Finished at His Coming: The millennium is only a restoration (). Satan, rebellious human nature, death, the sea, and the unsaved dead are dealt with only at its end.
  • Chastisement Is Not Perdition: We are chastened that we may not be condemned with the world (). Nothing in Scripture limits the Father's discipline to this age.
  • Ripe Ones Are Taken: One is taken and the other left (). The thief takes only treasure; the crop must be ripe to be reaped ().
  • Pay the Price Now or Later: The foolish virgins had oil in their lamps but none in their vessels (). The transforming Spirit in the soul costs a price, in this age or the next.

Three Periods, Three Sabbaths

The universe has three periods. Within the period of accomplishment, about seven thousand years, which to the Lord are as seven days (), each stage of God's work brings in a fuller rest.

What Remains after the Millennium

Most teachers say everything will be all right when the Lord returns. But five negative things are not finally dealt with until the thousand years end.

Reward and Chastisement in the Coming Age

At His coming the Lord will set up His judgment seat, and every believer will give an account of what was done after being saved (; ). This is not the white throne, which decides salvation or perdition; it decides prize or chastisement ().

Matthew 24

Two Living Saints

Working in the field and at the mill. The mature one is taken secretly, as by a thief (); the rest are raptured later ().

Matthew 25

Ten Sleeping Virgins

The dead saints, all saved. The lamp is our spirit (); the extra oil is the Spirit transforming the soul ().

An Incentive, Not a Threat

God would rather perfect us in this age. He ordained the millennial kingdom as an age of reward so that we would cooperate with His grace now and enter the rest that remains ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What makes God rest, and why is the church the first real Sabbath?Earthly substance, divine image.

The Principle: God rests when man, earthly in substance, bears His image and exercises His authority. Adam and Israel were only photographs of this.

The Reality: God sowed Himself as a grain into humanity; the many grains became one loaf (; ). In the church His image is expressed and His rule exercised, so the church is His Sabbath today.

Why is the millennium needed to complete God's purpose?Restoration is not yet renewal.

Only Restored: At the Lord's return the old heaven and earth are restored (), not made new.

Still to Be Dealt With: Satan, rebellious human nature, death, the sea, and the unsaved dead remain until its end. Only in the new heaven and new earth can we say everything is all right.

If chastisement in this age does not deny a perfect redemption, why would chastisement in the next?It is only a matter of time.

Same Principle: A wise father chooses when to discipline; a wrong child still needs it. We share in the Father's discipline as sons ().

Not Perdition: We are chastened precisely so that we are not condemned with the world (). A slothful servant would be ashamed to accept a crown.

Are you paying the price today for the extra portion of oil in your vessel?Lamp versus vessel; free versus bought.

Free and Costly: The Spirit who lit the lamp of our spirit at regeneration is free. The Spirit saturating our soul must be bought ().

Our Choice Is When: Beholding and reflecting the Lord, we are transformed (). If we will not pay the price in this age, we shall pay it in the next.
