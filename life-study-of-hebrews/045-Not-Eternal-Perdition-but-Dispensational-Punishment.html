Hebrews · Message 45

Not Eternal Perdition, but Dispensational Punishment

The "worse punishment" of Hebrews 10:29 concerns saved people, yet it is not perdition. Salvation is eternally secure; a believer who fails may still come under the Father's discipline, in this age or the next.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Errors: One school says nothing after salvation matters; the other teaches an "elevator salvation," saved and lost again and again. Both miss the pure Word.
  • Eternally Secure: Eleven things, from God's will to Christ's promise, guarantee that a saved one can never be lost.
  • Two Different Things: Eternal perdition is for unbelievers; dispensational punishment is for believers.
  • Discipline of Love: The Father disciplines His sons, now and possibly at the Lord's return, for their profit ().

The Security of Salvation

Our salvation rests entirely on what God and Christ are and have done, not on our works, so nothing we do can undo it.

Perdition and Punishment

Hebrews 10:29 speaks of one sanctified by the covenant blood, so it describes a believer. In context, the danger was forsaking the church for Judaism. That deserves punishment, but punishment is not perdition.

Unbelievers

Eternal Perdition

The second death in the lake of fire (); this is why they need the gospel.

Believers

Dispensational Punishment

The Father's chastening of sons who fail, never a casting away.

This Age and the Next

Discipline proves sonship: a father does not discipline children on the street (). Its aim is that we partake of His holiness. Most teachers place all chastening in this age, but the Lord's word shows unfaithful servants dealt with at His return; the time is the Father's choice.

A Closet of Love

Like parents who put a failing child in a dark room for a day, not a prison, God disciplines in love. Those who knew more are held to more ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can't salvation go up and down like an elevator?Whose works is it based on?

God's Doing: It rests on His will, calling, love, righteousness, covenant, power, and life, none of which change.

Christ's Doing: His eternal redemption, His hand, and His promise hold us regardless of our condition.

Why is it still wrong to say nothing matters after we are saved?Heb. 10:29.

Sons Are Disciplined: A saved person who disregards the Son, His blood, and the Spirit of grace is worthy of punishment.

Accountable: Having received the full knowledge of the truth increases our responsibility.

How should we view God's discipline?Heb. 12:5-11.

A Sign of Love: He chastens those He receives as sons; to be without it would mean we are not sons.

For Holiness: It separates us from the common so that we partake of His holy nature.

What does this warning ask of you now?Come forward.

Not Ignorant: Having heard God's economy, we cannot plead ignorance.

A Prayer: "Lord Jesus, help me to come forward" and not shrink back.
