Life-Study / Hebrews 030

Hebrews · Message 30

To Be Brought on to Maturity and Fleeing Into the Refuge with the Anchor and the Forerunner

Hebrews is a book on maturity. We go on by building on the foundation already laid, not by laying it again, and we flee everything that holds us back into the Holy of Holies, where our hope is anchored and our Forerunner has entered.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Just Go On: Forget the past, do not worry about the future, and go on; real maturity is found in the church life, not in individual spirituality.
  • One Foundation: Leave the word of the beginning of Christ and do not lay the foundation of repentance again ().
  • Not Needed, Not Possible, Not Right: Verse 1 says repeating repentance is unnecessary, verse 4 says it is impossible, verses 7–8 show it is wrong.
  • The Way to Maturity: Share Christ's attainments, enter the rest, come to the throne of grace, and feed on Christ as Melchisedec.
  • Flee: Flee everything but Christ and the church into the veil, held by hope as an anchor, following Jesus the Forerunner ().

Do Not Lay the Foundation Again

A building crew that stops after the foundation should resume on it, not pour another. A runner who falls rises from where he fell. Yet many revivals stir believers to go back and repent of the same things over and over. Verse 4 does not mean a fallen believer cannot be forgiven; it means the original repentance cannot be renewed. To try is, in God's eyes, to crucify the Son of God again ().

🌵

Thorns and Thistles

Earth that drinks the rain but bears the old religious things is disapproved and near a curse ().

🌱

Proper Vegetation

The tilled earth brings forth Christ unto maturity and partakes of blessing from God.

A Real Christian, a Real Dealing

One who was enlightened, tasted the heavenly gift, and partook of the Holy Spirit is no false believer. He will never perish, but if he bears thorns he faces God's governmental dealing. The earth is not burned away; only what it grows apart from God's economy is burned, as the wrong building is burned yet the builder is saved (). This is a word of righteousness, not a sugar-coated word.

Brought On to Maturity

The writer said "Let us go on" only after five chapters; the way to maturity is in them.

The Refuge, the Anchor, the Forerunner

The word "fled" () is intensive: flee to a safeguard. Every situation, rich or poor, even a church office, is a stormy sea that can hold us back.

Refuge

Within the Veil

Our spirit, the church life, and the Holy of Holies where Jesus is.

Anchor

Our Hope

Built on God's unchangeable promise and oath; fastened within the veil so we do not shipwreck.

Forerunner

Jesus

He fled everything, cut the way through the storm, and entered God's presence as our High Priest ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

After a failure, do you go on, or go back to lay the foundation again?Many foundations, no building.

Not Needed: The foundation was laid once when we believed; repeating the old repentance adds nothing.

Rise Where You Fell: Like a runner, simply get up and press on. The Lord wants to see us going on, not rehearsing the same confession.

Does Hebrews 6:4–6 teach that a believer who sins cannot be forgiven?Renew what?

Renewing Repentance: What is impossible is to renew the original repentance, that is, to lay the foundation a second time.

A Genuine Believer: The one described has truly tasted the heavenly things. He is not lost, but bearing thorns he comes near the curse of God's discipline.

Why is maturity tied to the church life rather than personal spirituality?Individualism is immaturity.

Not Self-Centered: Striving alone to be holy or victorious is still being in ourselves.

The Sign of Maturity: The remaining rest today is the church life; growth shows itself in being built up with others.

What do you need to flee today?Every situation is a stormy sea.

Anything That Holds Back: The world, religion, old concepts, ambition, a wandering mind, even a position in the church.

Into the Spirit: Turn from the staggering soul into the spirit, where hope is anchored within the veil and the Forerunner has already gone.
