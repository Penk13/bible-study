Hebrews · Message 42

The Two Manifestations of Christ

Hebrews 9 concludes chapters seven to nine with Christ's two manifestations and the interval between them. These three compose God's economy: all accomplished, now being wrought into us, then consummated in a full salvation.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Before Christ: There were a plan, promises, and models like the tabernacle, but nothing real was accomplished.
  • First Manifestation: In thirty-three and a half years Christ put away sin, found eternal redemption, consummated the covenant, and bequeathed the will.
  • The Interval: In the heavenly Holy of Holies He appears before God for us and executes the new testament ().
  • Second Manifestation: He comes apart from sin, unto a wider and higher salvation ().

God's Economy in Three Parts

Two thousand years seem long to us, but to the Lord they are less than two days ().

All Accomplished, Now Wrought In

Sin was put away long before we committed any; it is a bequest, so we should not believe Satan, our feelings, or our failures. Christ sits in the heavens because the work is finished, yet He intercedes () while, as the all-inclusive Spirit, He motivates the law of life to work the Firstborn into us.

The New Testament Is a Will

Not a book of promises to wait for, but a will whose every item is already ours.

Unto Salvation

The salvation His coming brings is far more than being rescued from hell. His ministry today is building it up; when it is complete, He will appear.

Body Redeemed

Our vile body transfigured into a glorious one ().

Freed from Vanity

Delivered from the slavery of corruption into the freedom of glory ().

Glorified, Full Sonship

Brought into glory (); the sonship in our spirit made full ().

The Full Taste of God

The Spirit's firstfruit is a foretaste; then spirit, soul, and body will enjoy God in full.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How should you answer when your failures accuse you?Sin is a history.

Already Done: Christ put away sin at His first manifestation, before any of our sins were committed.

Stand on the Fact: Feelings and accusations are lies against what God has accomplished and bequeathed.

Why is Christ sitting, and yet still active, in the heavens?Finished work, ongoing ministry.

Sitting: In the Bible, to sit means the work is finished; nothing more needs to be accomplished.

Ministering: He intercedes and executes the will so that what He accomplished is wrought into our being.

Why will His second coming be "apart from sin"?Heb. 9:26-28.

Once for All: He died once to bear the sins of many, so sin is no longer an issue.

For Glory: His return is wholly for completing our salvation.

How does today's transformation relate to the coming salvation?Spirit, soul, body.

In Process: Regenerated in spirit, we are now being transformed in soul.

Completed: At His coming our body is transfigured and our whole being saturated with God; His present ministry prepares us for that day.
