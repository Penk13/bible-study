Life-Study / Hebrews 009

Hebrews · Message 9

The Captain of Salvation (1)

Glory is not an outward shining we are carried into; it is the blossoming of the divine life sown into us at regeneration. Jesus, perfected through sufferings, fought His way into this glory as the Pioneer, and now as Captain He leads God's many sons along the same way.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Why a Captain? A captain leads a people into a place. Salvation needs a Savior to rescue us, and also a Captain to lead us into glory ().
  • Glory Is Subjective: Already given (), ordained before the ages (), it is Christ in us, the hope of glory (), the seed that will blossom.
  • Glory Through Fighting: A carnation seed fights its shell and even the soil that feeds it before it blossoms. Israel's glory filled the temple only after the battles were won ().
  • The Pioneer: Jesus' whole life was a fight for glory, and He entered glory in resurrection (). Perfected, that is, qualified, through sufferings, He became our Captain.
  • Kiss the Sufferings: We are not kidnapped into glory; we are saved into it through suffering (). Every trial is a measured helper on the way.

What Is Glory?

Many think of glory as a luxurious shining state in the future. That may not be wrong, but it is too objective. The Bible's glory is the blossoming of God's divine element from within us.

🌱

The Seed of Glory

At regeneration God's divine element, all that He is, was sown into us. This is Christ in us, the hope of glory ().

🌸

The Blossom

When Christ appears, we appear with Him in glory (). Like the transfiguration, the glory shines out from within, not down from without ().

The Glory Was Already There

Israel had God's glory in the pillar of cloud and fire from the Red Sea on. It filled the temple only after they had fought through the land and built the house (). Glory waits for growth.

God's Glory Business

God runs a great corporation, and its product is not money but glory: leading many sons into glory ().

Perfected Through Sufferings

Jesus was always perfect in virtue. "Perfected" in means carried to completion, that is, qualified. Before incarnation He had no experience of human suffering; having passed through it all, He is fit to lead.

Cut the Way

Pioneer

His life on earth was a fighting story, the seed of glory breaking out. He entered glory in resurrection ().

Took the Lead

Leader

He crossed the river first, the real Joshua, and is now on the other side in the land of glory.

Brings Us In

Captain

He leads His followers along the paved way. We need only look to Him and follow His steps.

The writer mentions suffering because the Hebrew believers were suffering (). Their sufferings were the very process carrying them into glory.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is glory better understood as the blossoming of an inward seed than as an outward shining?Consider Colossians 1:27, John 17:22, and the transfiguration.

Already Given, Not Yet Entered: The Lord has already given us the glory the Father gave Him (), yet we must still enter it. That is only possible if glory is a seed within that must grow.

Shining from Within: On the mountain, glory did not descend on Jesus; it shone out of Him (). Likewise, Christ in us is the hope of glory (). A merely outward glory would be one more vanity.

How could Jesus, who was always perfect, need to be "made perfect through sufferings"?Think of perfection in virtue versus qualification for an office.

Qualified, Not Corrected: The word means to carry to completion. It implies no flaw in His virtue, only the completing of His experience of human suffering.

Fit to Lead: A captain must have walked the road himself. Having passed through every human suffering and entered glory first (), He can lead us through the same.

How does seeing glory as a fight change the way we face the "soil" of daily life?Recall the carnation that fights even the soil that feeds it.

Helpful Frustrations: The soil both helps the plant grow and frustrates it. Our family, work, and church life are the same: needed for growth, yet pressing on us.

Fighting to Blossom: The divine life must break through our shell and press through our environment. This fight is not a sign of failure but the normal process by which the seed of glory develops.

What does it mean practically to "kiss the sufferings"?Read Romans 8:18 and look back on a past trial.

Measured Helpers: Looking back, our problems were neither too long nor too short and came at the right time. They are helpers in the process of getting into glory.

Following the Captain: Instead of being troubled, say, "Praise the Lord, this is the way into glory." Present sufferings are not worth comparing with the glory to be revealed in us (), and our Captain has already gone ahead.
