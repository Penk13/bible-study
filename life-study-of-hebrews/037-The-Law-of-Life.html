Hebrews · Message 37

The Law of Life

As the indestructible life is the focus of Hebrews 7, the law of life is the focus of Hebrews 8. It is the spontaneous function of the divine life within us, spreading from our spirit into every inward part to transform us into Christ's image.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Focus of Hebrews 8: Only life can be imparted into our inward being, so the laws put into our mind must be the law of life.
  • Things Are History: Satan and sin were terminated on the cross; we were regenerated when Christ rose ().
  • Definition: The law of life is the innate, automatic function of the divine life; the higher the life, the higher its law.
  • Function: It kills the old element of Adam and supplies the new element of Christ, a metabolism that transforms us.
  • Intercession: Christ on the throne intercedes so the life He sowed in us develops into mind, emotion, and will.

Origin and Nature

Life is God Himself, expressed in the Son and realized as the Spirit (; ). Through regeneration by the Spirit of life (), this life entered us, and with it came its law. No one teaches a peach tree to bear peaches; bearing peaches is the law of its life.

Location, Function, Power, Issue

Jeremiah speaks of one law; Hebrews speaks of laws. The one law in our spirit becomes many laws in function as it spreads into the parts of our heart: conscience, mind, emotion, and will.

AspectThe Law of Life
LocationOne law in the spirit, many laws in the inward parts ()

PowerUnlike the letter, which perfected nothing (), it is of an indestructible life

The Subjectively Objective Christ

On the cross Christ put away sin; in resurrection He imparted Himself as life into all God's chosen ones; now He sits on the throne interceding (). Many of our prayers are quotations of His intercession, and many inner touchings are reactions to it.

In Heaven

Interceding

For the seed He has sown to grow, function, and mature.

In Our Spirit

Growing as Life

Renewing the mind, saturating the emotion, and remaking the will with His own.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How would you preach the gospel as a testament?The highest gospel.

Read the Will: Sins are already put away and regeneration already accomplished; the bequests belong to the hearer.

Receive and Thank: The more new believers thank the Lord for the bequests, the more anointing and riches of life they enjoy.

Where does the renewing of the mind really come from?Teaching or life?

Not from Without: Messages help, but renewal comes from one of the laws of the divine life working within.

Growth into the Mind: When life spreads from the spirit into the mind, our whole way of thinking is revolutionized.

Is a submissive will better than a stubborn one?Natural versus Christ.

Both Natural: Stubborn, submissive, or neutral, any natural will is useless for the life of Christ.

Remade: The law of life saturates our will with Christ's will until His becomes ours.

How do God's desire and the law of life relate?God and Father; people and children.

More Than Creator: He wants to be our Father, not merely our God by outward letters.

More Than Creatures: He wants children who live by His life; only life satisfies Him.
