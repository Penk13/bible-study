Hebrews · Message 46

The Kingdom Reward and the Gaining of the Soul

The keys of the fourth warning are the "worse punishment" and the "great reward." Salvation is settled; what remains open is whether we gain our soul and the kingdom reward, which is the goal of God's salvation itself.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Words: Unbelievers face salvation or perdition; believers face reward or punishment.
  • Saved, Yet Suffering Loss: A saved one may lose the reward and be saved "as through fire" ().
  • Kingdom Reward: By righteousness and works, decided at Christ's judgment seat, enjoyed in the coming kingdom.
  • Gaining the Soul: Losing the soul's enjoyment for the Lord now means gaining it at His return ().

Salvation and Reward

Even Paul was not sure of the reward until the end of his race (; ). The reward is something added to salvation, not salvation itself.

The Gaining of the Soul

We are of three parts. The spirit was regenerated at salvation and the body awaits redemption, but the soul's saving depends on how we follow the Lord now. Moses, choosing the reproach of Christ, looked away to the reward ().

This Age

Losing the Soul

Suffering for the Lord and the gospel: forgoing comfort, rank, and worldly pleasures.

Coming Age

Gaining the Soul

Entering the Lord's joy and reigning with Him; the whole being fully satisfied.

Those who keep their soul's enjoyment today will lose it then: not perishing, but set aside from the joy and reign of the kingdom.

The Goal Made a Reward

The goal of salvation is not heaven but conformity to the image of the Firstborn (). Foreseeing that many would not cooperate, God made this goal a reward for those who do. Israel was meant to be a kingdom of priests, but after the golden calf the priesthood became the reward of the tribe of Levi alone.

Punishment Does Not Excuse

Discipline does not cancel the lessons. A child punished for failing still has to finish school; every believer must eventually reach God's goal, the New Jerusalem.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is it logical that God both rewards and punishes His children?A wise Father.

Fair and Wise: A Father who rewarded the faithful but ignored the unfaithful would not be just.

Two Destinies: Since salvation is secure, our future rests on reward or punishment, which is why the warning is serious.

How is "gaining the soul" different from being saved from hell?1 Cor. 5:5.

Spirit Saved: Even a brother handed over for discipline has his spirit saved; that salvation is eternal.

Soul Conditional: The soul is gained at the Lord's coming only if we lose it for Him now.

What does losing your soul look like in daily life?A human way of suffering.

Ordinary Costs: A lower grade or a missed promotion for being a straight follower of Christ.

Reward: These losses are the condition for full enjoyment and reigning with Him in the next age.

Why did God make the goal of salvation a reward?Israel and Levi.

An Incentive: Because many would not cooperate, reaching the goal early becomes a prize for those who do.

Enjoy Christ Now: Those who enjoy Christ daily today will reign with Him; those who neglect it lose both now and then.
