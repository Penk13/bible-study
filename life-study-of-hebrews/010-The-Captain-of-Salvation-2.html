Hebrews · Message 10

The Captain of Salvation (2)

Glory is God expressed. God's eternal goal is a corporate people shining with Him, consummating in the New Jerusalem. The Captain who crossed every river into glory has come into us as the seed of glory, and from within the veil He ministers grace until He is glorified in His saints.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Glory Is God Expressed: The unseen God is God; the seen God is glory. The cloud and fire () and the Word made flesh () were God seen.
  • The Corporate Expression: The New Jerusalem has the glory of God (). God as light shines in the Lamb as the lamp () and through a jasper wall that bears His appearance ().
  • Cross Every River: Euphrates, Red Sea, Jordan, and the laver daily (). The goal of crossing was the temple filled with glory.
  • Christ in Us, the Hope of Glory: The Pioneer entered within the veil, yet He came into us, bringing the glory with Him (). He is there and here, like electricity in the plant and in the room.
  • Lightness vs. Weight: Present affliction is light; glory is the eternal weight (). Christ will come from within to be glorified in His saints ().

Glory: God Expressed Corporately

When God comes into us He is life; when He works in us He is light; when He is expressed through us He is glory. His eternal purpose is a corporate man that contains Him and expresses Him.

Crossing the River into Glory

Fallen man became Chaldea and Babylon. God called Abraham to cross out of it, and the goal of that call was the temple, God's habitation filled with glory (). The stone temple symbolized the people; God's glory filling it meant Israel was brought into glory.

The Captain Within the Veil and Within Us

Jesus was the seed that fell into the ground, died, and grew (). Crossing the river of death, He entered within the veil (), into God's full expression. There is a Man in the glory.

There

Within the Veil

The Pioneer and Forerunner, the effulgence of God's glory (), ministering as High Priest: bread, wine, and sufficient grace ().

Here

Within Us

He did not leave the glory to come in; He brought it with Him. Christ in us is the hope of glory, the seed waiting to blossom ().

Put into Glory

Coming from without, like adding color to a pale face. Easy, and He could do it at any time.

🌅

Glorified

Coming from within, when glory has saturated our whole being (; ). This takes growth.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

If glory is "God expressed," what does it mean for us to be brought into glory?Look at the light, lamp, and jasper wall in Revelation 21.

Not a Place of Brightness: The New Jerusalem is a sign, not golden streets to walk on. Its glory is God as light shining through the Lamb and out through a wall that bears His appearance (, ).

Becoming His Expression: To enter glory is to become part of that corporate expression: God shining from within His redeemed people.

Why is the laver, not only the Red Sea and the Jordan, essential to entering glory?How often did the priests wash, and why?

Every Time: The priests washed whenever they approached God (). One washing was never enough, because they were dirtied again.

Daily River Crossing: Major crossings deal with the world and the self; daily washings deal with whatever does not match God's glory. We wash until we reach the sea of glass, where no dust remains.

How can Christ be within the veil and within us at the same time?Consider the illustration of electricity.

Plant and Room: Electricity in a room has not left the power plant. Christ came into us without leaving the glory; He brought the glory into us.

Ministering from There to Here: As High Priest He supplies us from within the veil with grace sufficient for every thorn (), and within us that supply produces glory.

Why does Paul call our affliction "light" and glory a "weight"?Notice the three contrasts in 2 Corinthians 4:17.

Three Contrasts: Affliction against glory, lightness against weight, and the present against the eternal (). What feels heavy to us is, in God's view, light.

Glory Now: We need not wait for eternity. Saints who pass through sufferings already show God's expression. Each affliction works more of the inward glory out, toward the day He is glorified in His saints ().
