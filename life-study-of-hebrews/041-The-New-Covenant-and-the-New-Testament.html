Hebrews · Message 41

The New Covenant and the New Testament

A covenant becomes a testament when its maker dies. Christ's death turned God's promises into accomplished facts and left them to us as bequests in a will, which He now executes in resurrection.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • One Word, Two Meanings: The Greek word is the same; while the maker lives it is a covenant, after he dies it is a testament, a will.
  • A Better Covenant: Enacted on better promises, with the inward law of life as the focus ().
  • Better Sacrifices, Speaking Blood: Christ's blood purified even the heavenly things and speaks to God for us ().
  • Four Stages: God's word, God's promise, the new covenant, and the new testament.
  • Heirs, Not Beggars: Every blessing is a bequest; we receive it with thanks rather than begging ().

From Word to Will

A promise is God's word to give or do something. Sealed by His oath (), it became an unchangeable covenant. Christ's death made every item an accomplished fact; when He left the covenant with us, it became a testament ().

The Covenant Made Better

Everything in the old covenant was a shadow; the new is the reality, with Jesus as its Surety ().

Old Covenant

Letters and Shadows

An outward law, animal blood that could not take away sins, and a mortal high priest.

New Covenant

Life and Reality

The inward law of life, Christ's once-for-all sacrifice, and the eternal Son as High Priest with a more excellent ministry.

Receiving the Bequests

Forgiveness, rebirth, our co-crucifixion, God's selection (), and even glorification () are all itemized in the will. Even Revelation speaks in the past tense of the New Jerusalem (). The resurrected Christ, resting on the throne, intercedes so that the heirs will take what is theirs.

The Proper Christian Life

An heir does not beg for a house left to him; he says thank you and takes it.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the difference between a covenant and a testament?Living or dead.

Covenant: An agreement with promises to accomplish things, made by one still living.

Testament: A will with accomplished things bequeathed, in force only after the maker's death.

Why can prayer turn from begging to thanksgiving?Eph. 5:20.

Already Accomplished: The Father promised, the Son accomplished, and the will itemized everything for us.

Taking with Thanks: Whether we need peace or anything else, we can thank Him and receive it as ours.

How would seeing the will change your preaching of the gospel?Not "if," but "it is yours."

Not Future Promises: Instead of "God will forgive you if you believe," we can present a will already in force.

Accept and Thank: Forgiveness, rebirth, justification, and glorification are offered to be received with thanks.

What is Christ doing about the will today?The Executor.

Executing It: As Mediator in the heavens He restfully intercedes for the heirs.

Within Us: His intercession motivates the law of life so that every bequest becomes real in our being.
