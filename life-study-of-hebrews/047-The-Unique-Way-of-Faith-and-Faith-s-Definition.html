Hebrews · Message 47

The Unique Way of Faith and Faith's Definition

After ten chapters showing which way is closed and which is open, Hebrews tells us how to take the open way: by faith alone. Faith is not a substance we produce but a divine, substantiating sense infused into us.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Signs: Over Judaism hangs "Closed"; over the new way, "Open, freshly slain." Faith is how we drive on the open road.
  • One Thing, Three Names: Perfection, glorification, and the gaining of the soul all point to the same goal.
  • The Unique Way: Receiving the gospel, taking God, entering the rest, inheriting the promises, and gaining the soul all come by faith.
  • Substantiating: Faith is an action, not a solid thing: a sixth sense that makes things hoped for and not seen real to us ().

Ruined Material or Gained Soul

Washed, regenerated, and indwelt, we are good material in which the law of life can work Christ into us. Losing the soul's enjoyments today gives that law room to work; shrinking back ruins the material and sells our birthright of glory.

Faith, the Unique Way

Before chapter eleven defines faith, the book has already shown it to be the only way to receive everything God offers. Without faith, God and His economy are nothing to us; with it, there is great wealth before us.

The opposite is the evil heart of unbelief (). Nothing insults God more than not believing Him; nothing honors Him more than believing.

Faith's Definition

The Greek word in 11:1 is the same used for "substance" in 1:3 and "assurance" in 3:14, but here it denotes an act. Touching paper does not make the paper; it substantiates it to you. Likewise faith makes real to us eternal life and the indwelling Christ, things no one can show to others ().

Things Hoped For

Substantiating

Our life is one of hope: Christ in us, the hope of glory (). Faith gives that hope its ground.

Things Not Seen

Conviction

The evidence and proof of what is unseen and eternal (; ).

Not Natural, but Infused

Faith is not part of our natural makeup. It is God Himself infused into us as the ability to believe; the more we contact Him, the more this sense grows.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does the writer turn to faith right after the comparison of Judaism with God's economy?The road signs.

A Clear Contrast: Ten chapters show the old way closed and the new way open.

How to Take It: Seeing the open way is not enough; it is entered and walked only by faith, not by sight.

What is the difference between faith as a "substance" and faith as "substantiating"?A sixth sense.

Not an Object: Faith is not something we hold, but an ability that realizes what is real yet unseen.

A Sense: As the nose substantiates fragrance and the eyes color, faith substantiates the things of God.

Why is an unbelieving heart called evil?Heb. 3:12.

An Insult: Not believing God's word treats Him as untrustworthy; it dishonors Him more than anything else.

A Closed Door: Without faith, all the riches of God's economy become nothing to us.

How can you have more faith today?Faith is infused.

Not by Effort: Trying to believe harder does not produce faith; it is a divine element, not a natural one.

By Contact: Contacting God and His word lets Him infuse more of Himself, and believing follows spontaneously.
