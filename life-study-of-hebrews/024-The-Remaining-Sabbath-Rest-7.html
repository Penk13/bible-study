Hebrews · Message 24

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (7)

The five warnings in Hebrews are not about losing salvation but about reward: a prize or a punishment. To forsake the church meetings is to sin willfully; to be disciplined is the mark of a son; to sell the birthright is to lose the kingdom, not eternal life. The proper church life is today's Sabbath, preparing us for the Sabbath of the kingdom.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Prize or Punishment: Neither depends on our salvation, but on how we live and work after being saved ().
  • Sinning Willfully: In context, it is forsaking the assembling of the church and going back to Judaism's sacrifices, which no longer exist in God's economy ().
  • Discipline Proves Sonship: The Father scourges His own sons, not strangers; discipline makes us more secure, not less ().
  • The Birthright: Esau sold it for one meal. To lose the birthright is to lose the kingdom as a prize, not eternal life ().
  • Today's Sabbath: The proper church life is the present rest, where we mature for the kingdom Sabbath ().

Five Warnings, One Nature

Hebrews has five warnings, matching the seven in Revelation 2 and 3. Arminians read them as losing salvation, but properly they concern reward: each warning carries a promise for those who heed it.

Willful Sin and the Father's Discipline

The Hebrew believers stood between the church and Judaism. To return to the animal sacrifices would trample the Son of God, count His blood common, and insult the Spirit of grace (). The judgment that follows is on "His people," not on unbelievers.

The Warning

No Sacrifice Remains

Not that sins cannot be forgiven, but that the types are over, replaced by Christ. Going back to them is in vain.

The Discipline

Dealt with as Sons

"Whom the Lord loves He chastens" (), for our profit, that we may partake of His holiness and grow ().

Now or Later

The Father deals with us in this age or in the next. Resurrection does not cancel discipline. Better to behave by His grace; but if punishment must come, better now than in the coming age.

The Church as Today's Sabbath

Since we are receiving an unshakable kingdom, we must hold to grace (), for our God is a consuming fire (). Christ sits in the heavens at rest, having produced the church; outside it we wander in the wilderness.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does it mean to "sin willfully" in Hebrews 10:26?Read verse 25 first.

The Context: The "for" links it to forsaking the assembling (). For the wavering Hebrews, leaving the meetings meant leaving the church for Judaism.

No Sacrifice: It does not mean sins cannot be forgiven; it means the old offerings have been fulfilled and replaced by Christ.

Why does discipline make us more secure, not less?Children on the street are not scourged.

Sons, Not Bastards: A father disciplines his own children. Being without discipline would be the real worry ().

Its Goal: Discipline is for growth, to partake of His holy nature, not to destroy.

What "morsel" might you trade your birthright for?Esau's one meal.

The Birthright: It includes the kingship in the coming kingdom. Losing it is losing the right of life, the prize, not eternal life.

Today: A little enjoyment of material things or a morsel of religion can cost us the enjoyment of the kingdom. Hold fast to grace.

How is the church life today's Sabbath rest?Christ sits; we come home.

Rest: Christ has accomplished everything to produce the church, God's satisfaction. In the church we stop wandering and rest with Him.

Maturity: The kingdom needs matured people. The church is the farm where the seed of life grows into harvest, ushering us into the next Sabbath.
