Life-Study / Hebrews 036

Hebrews · Message 36

The New Covenant

To carry out His purpose of dispensing Himself into man, God put away sin and imparted His life with its law. These four items are no longer promises but accomplished facts, bequeathed to us in a testament to be taken and enjoyed.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • God's Purpose: To work Himself into us as life until divinity and humanity are mingled and we become His expression.
  • Picture, Then Reality: The old covenant was a photograph; when the real person comes, the photograph is replaced.
  • Promise → Covenant → Testament: Promises confirmed by oath became a covenant of facts; through the Testator's death the facts became bequests.
  • Four Contents: The law of life, having God and being His people, the inward ability to know Him, and propitiation with forgiveness.
  • Forgiving Is Forgetting: God remembers our sins no more, and this gives Him the ground to impart His life ().

Old and Decrepit

The old covenant was weak and unprofitable because it was only a shadow; it perfected nothing (). By speaking of a new covenant through Jeremiah, God declared the first one old, decrepit, and near to disappearing. To cling to it now is like talking to a photograph and expecting it to answer.

Old Covenant

A Photograph

Covering of sin, the law of letters, outward teaching; form without life.

New Covenant

The Real Person

Forgiveness of sin, the law of life, inward knowing; constituted with the indestructible life.

Facts Bequeathed to Us

A promise is God's word; an oath confirms it; a confirmed promise is a covenant; and when the One who made it dies, it becomes a testament. So we should not beg God to keep His promise about our temper. Sin has been put away; we simply declare the bequest and enjoy it.

The Four Contents

The primary blessing is life: one law of life () imparted into our spirit spreads into mind, emotion, and will as many laws, and is inscribed on our heart through experience. Propitiation and forgiveness come last in the list, yet they are the ground for all the rest; that is why verse 12 begins with "for."

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did God give an old covenant at all?The kindergarten picture.

A Teaching Picture: The realities of the new covenant are mysterious; a picture first impressed people with what was coming.

Now Replaced: With the reality here, treasuring the shadow is foolish.

When an old sin comes back, how should you pray?Promise versus bequest.

Not Begging: Asking God to keep His promise treats a bequest as if it were still unfulfilled.

Declaring: Sin has been put away; we stand on the accomplished fact and enjoy it by faith.

How do you know what pleases God where the Bible has no rule?Getting up, haircuts, shopping.

The Inner Sense: The law of life within tells us; to ignore it is to lose His presence.

Two Kinds of Knowing: Objective knowledge can be taught; the subjective consciousness of God comes only from life ().

Why is forgiveness the ground rather than the goal?The word "for" in verse 12.

The Goal Is Life: God's original intention was to impart His life into man; sin came in to frustrate it.

The Ground Cleared: Propitiation satisfied His righteousness, so He is free to dispense Himself into us.
