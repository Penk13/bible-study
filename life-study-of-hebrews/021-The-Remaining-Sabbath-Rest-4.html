Hebrews · Message 21

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (4)

There are three real Sabbaths: the church, the millennial kingdom, and the New Jerusalem. The last is all grace and every child of God will be there; the second is a prize. The matter of prize or loss is the bridge between Calvinism and Arminianism.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Church Is the Kingdom Today: The church as God's expression is the kingdom of God now (). We are still receiving an unshakable kingdom ().
  • The Ultimate Sabbath: The New Jerusalem, a city built of transformed people, God's complete expression in eternity (). It is wholly of grace, not reward.
  • Enjoyment, Then Prize: The present church is the Sabbath of enjoyment; the coming kingdom is the Sabbath of prize. Enjoy Christ well in the Father's house and receive a prize in His kingdom.
  • Two Schools: Calvinism rightly says the saved never perish but wrongly says there are no problems. Arminianism misreads God's chastisement as perdition.
  • Saved as Through Fire: Wood, hay, and stubble are burned and the builder suffers loss, "but he himself shall be saved" ().

Three Real Sabbaths

The first Sabbaths, after Adam and after the temple, were figures. The real Sabbaths come whenever God is truly expressed and represented by man, and each is fuller than the one before.

The Bridge Between Two Schools

Neither school can reconcile God's warnings with His security, because neither sees the matter of prize and punishment in the kingdom.

Calvinist

Once Saved, No Problems

Right that no one can snatch us out of His hand (). Wrong to say a born child has no problems; it calls Hebrews 6's tasters "false Christians."

Arminian

Saved, Then Lost Again

Takes those who tasted the heavenly gift () and the burned land as perdition, misusing God's chastisement of His children.

The Pure Word

Prize or Loss

The thorns are burned but the land remains (). Eternal life keeps us; God's righteousness still deals with our sins.

Building Materials and the Day of Fire

The believers are God's farm and building (). The day will declare each man's work ().

Not Purgatory

The Catholic teaching lets money shorten suffering after death. This is neither that, nor Calvinism, nor Arminianism, but the plain Word.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the New Jerusalem certain for every believer, while the millennial kingdom is not?Grace versus reward; eternal portion versus prize.

All Grace: The New Jerusalem is the Father's eternal portion for all His children. God's choice is irrevocable, and nothing, not even Satan, can stop Him from maturing us.

A Prize: The harvest Sabbath rewards those who enjoyed Christ, grew, and overcame in this age. Missing it is not perdition, but it is a real loss.

How does the matter of prize or punishment reconcile Calvinism and Arminianism?Hold John 10:28 and Hebrews 6:7–8 together.

Security: Two mighty hands uphold us; eternal life keeps us, and we shall never perish ().

Accountability: God is righteous, so His children's sins are chastised, today or in the coming age. What is not of God is burned, but the land, the believer, remains ().

What are you building with today: gold, silver, and precious stones, or wood, hay, and stubble?The Triune God's nature and work versus the flesh, the self, and the world.

Proper Materials: Gold is the Father's divine nature, silver the Son's redemption, precious stones the Spirit's transforming work. Such work abides and receives a reward ().

A Sober Warning: Whatever this fire is, no one should want to be "saved as through fire" (). Being saved does not mean everything is all right.

Why is it foolish not to enjoy Christ well in the church today?Think of a father who prepares a feast and urges his children to eat.

Free Enjoyment: The feast costs nothing, and the more we eat, the happier the Father is. The church life is the means to the harvest Sabbath.

The Goal: Press on toward the prize of the high calling (). Neglecting today's Sabbath means missing the next one.
