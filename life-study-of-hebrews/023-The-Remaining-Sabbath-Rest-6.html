Hebrews · Message 23

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (6)

Judgment begins at God's house. Eternal salvation is by faith, but the coming Sabbath rest in the kingdom is a reward by works: a watchful life like the wise virgins and a faithful work like the good servants. Growth decides when we mature; the use of our gift decides where we reign.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Judgment at the House: To be just in judging unbelievers, God first judges His own children ().
  • Growth, Not Mere Cleanness: God wants a people saturated with Him, not merely sinless. Forgiveness is minor; deliverance from the frustration of sin, the world, and the self to grow in life is major.
  • Life and Work: The ten virgins show the life we need; the talents show the work we need. Both are required for the coming Sabbath rest.
  • Grace for Responsibility: "Be saved" (), like a child told to open its mouth and be fed. Grace comes as we exercise our gift.
  • Discipline, Not Perdition: Weeping and gnashing of teeth () is the Father's wise discipline in the kingdom age, not eternal loss.

Two Parables in Matthew 25

Of the two living ones, one is taken (); of the ten dead ones, five are accepted and five temporarily rejected. The saved are never rejected eternally, but may be rejected dispensationally, like a child left out of a prize dinner.

Our Life

The Wise Virgins

Watchful, bearing testimony, going out to meet the Lord, with oil in the lamp and an extra portion in the vessel ().

Our Work

Talents, Gifts, and Kingship

Talents are given according to ability, but natural ability must pass through the death of Christ into resurrection to match the talent. The danger lies not with the five-talent servant but with the one-talent servant who excuses himself. The talent in Matthew is the gift in the Epistles ().

Growth in Life

Decides the Time

Transformed by the extra oil, we mature earlier and are ready for the early rapture.

Exercise of the Gift

Decides the Position

"Ruler over many things" (); "authority over ten cities" (); "authority over the nations" ().

The Joy of Your Lord

Entering into the Lord's joy is entering the Sabbath rest with Him in the millennial kingdom. When He sees the overcomers, He is satisfied and rests, and they rest with Him.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is having our sins forgiven a minor thing?Clean people versus saturated people.

God's Intention: Not a universe of neat, sinless people, but a people permeated with Him and built up in Him.

Why Deal with Sin: Sin, the world, and the self are Satan's frustration to growth. If we desire to grow, God takes care of our sins (); if we only want forgiveness, we are not in the fulfillment of His purpose.

Why can't the one-talent servant be a false Christian?Calvinism's escape.

An Escape: Teachers who cannot reconcile outer darkness with eternal security call him false. But he was the Lord's servant, given the Lord's talent.

The Full Revelation: The Word shows both eternal salvation by faith and dispensational reward by works, with prize or punishment.

How do grace and responsibility fit together?"Be saved," not "save yourselves."

Grace for Responsibility: The Lord saves, but we must be willing, open, and cooperating ().

In Work: The profit comes from grace, but grace comes as we use the talent. Without exercise, the grace does not come.

Are you excusing yourself as a one-talent believer?Five, two, or one: the principle is the same.

No Excuse: Whatever we were given, we must gain the same again for the Lord. The one who hid his talent was the one rebuked.

Practice: Stir up the gift () and pay the price for the oil today. Salvation is free; transformation is not.
