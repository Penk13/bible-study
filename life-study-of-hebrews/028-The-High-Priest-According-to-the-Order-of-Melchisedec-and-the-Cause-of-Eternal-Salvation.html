Hebrews · Message 28

The High Priest According to the Order of Melchisedec and the Cause of Eternal Salvation

Christ is not merely a sin-offering priest like Aaron. Glorified in resurrection and ascension, He is a Priest forever according to the order of Melchisedec, coming from God with bread and wine to minister the processed God into us, and so He is the cause of eternal salvation.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Glory, Not Only Honor: Aaron received the honor of a position; Christ was glorified by God in resurrection () and ascension ().
  • Two Orders: Aaron's priesthood was in humanity only and ended at death; Melchisedec's is in humanity and divinity, and forever.
  • Bread and Wine: Melchisedec came from God to Abraham with bread and wine (), as the Lord did at His table ().
  • Beyond Redemption: Redemption deals with the fall; ministering God into us fulfills God's original purpose.
  • The Cause: Christ's Person as this High Priest is the cause of an eternal salvation, not limited by time or place ().

Two Orders of Priesthood

Aaron was called by God, offered gifts and sacrifices for the people and himself (), and served until death. Christ was ordained according to a higher order, constituted by the power of an indestructible life ().

Order of Aaron

Going to God

Offers sacrifices for sin, deals with the negative, in humanity only, for a lifetime.

Order of Melchisedec

Coming from God

Ministers bread and wine, fulfills God's purpose, in humanity and divinity, forever.

Melchisedec and the Called Race

The created race under Adam ended in the idolatry of Chaldea, so God called Abraham to head a new race. The first priesthood in the Bible came to this father of the called race. As king of righteousness and king of Salem (peace), Melchisedec served him on the basis of righteousness and peace. Likewise, justified and at peace with God, we eat and drink at the Lord's table.

🍞

Bread

The life supply: Christ the bread of life (), His body given for us.

🍷

Wine

His redeeming blood (), a liquid of life that quenches the thirst of those under condemnation.

A Daily Breakfast

Confessing failures each morning only knows Christ as Aaron. Coming to the throne of grace to eat and drink Christ as our Melchisedec makes the day a success.

The Cause of Eternal Salvation

Aaron's atonement only covered sin; Christ's eternal redemption put it away (). Eternal salvation also saves us positively into His perfection and glory, and it carries the divine element and nature. Christ as the Captain leads us; Christ as Melchisedec is the cause of this salvation, so it holds at home, in our city, and in any church situation. We experience Him in His sufferings, through which He was perfected.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

If we had never fallen, which priesthood would we still need?God's original purpose.

Aaron for the Fall: Sacrifices for sin rescue and reconcile; they deal with the negative situation.

Melchisedec for the Purpose: God's intention was always to work Himself into man. Even without the fall, Christ would minister God into us as bread and wine.

What does the Lord's table declare to the universe?Genesis 14 and Matthew 26.

Our Way of Living: We live by eating the divine bread and drinking the divine wine as our daily supply.

Our Basis: We come not on pity but on righteousness and peace, served by the One who brought both in.

Why is "honor" changed to "glorify" when the writer turns to Christ?Position versus Person.

Honor: A high priest taken from men had only the preciousness of a position.

Glory: Christ has the splendor of His Person, brought into resurrection with His humanity. In humanity He sympathizes; in divinity and resurrection He is able to do all things for us ().

Is there a place where your salvation does not work?Eternal means unlimited.

The Test: Avoiding a difficult home or church suggests a salvation limited by circumstances.

The Cause Is With Us: Christ as our High Priest goes everywhere we go. Enjoying Him, we can return to hard situations and overcome them by His life.
