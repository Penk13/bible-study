Hebrews · Message 20

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (3)

God's rest is on earth with man, and it develops progressively. The church life is the real Sabbath, but it is the growing Sabbath of the seed, not yet the harvest. How we grow today decides whether we share in the coming Sabbath of maturity, which is a prize, not salvation.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Rest on Earth: God's full rest is not with angels in heaven but with redeemed man on earth: "Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven" ().
  • Types, Then Reality: The Sabbath after Adam () and the glory-filled temple () were pictures. The church, the new man, is the first real Sabbath.
  • Seed, Not Harvest: God sowed Himself into man, and the field is growing (). The harvest comes when the Lord returns (). The highest life takes the longest to grow.
  • A Prize for Growth: The Sabbath of maturity in the millennium is a reward for proper growth today. A castaway () loses the prize, not salvation.
  • Paul's Assurance: Unsure in 1 Corinthians, still pressing on in Philippians (), sure only at the end: "There is laid up for me the crown" ().

The Progressive Development of the Sabbath

God does not do everything at once. Even in creation He took six days. Each Sabbath came after He gained a people on earth to express Him and represent Him.

The Growing Sabbath and the Sabbath of Maturity

The church life is a real Sabbath, but not yet complete or mature. In the parable of the sower, the same seed gave different results in four soils ().

🌱

The Growing Sabbath

Today's church life with the riches of Christ. All believers may enter, yet few do ().

🌾

The Sabbath of Maturity

The harvest at the Lord's return; reigning with Christ a thousand years ().

Why Hebrews Was Written

Of the two million who left Egypt, most fell in the wilderness. Many Christians today are still in Egypt or wandering. Hebrews urges the saved not to forsake the church life but to be diligent to enter today's rest.

A Reward for Proper Growth

Salvation is by faith. Reigning with Christ in the coming age is a prize, used by a wise Father to encourage us to run well now.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the church the first real Sabbath, when Adam and the temple came first?What did God do in the church that He never did before?

Pictures: Adam and Israel were types. God rested with them, but He never sowed Himself into them.

Fulfillment: Through incarnation, death, and resurrection God sowed Himself into man, and the church was produced. The church is the real soil, so it is the real Sabbath.

If the church life is a real Sabbath, what is still lacking?Think of the field and the harvest.

Maturity: What we have today is the seed, not the harvest. The field is growing (), but the reaping comes at the Lord's return ().

Time: The highest life takes the longest to grow. We should not expect to mature overnight, nor be careless about ripening.

What does Paul mean by fearing to become a castaway?Read 1 Corinthians 9:24–27 with 10:5.

Not Perdition: "Obtain" refers to the prize, not salvation, which Paul already had ().

Missing the Prize: All Israel left Egypt, "but" most fell in the wilderness (). A castaway misses the coming Sabbath in the millennium.

How is the coming Sabbath a sign of the Father's wisdom and love?Recall the father with five children and a final exam.

Encouragement: A wise father rewards children who do well and disciplines those who fail. God uses the coming Sabbath to encourage us to enjoy and grow in the present one.

Our Response: Like Paul, run without sloppiness, forget what is behind, and press on (), so that at the end we may say a crown is laid up ().
