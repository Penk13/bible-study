Hebrews · Message 33

A Divine High Priest, Living and Able to Save

Christ is kingly in status and divine in constitution. Made a High Priest by the power of an indestructible life, He is the absence of death and the presence of life, and so He is able to save us to the uttermost.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Aspects: Hebrews 7 opens with the King of righteousness and peace () and closes with the Son perfected forever (): kingly and divine.
  • Absence of Death: As light means no darkness, His divine priesthood means no death and the presence of life.
  • A Threefold Transfer: From Aaron to Melchisedec, from Levi to Judah, and from mortal men to the Son of God, with a change of law as well ().
  • The Surety: Christ has pledged Himself, like a limb to the body, to fulfill the better covenant ().
  • To the Uttermost: Because He always lives and intercedes, He saves completely in extent, time, and space ().

Kingly Status, Divine Constituent

Kingship is His standing; it keeps an order of righteousness and peace in which He can minister the processed God to us. Divinity is His very element, as wood is the element of a table. His kingly priesthood is for ministering; His divine priesthood is for saving.

Kingly

A Matter of Status

From Judah, the royal tribe; righteousness and peace for the enjoyment of bread and wine.

Divine

A Matter of Nature

The Son of God, full of life; wherever He is, death is swallowed up.

The Only Begotten and the Firstborn Son

As the Only Begotten, the Son was eternally perfect but had only divinity. To be our High Priest He also had to become the Firstborn Son, born in resurrection (). This is why He is said to be perfected forever.

Living and Able to Save

The Levitical priests could not save even themselves; death kept replacing them. Christ continues forever with an unchangeable priesthood (), the same yesterday, today, and forever (). The law perfected nothing; the transfer of law brings in a better hope, the priesthood in life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does it mean that Christ's priesthood is the absence of death?Light and darkness.

His Element: His priesthood is composed of divine life, so where He ministers, death cannot remain.

Our Experience: Touching Him is not merely being forgiven; it is life swallowing up what is deadening in us.

Why did the Son need to be perfected if He was already eternally perfect?Only Begotten and Firstborn.

Humanity Needed: As the Only Begotten He had no humanity. A High Priest for men must be a man.

Through a Process: Incarnation, human living, death, and resurrection made Him the Firstborn Son, fully qualified for us.

How does Christ being the Surety give you confidence?A pledged limb.

Bound to Us: He has pledged Himself to the covenant and to us; it is too late for Him to withdraw.

Unlimited: Unlike a friend with few dollars, He has unlimited resources, so the covenant cannot fail.

Do you need to feel His intercession for it to work?Rest and trust.

Not by Realizing: His intercession does not depend on our awareness; we simply rest in it and enjoy it.

Sooner or Later: He never forgets those who called on His name; His interceding will subdue and save us to the uttermost.
