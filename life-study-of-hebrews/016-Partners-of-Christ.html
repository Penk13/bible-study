Hebrews · Message 16

Partners of Christ

The same Greek word means both partaker and partner. When we enjoy Christ we are His partakers; when we follow Him we are His partners. Christ, appointed and anointed to carry out God's plan, is the real Joshua taking the good land, and we are His Calebs sharing His anointing and His enterprise.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Church, a Miniature: All that will be in the New Jerusalem () we have in the church today in miniature: a foretaste of glory and rest.
  • Partaker or Partner: One Greek word, two meanings. In , in the context of entering the land, "partners" fits: Caleb did not enjoy Joshua; he followed him ().
  • Appointed and Anointed: Christ was appointed in eternity and anointed in time (). The oil on Aaron's head runs down to his garments (): we share His inauguration.
  • Spirit to Enjoy, Christ to Follow: As our enjoyment He is the Holy Spirit (); as our leader He is Christ the Captain. First partakers, then partners.
  • An Army, Not One Man: God could have brought Joshua in alone, but two million marching made it glorious. Christ's enterprise is to take the land, possess it, and build God's house.

Partakers and Partners

The King James Version renders "partakers of Christ." Since 3:7–14 is about entering the good land, Witness Lee reads it as "partners."

🍽️

Partakers

Like partaking of breakfast: we enjoy Him. Here He is the Holy Spirit, our nourishment even in troubled times ().

🤝

Partners

Like joint-owners of a corporation: we follow Him and share His operation. Here He is Christ, the Captain, and we are His army.

Testimonies of Partnership

Many testify of enjoying Christ; few testify of being His partners. Preaching the high gospel on the campuses is sharing in the operation of the anointed Heir. His office is also our office.

Christ, God's Appointed and Anointed

God's operation in the universe aims at a glorious expression of Himself. The Son was appointed to run this "corporation."

"O God… Your God" (): as God, the Son is addressed as God; as man, God is His God. Witness Lee stresses that God is one (), yet Father, Son, and Spirit, pointing to , , and , and urging us to take the whole Bible rather than tradition.

Today's Joshua and Caleb

What is the good land? Not heaven after death, and not merely the heavenlies full of evil spirits. It is the fulfillment of God's purpose in Christ: a land to take, possess, and build God's house in, a land of rest and glory ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the difference between being Christ's partaker and His partner?Compare enjoying breakfast with owning part of a company.

Enjoying vs. Following: A partaker enjoys Christ as the Spirit (). A partner follows Christ as the Captain and shares His work ().

In That Order: We must first partake, being nourished, so that we can march as partners. Partaking is for partnership.

How do we share in Christ's anointing?Picture Psalm 133:2.

Oil Flowing Down: The oil poured on Aaron's head ran down to the edge of his garments (). The whole body shares the Head's anointing.

Put into Office: Christ was anointed "above His partners" (). His inauguration includes us; we too are anointed to carry out God's operation.

Why did God want an army of joint-heirs instead of Christ entering glory alone?Think of Jericho.

Glory in Numbers: God could have brought Joshua in alone, but it would not have been glorious. An army marching terrified the enemy.

Our Part: The more joint-heirs, the more glory. As Calebs following fully (), we march with the real Joshua into rest ().

Why is the good land neither heaven after death nor simply the heavenlies?What was Joshua's conquest for?

Not Heaven: Canaan had enemies; heaven has none. And if Canaan were the heavenlies full of evil spirits, where would the rest be?

God's Purpose: The land is where God's people take possession and build His house for His expression. Christ's enterprise is this, and we are His partners in it.
