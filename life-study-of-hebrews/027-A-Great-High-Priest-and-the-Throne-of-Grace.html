Life-Study / Hebrews 027

Hebrews · Message 27

A Great High Priest and the Throne of Grace

Christ is now a great High Priest in the Holy of Holies, bearing us before God and ministering God into us. Since the throne of grace is joined to our spirit, we can come forward at any moment to receive mercy and find grace for timely help.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A New Comparison: After God, the angels, and Moses, Hebrews now compares Christ with Aaron (4:14–7:28), with a third warning in the middle.
  • Where He Is: Our High Priest is not at the altar or in the Holy Place but in the Holy of Holies, where God's presence and glory are.
  • Shoulder and Breast: As Aaron bore Israel's names before God, Christ bears us in His strength and love (, ).
  • Heaven Joined to Our Spirit: The Christ interceding in heaven is also in us (; ); our spirit is the gate of heaven.
  • Mercy, Then Grace: Mercy reaches us in our pitiful state and qualifies us; grace then supplies us ().

A Great High Priest

Many know Christ only on the cross, at the altar, or at most in the Holy Place. But His redemptive work is finished; now He is in the Holy of Holies ministering God into us. Paul's thorn was not removed, yet he received sufficient grace (). This enjoyment is the third stage of salvation.

Person

God and Man

The Son of God and the Son of Man, with both the divine and human natures.

Work

All Accomplished

Purified sins, tasted death, destroyed the Devil, cares for God's house, and brings us into rest.

Attainment

Crowned in the Heavens

Having passed through the heavens, He stripped off the principalities and powers () and sits at God's right hand.

Having been tried in all respects yet without sin, He is able to help those being tried () and quickly feels with us in our weaknesses.

The Throne of Grace

To the universe, God's throne is the throne of authority. To believers it is the throne of grace, typified by the propitiation cover sprinkled with blood (). In the end it is the throne of God and of the Lamb, from which the river of water of life flows with the tree of life (). This flowing supply is grace.

🏛️

In the Heavens

Christ sits on the throne at God's right hand.

🪜

In Our Spirit

God's habitation is in spirit (); there Christ is the ladder at Bethel, the gate of heaven (; ).

Like Electricity

The current joins the power plant to our home. So Christ joins the third heaven to our spirit (). No distance remains; calling "O Lord Jesus" from deep within brings us to the throne.

Receiving Mercy and Finding Grace

Both mercy and grace express God's love, but mercy reaches farther. The prodigal son received compassion first, then the best robe and the fattened calf (). We come as beggars, are qualified by mercy, then sit down to enjoy grace. The proper Christian life abides before the throne and continually receives timely help.

Mercy

Bridging the Gap

Given to us in our unworthy condition; it brings us to where God can favor us.

Grace

Timely Supply

God in the Lamb flowing out for our enjoyment, always fresh and fitting our need.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Where is the Christ you usually contact: the altar, the Holy Place, or the Holy of Holies?His work is finished.

Not Back to the Cross: Redemption is done; we need not ask Him to repeat it. Many stay with a crucified Christ and miss the ministering High Priest.

In the Holy of Holies: There He bears us on His shoulder and breast and ministers God into us. This is the highest enjoyment.

How can we come to a throne in heaven while still on earth?Bethel.

Joined to Our Spirit: The same Christ is on the throne and in our spirit, where God's habitation is. Our spirit is today's Bethel, the gate of heaven.

Divided from the Soul: A wandering mind keeps us far off. The living word separates spirit from soul (), and turning to the spirit brings us immediately before the throne.

Why must we often receive mercy before we can find grace?The prodigal son.

Our Condition: We usually come in a pitiful state, unworthy of anything. Mercy reaches us there.

Then Grace: Once mercy has clothed and qualified us, we can sit and enjoy the supply, as the son enjoyed the fattened calf.

What does it mean that our High Priest sympathizes with our weaknesses?Tried, yet without sin.

Fellow-Suffering: Having passed through every kind of trial, He feels what we feel and enters into it with us ().

Able to Help: Because sin never touched Him, He can carry us through trials without entanglement. Instead of fixing our eyes on our troubles, we remember we have such a High Priest.
