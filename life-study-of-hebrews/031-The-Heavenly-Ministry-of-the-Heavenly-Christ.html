Hebrews · Message 31

The Heavenly Ministry of the Heavenly Christ

At 7:1 Hebrews turns from earth to heaven: from Christ's work on the cross, typified by Aaron, to His ministry on the throne according to the order of Melchisedec. The cross gives forgiveness of sin; the throne gives the overcoming of sin.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Turn at 7:1: Chapters 1–6 are the word of foundation; from 7:1 the word of perfection unveils Christ ministering in heaven.
  • Two Floors: Forgiveness of sin is the ground floor; the overcoming of sin is the second floor, where Christ is on the throne ().
  • Cross and Throne: The cross brought us out of Egypt; the throne brings us into Canaan. Do not linger at the altar; stay at the throne of grace ().
  • Grace Defined: Grace is God Himself flowing into, through, and out of us to do everything for us.
  • Eleven and Eleven: Christ's ministry has eleven items on earth and eleven in heaven, His more excellent ministry ().

The Turn

The tabernacle had the outer court, the Holy Place, and the Holy of Holies. The court and the Holy Place exist to bring us into the Holy of Holies. Many remain around the altar, the cross, which is precious but is not the goal. God speaks from the propitiation-cover, the throne of grace, not from the cross. There sin is under our feet and grace is everything.

Two Aspects of His Priesthood

Few see that Christ's priesthood has two aspects. Both are real; the point is to press on from the first into the full enjoyment of the second.

Typified by Aaron

On Earth

Offering Himself for sin on the cross: the forgiveness of sin, out of Egypt.

Order of Melchisedec

In Heaven

Within the veil as the Forerunner (): the overcoming of sin, into Canaan.

The Section on Earth

These are accomplished facts, glad tidings to declare even when feelings and experience say otherwise.

The Section in Heaven

His ministry on the throne is more excellent than that of any Old Testament priest. It not only saves us from negative things but supplies the heavenly life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why have so many sought victory over sin and failed?Wrong floor.

Seeking Upstairs Things Downstairs: Forgiveness belongs to Christ's work on earth; overcoming belongs to His ministry on the throne.

Come Up: Only by enjoying the heavenly Christ according to Melchisedec is sin put under our feet.

Is your Christ today mainly on the cross or on the throne?Where is your fellowship centered?

Not Giving Up the Cross: The altar is dear, for it dealt with sin, but it is in the outer court.

Lingering at the Throne: God's voice, presence, and grace are at the propitiation-cover. That is where we should remain.

How should we respond when feelings say sin and problems with God remain?Facts versus feelings.

Declare the Facts: Sin has been taken away, God has been appeased, and we have been purified, sanctified, and perfected.

Believe Now: These will be manifest in eternity; believing them today lets us enjoy them today.

What is grace, in experience?More than unmerited favor.

God Flowing: Grace is God Himself flowing into, through, and out of us to do everything for us.

Found at the Throne: We receive it by coming forward to the throne of grace moment by moment.
