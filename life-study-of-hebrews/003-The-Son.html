Life-Study of Hebrews · Message 3

The Son

God is no longer a hidden mystery, but an unveiled story because He has spoken in the Son. Behold the Son in His supreme Person—the effulgence of God’s glory and the impress of His substance—and in His all-inclusive work throughout the ages in creation and redemption.

⏱ 8–10 min review 📖 Verses: 🎯 Person & Work · Effulgence & Impress · Seated High Priest

⚡ Rapid Recall (Read this in 60 seconds)

  • God's Story is in the Son: God in Himself is mysterious and unknowable, but through His speaking He has become an open story. The central focus, content, and embodiment of this divine story is the Son ().
  • The Person of the Son ():
    1) The Effulgence of God’s Glory: Like the rays of the sun, Christ is the shining out of God’s unapproachable glory reaching humanity ().
    2) The Impress of God’s Substance: Like a signet seal pressed into wax, Christ is the exact, tangible representation of God’s essential being ().
    3) God Himself & The Lord: The Father Himself addresses the Son as “O God” () and proclaims Him as the eternal Lord who founded the heavens and the earth ().
  • His Twofold Threefold Work:
    • In Creation: Past: Created the universe (; ). Present: Upholds all things by the word of His power (). Future: Appointed Heir of all things ().
    • In Redemption: Past: Made purification of sins by Himself on the cross (). Present: Sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high (; ). Future: Waiting until His enemies are made a footstool for His feet (; ).
  • The Seated Christ: There was no chair in the Old Testament tabernacle—Levitical priests stood daily because animal blood could never take away sins (). But after offering one sacrifice forever, Jesus sat down! Redemption is eternally finished, accepted by God, and entered into divine rest.
Core Revelation 01

The Person of the Son: Expressing the Unseen God

God in Himself is invisible and unapproachable (). How does God reach us and express His very being? unveils Christ through two matchless divine metaphors:

☀️

The Effulgence of His Glory

The Sun & Its Radiant Rays: Just as the rays shining from the sun cannot be separated from the sun itself, the Son cannot be separated from God. When the rays reach our earth, the sun reaches us. Christ is the shining out of God’s glory, bringing the warm, illuminating divine nature directly into our hearts.

📜

The Impress of His Substance

The Seal & Its Exact Impression: When a signet ring or engraved seal is pressed into soft wax or paper, the impression bears the exact shape, image, and details of the seal. Christ is not a vague shadow or symbol; He is the exact visible representation and embodiment of the very substance of God.

Core Revelation 03

The Standing Priests vs. The Seated Son

Under the Old Covenant, the Levitical priests served within a tabernacle that had no chairs. Why did Jesus sit down? This contrast reveals the absolute finality and victory of the New Covenant:

Old Covenant · Levitical Priests
Standing Daily (Unfinished)

“And every priest stands ministering daily and offering repeatedly the same sacrifices, which can never take away sins” ().

  • No chairs in the tabernacle: Standing was required because their sacrificial duty was never finished.
  • Repeated animal blood: Shadows and types that could only cover sins temporarily, never purge them inward.
  • Constant human labor: Continuous striving under the demands of the law with zero divine rest.
New Covenant · Christ the Son
Sat Down Forever (Finished!)

“But this Man, after He had offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down at the right hand of God” (; ).

  • Seated at the Majesty’s right hand: Indicates supreme honor, total divine acceptance, and eternal rest!
  • One sacrifice forever: Purified our sins completely by Himself; nothing can be added or improved.
  • Believers enter His rest: We no longer strive in religious self-effort; we rest in His accomplished redemption.
Review & Reflection

Active Recall: Deep Reflection Questions

Ponder these open-ended questions to test your grasp and personal application of the message, then open each section to review the key insights.

Reflection 01

How do the two metaphors in Hebrews 1:3—the "effulgence of His glory" and the "express image of His substance"—explain the mystery of how God the Father relates to Christ the Son?

Reflect on the sun and its rays, and a signet seal stamping an image into wax. Can the sun ever be separated from its light? Can the impress be separated from the seal?

🔍 Reveal Key Insights & Meditation

The Effulgence of Glory (The Sun & Rays): The Father is the source, like the sun; the Son is the effulgence, like the rays shining forth from the sun. The rays cannot be separated from the sun, and the sun cannot reach us except through its rays. Christ is God shining forth to reach us, enlighten us, and transmit the divine life into us ().

The Impress of Substance (The Seal & Stamp): The substance of God is invisible and intangible, but when a seal is pressed into paper or wax, its exact character and image are visibly manifested. Christ is the exact expression of God’s very essence. When we see, touch, and experience Christ, we are touching the living substance of God Himself ().

Spiritual Takeaway: Christ is not an inferior messenger or created angel; He is the out-shining and exact embodiment of God Himself bringing the divine reality into our spirit.

Reflection 02

In the Old Testament tabernacle there was never a chair, yet Hebrews emphasizes that Christ "sat down." What is the tremendous spiritual significance of Christ being seated?

Contrast Hebrews 10:11 ("every priest stands ministering daily...") with Hebrews 1:3 and 10:12 ("after He had offered one sacrifice for sins forever, sat down..."). What does sitting signify regarding redemption and our spiritual conscience?

🔍 Reveal Key Insights & Meditation

The Standing Priests (Unfinished Work): The Levitical priests could never sit down because animal sacrifices could never take away sins (). Their standing posture was a perpetual testimony that their work was incomplete and unable to satisfy God’s righteousness permanently.

The Seated Christ (Finished Redemption): Christ offered one all-inclusive sacrifice on the cross and then sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high (; ). Sitting signifies that redemption is completely finished, God is fully satisfied, and Christ has entered into eternal rest.

Application to Our Faith: Whenever Satan accuses us or our conscience feels condemned, we must look away to the seated Christ! We do not need to perform religious works to earn God's acceptance. In Christ, our redemption is totally accomplished, and we can rest peacefully in His finished work.

Reflection 03

Why does the writer of Hebrews connect Christ's work in Creation (past: created, present: upholds, future: inherits) with His work in Redemption (past: purified, present: seated, future: subduing)?

Consider Hebrews 1:2–3, Colossians 1:16–17, and Hebrews 1:13. How does this elevate our appreciation of who our Redeemer is?

🔍 Reveal Key Insights & Meditation

The Grandeur of Our Savior: The One who died on Calvary to purify our sins is none other than the Creator and Sustainer of the vast universe (; ). He is not a minor religious leader; the entire cosmos was made through Him, is maintained by Him, and heads up in Him as the universal Heir!

Complete Assurance: If the word of His power is mighty enough to uphold galaxies and planetary orbits, that same power is more than sufficient to uphold your personal Christian life, supply your daily needs, and preserve the church.

The Victorious Goal: Both creation and redemption move toward the same consummation: Christ inheriting all things and all His enemies being made a footstool beneath His feet (; ). We are united to the sovereign, triumphant Lord of all.

Reflection 04

In practical daily life, what does it mean to experience Christ "upholding all things by the word of His power" rather than carrying situations in your natural strength?

Contrast anxious human worrying and self-effort with contacting the living Word in prayer and trusting the sovereign Christ.

🔍 Reveal Key Insights & Meditation

The Word of His Power: Christ does not sustain the universe by frantic muscular exertion or anxiety; He upholds and carries all things simply by the word of His power (). When God speaks, everything falls into place.

Relinquishing Natural Anxiety: We often become exhausted trying to manage family crises, health struggles, or financial fears in our natural strength. When we try to hold up our universe, we inevitably break down.

The Practice of Faith: Turn to the Lord in your spirit. Pray-read His living promises. Hand over every care to the seated Christ, knowing that He is actively carrying you, your family, and your circumstances forward according to His sovereign purpose.
