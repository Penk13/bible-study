Hebrews · Message 48

The History of Faith and Its Witnesses

Hebrews 11 traces faith from creation to the New Jerusalem, proving it the unique path for God's seekers. Yet the witnesses of old wait for us: apart from the believers of the new covenant they cannot be made perfect.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Witnesses Are Martyrs: In Greek the two words are the same; each witness paid a price for the testimony of faith.
  • Sojourners: The patriarchs lived as strangers on earth, waiting for the city God built ().
  • God's Silence: Faith brings not only miracles; often it brings the deeper enjoyment of God's silence in suffering.
  • Waiting for Us: The Old Testament saints need the New Testament believers for their perfection ().

A History of Substantiating

Each witness acted on what was not yet seen. Noah built an ark under a clear sky; Joseph gave orders about his bones for an exodus still to come.

Miracles and Silence

Gideon, David, Samuel, and the prophets did marvelous things, but others were stoned and sawn in two. God did not rescue Stephen; at the cross, too, heaven was silent. Some refused release to gain "a better resurrection," the reward of the overcomers ().

Silence Is Greater

We prefer miracles, but enduring with no outward rescue takes greater faith. Of such people the world is not worthy.

Something Better Concerning Us

"Better" runs through Hebrews: a better hope, covenant, sacrifice, country, resurrection. The old saints had shadows; we are in the reality. The kingdom and the New Jerusalem are corporate, so they cannot enter apart from us.

New Testament

In Christ

Like spectators in a stadium, the foregoing overcomers watch and wait for the full number to be reached. This great cloud of witnesses is also the cloud by which we follow the Lord and enjoy His presence ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Hebrews 11 skip Israel's forty years in the wilderness and even the crossing of the Jordan?Kadesh-barnea.

Unbelief Unrecorded: Those years were spent provoking God; nothing in them was done by faith.

A Needless Delay: With faith at Kadesh-barnea they could have entered decades earlier; the Jordan crossing only resulted from that delay.

How could Moses bear "the reproach of Christ" before Christ came?The Angel of the Lord.

Christ with Israel: As the Angel of the Lord He shared His people's afflictions, so their reproach was His.

Our Pattern: Suffering believers today can likewise count His reproach greater riches than what they lose.

Would you rather have God's miracles or God's silence?Stephen and the cross.

Honest Preference: Most of us want rescue; yet the record of faith includes many who were not rescued.

Inward Enjoyment: God often helps not outwardly but inwardly, letting His saints enjoy His silence by faith.

What responsibility does Hebrews 11:40 lay on believers today?The full number.

They Are Ready: The saints before us wait for the number of overcomers to be completed.

A Golden Opportunity: Living at the consummation, we can fill the gap and share in fulfilling God's eternal purpose.
