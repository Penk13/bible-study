Hebrews · Message 44

Come Forward to the Holy of Holies and Do Not Shrink Back to Judaism

The fourth warning (Heb. 10:19-39) calls us to leave the first tabernacle and come forward into the Holy of Holies, the new covenant age. To shrink back to the old way after seeing this is the willful sin.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Come Forward: Used four times in Hebrews: to the throne of grace, to God, and to the Holy of Holies ().
  • Way Cut: The veil, His flesh, was rent, and with it the old creation; a freshly slain, living way is open ().
  • Through the Spirit: The Holy of Holies is in heaven, yet the Christ there is in our spirit, so turning to the spirit is entering in.
  • Willful Sin: For the Hebrew believers, forsaking the assembly to return to Judaism's sin offerings after knowing the truth ().

Two Tabernacles, Two Ages

The Holy of Holies is not only a place but a covenant, an age, and a dispensation. God's purpose is fulfilled only where four things meet: God on the throne, the throne of grace, the Holy of Holies, and the new covenant age.

First Tabernacle

The Holy Place

A figure; its furniture typified Christ. The old age, ended by His death.

Second Tabernacle

The Holy of Holies

Realized in the new covenant dispensation, opened by His resurrection and ascension; God's presence and speaking are real.

Entering In

We have boldness by His blood, a new and living way through the rent veil, and a great Priest over the house of God (). Since that house is in the spirit (), we are God's house only when we are in the spirit; in the flesh we become quarrelling scorpions.

Shrinking Back

"No longer a sacrifice for sins" does not mean a believer who sins after salvation is beyond forgiveness. It means the old sacrifices had been replaced by Christ; going back to them would offer something God had ended. Such shrinking back leads to ruin, punishment from the living God, not eternal perdition.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How can we enter a Holy of Holies that is in heaven?The spirit is the secret.

Christ in Two Places: The same Christ is in heaven and in our spirit (), joining the two like the heavenly ladder.

Turning In: Whenever we turn to our spirit, we meet God on the throne of grace.

What does the rent veil say about the old creation?Cherubim on the veil.

All Crucified: The cherubim woven on the veil signify the creatures; when His flesh was crucified, the old creation, including us, was slain.

From Above: The veil tore from top to bottom (); God opened the way.

What is the "willful sin" of Hebrews 10:26, and what is it not?Read it in context.

What It Is: Forsaking the church to return to a terminated religion after being shown that God has annulled it.

What It Is Not: It is not saying that any sin after salvation makes forgiveness impossible.

Where are you: at the altar or at the throne?Thorns or glory.

Still Outside: Many believers stay at the cross, beholding the One crowned with thorns.

Come Forward: The call is to touch the throne of grace and the One crowned with glory, for God's purpose.
