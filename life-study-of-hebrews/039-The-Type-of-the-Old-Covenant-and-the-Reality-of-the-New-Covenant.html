Hebrews · Message 39

The Type of the Old Covenant and the Reality of the New Covenant

The two tabernacles of Hebrews 9 are two covenants and two ages. The Holy Place, the old covenant, was only a type; the Holy of Holies, the new covenant, is the reality, and its way is now open.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Two Tabernacles: The first is the Holy Place, the second the Holy of Holies; they symbolize the old and new covenants and the two ages.
  • Four No's: The Holy Place gave nourishment and light, but no presence of God, no way in, no oracle, and no meeting with Him.
  • The Key: The Holy of Holies is the new covenant; to enter one is to enter the other ().
  • The Way Opened: Christ's death rent the veil, His flesh, and manifested the way of the Holy of Holies ().
  • Mediator and Executor: Through eternal redemption Christ brings the called ones into the eternal inheritance, God Himself ().

Two Tabernacles, Two Covenants

The Holy Place had the table and the lampstand, good things but far from God. The Holy of Holies had the incense altar, the way into God's presence, and held the ark with the propitiation-cover, where God speaks and meets with man. The Hebrew believers were staggering in the Holy Place, unsure whether to press in or shrink back.

Old Covenant · Type

The Holy Place

Of this world, with ordinances of flesh (); a figure that could not perfect the worshippers' conscience.

New Covenant · Reality

The Holy of Holies

Not made by hand, not of this creation; God's glory, presence, speaking, and fellowship.

The Time of Setting Things Right

Under the old covenant the way into the Holiest was not yet manifested. At His first coming Christ fulfilled the shadows and set everything in order; this reformation differs from the restoration at His second coming. Now we need not remain in the Holy Place, in the soul; we must enter the Holy of Holies, in the spirit.

The Precious Blood and the Eternal Inheritance

Offered through the eternal Spirit, Christ's sacrifice has an eternal effect; its span covers the whole span of sin. His blood purifies the conscience, the leading part of our spirit, so that we can touch the living God in spirit () rather than serve Him religiously in the mind.

The Eternal Inheritance

God Himself, with all He is, has, has done, and will do, secured by Christ's redemption and not by our work.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is lacking in a Christian life lived in the Holy Place?The four no's.

Some Benefit: There may be nourishment and enlightenment, like a table and a lampstand.

No God: Yet there is no presence of God, no way in, no divine speaking, and no meeting with Him.

Why is the Holy of Holies called a reality, not a type?The shekinah.

Real Even Then: God's glory, presence, and speaking were truly there in the Old Testament.

Only the Way Lacking: What was missing was the way in; Christ's death has now opened it to all.

Why must the conscience be purified to serve the living God?Heb. 9:14.

Dead Religion: Serving dead things or worshipping in the mind requires no purified conscience.

Living Contact: The living God touches our spirit through the conscience; a defiled conscience blocks this contact.

How do you move from the Holy Place into the Holy of Holies today?Soul to spirit.

Through Prayer: Praying in the Lord's name, with Him as the resurrection incense, brings us into God's presence.

Into the Spirit: Leaving the soul and turning to the spirit is entering the new covenant and enjoying its bequests.
