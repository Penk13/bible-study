Hebrews · Message 35

A Heavenly Minister with a More Excellent Ministry

Our Melchisedec sits on the throne in the heavens, interceding for fighters until we are saved to the uttermost, that is, glorified. As Mediator of a better covenant, He makes its facts effective and executes its bequests.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Three Steps: Justification meets God's righteousness, sanctification His holiness, and glorification His glory.
  • Uttermost = Glorified: Hebrews 7:25 and Romans 8:34 are the only two verses on Christ's intercession; both aim at our glorification ().
  • For Fighters: Chedorlaomer typifies the by-products of death; we slay them while Christ intercedes for us.
  • Seated in Heaven: He is not Aaron standing on earth but Melchisedec sitting on the throne of the Majesty ().
  • A Better Covenant: Mediated by Him, enacted on better promises: forgiveness of sins and the law of life ().

Interceding for the Fighters

Romans 8 shows the whole creation groaning to be freed () and Christ interceding in heaven. Hebrews 7 tells us who this interceding One is: not merely the Savior, but the kingly and divine High Priest. His intercession is like a motor that energizes the machine on earth.

⚔️

On Earth: Fighting

Joshua fought Amalek while Moses held up his hand (); Abraham pursued the kings to rescue Lot.

🙌

In Heaven: Interceding

Melchisedec blessed God for delivering the enemies into Abraham's hand ().

A Heavenly Minister

His sitting shows redemption is accomplished; now He intercedes restfully for the perfecting of His redeemed. Sanctuary, priesthood, and law always go together; today all three are new and real. The tabernacle in heaven is joined to our spirit by Christ as the ladder ().

Priests on Earth

Serving the Shadow

A copy of the heavenly things ().

Minister in Heaven

Serving the Reality

The true tabernacle that the Lord pitched ().

A More Excellent Ministry

As Mediator, Christ is also the Executor in resurrection of the testament He bequeathed by His death. A covenant holds accomplished facts; once bequeathed in a testament, those facts become bequests legally designated for us.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is Christ interceding for today?Romans 8 and Hebrews 7.

Not Justification: Justification is already in Romans 4; His intercession in Romans 8 is for our glorification.

Uttermost Salvation: He intercedes until every by-product of death is swallowed up and we enter the freedom of glory.

When a brother is captured, do you rebuke him or slay Chedorlaomer?Lot's capture.

The Real Enemy: Lot's capture pictures saints held by the by-products of death, not merely by sin.

Fight, Then Feast: Rather than condemning, we fight for them; then our Melchisedec comes with His ministry.

Is glorification something we simply wait for?The operating machine.

Not Merely Objective: God needs a people on earth through whom He brings in the freedom of glory.

Energized Within: The heavenly Minister's intercession transmits power into our spirit, making us aggressive fighters.

What is the difference between a fact and a bequest?Covenant and testament.

Fact: Something accomplished in the covenant but not yet designated to anyone.

Bequest: The same thing legally willed to us in the testament, which the living Executor now carries out.
