Life-Study / Hebrews 007

Hebrews · Message 7

Jesus as the Son of Man — as Man Superior to the Angels

God never subjected the coming inhabited earth to angels; He ordained man to rule it. What the first man lost and Psalm 8 prophesied, the second Man, Jesus, has fulfilled: made a little lower than the angels to taste death, then crowned with glory and honor as Lord, Christ, Captain, and Savior.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • God and Man, Both Superior: Hebrews 1 shows Christ as the Son of God, that is, God; Hebrews 2 shows Jesus as the Son of Man, that is, man (). In both natures He is superior to the angels.
  • Man Above Angels: Angels are ministering spirits sent to serve the heirs of salvation (). We are the masters; they are the servants. Do not look up to them.
  • The Coming Inhabited Earth: This earth in the coming kingdom age (), subjected not to angels but to man (). Missing a share in it is the "just recompense" for neglecting so great a salvation, not the loss of eternal life ().
  • Kingdom → Authority → Glory: Genesis 1, Psalm 8, and Hebrews 2 share one point: God ordained man to rule the earth so He would have a realm to exercise authority and express His glory.
  • Three Stages of Man: In creation, Adam failed (). In prophecy, Psalm 8 foretold the recovery. In fulfillment, Jesus, the second Man (), is crowned with glory and honor ().

The Coming Inhabited Earth

The word for in ties the coming inhabited earth to the warning of verses 1–4. It is this earth after it becomes Christ's kingdom (; ). The stone cut out without hands smites the image of the nations and becomes a great mountain filling the whole earth (). That stone is Christ:

To the Believers

The Building Stone

Foundation, cornerstone, topstone, and living stone, producing us as living stones for God's building ().

To the Unbelieving Jews

The Stumbling Stone

Those who reject Him stumble over Him. The Lord is not merely kind to everyone.

To the Nations

The Smiting Stone

He descends from heaven, breaks the kingdoms in pieces, and becomes the kingdom filling the earth ().

Why God Needs Man to Rule

"For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory." The kingdom is for the authority, and the authority is for the glory. The church life today is this kingdom on a small scale (), a dominion where God's glory is expressed.

The Man in Three Stages

God's purpose for man unfolds in creation, prophecy, and fulfillment. The first man failed; the second Man succeeded and recovered man's lost ordination.

Image

Israel, the Prince of God

The supplanter Jacob, transformed and matured, only stretched out his hands to bless, even blessing Pharaoh. That was God expressed.

Dominion

Joseph, the Reigning Part

Joseph ruled the earth of his day. Israel with Joseph was one complete man: expressing God and representing Him.

Crowned with Glory and Honor

The little Jesus of the manger and of Nazareth has been crowned in the heavens. Glory is the splendor of His Person; honor is the preciousness of His worth and the dignity of His position. He was glorified in resurrection () and crowned in ascension as a man:

Lord and Christ

As God He was always Lord; as a man He was crowned to be Lord of all. As Christ, the anointed and appointed One, He carries out all of God's plan.

Leader and Savior

The same word as "Captain" in : Pioneer, Forerunner, Author. He has cut the way into glory, and we follow.

Angels, having no humanity, can never be our Lord, Christ, Captain, or Savior. Only the man Jesus can, in a humanity that is resurrected, uplifted, and crowned with God's glory.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is man, though physically lower than the angels, positionally superior to them?Consider who serves whom in Hebrews 1:14 and to whom the coming earth is subjected in 2:5.

Physical vs. Positional: "A little lower than the angels" () describes our make-up, not our standing. In God's economy, angels are ministering spirits sent to serve the heirs of salvation ().

Ordained to Rule: God never subjected the coming inhabited earth to angels (). From creation He ordained man to rule (). Seeing this frees us from looking up to angels and shows us the dignity of our calling.

What is the "just recompense" for neglecting so great a salvation, and how does it fit with eternal security?Follow the word "for" from Hebrews 2:3 to 2:5, and compare John 10:28.

Not Perdition: Once saved, we can never perish (). The recompense is not the loss of eternal life.

Missing the Kingdom Share: The word for links the warning of to the coming inhabited earth of 2:5. Those who neglect this salvation may miss sharing with Christ, as His partners, in the earth that has become His kingdom.

How is the church life today "the kingdom on a small scale," and what does that ask of us?Trace the sequence kingdom → authority → glory, and read Romans 14:17.

Same Principle, Smaller Scale: God needs a dominion where His authority is exercised so His glory can be expressed. Every local church is such a dominion now, with righteousness, peace, and joy in the Holy Spirit ().

Practical Meaning: Where we submit to God's authority, there His glory appears. The church is not a social club; it is the realm where God's eternal purpose for man is being carried out before the kingdom comes in full.

Why is Jesus' being our Lord, Christ, Captain, and Savior based mainly on His humanity rather than His divinity?Contrast Hebrews 1 with Hebrews 2, and consider why angels could never fill these roles.

Crowned as a Man: As God, He was already Lord. But it was as a man, made lower than the angels for the suffering of death, that He was crowned with glory and honor () and made both Lord and Christ ().

A Humanity Uplifted: His humanity is not natural but resurrected, ascended, and glorified. As the Captain (; ), He has gone ahead into glory as a man so that we, men, can follow Him there.
