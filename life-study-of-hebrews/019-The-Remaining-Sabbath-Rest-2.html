Life-Study / Hebrews 019

Hebrews · Message 19

The Remaining Sabbath Rest (2)

Rest is satisfaction, and God is satisfied when man expresses and represents Him. Israel with the temple, then Jesus, and now the church as His enlargement have each been God's Sabbath. The church is the new man, God's habitation, and His kingdom today: the remaining rest we must be diligent to enter and never leave.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Rest Is Satisfaction: From Genesis 1 (image and dominion) to the New Jerusalem bearing God's jasper appearance () with His throne at the center (), God rests when He is expressed and represented.
  • Three Ages: Israel in the land of Immanuel () with the glory-filled temple; Jesus, in whom God was well pleased (); now the church, Christ enlarged.
  • Why the Church Is Rest: It is the new man (), God's habitation (), and God's kingdom today (; ).
  • The Key "Wherefore": "Whose house we are… Wherefore… Today" (–) connects the house of God to the remaining rest. Be diligent to enter it ().
  • Do Not Give It Up: Barnabas left Paul and disappears from the record. Watch out for those who cause divisions (); the God of peace crushes Satan ().

The Sabbath Rest in Various Ages

God works gradually: Adam, Abel, Enosh, Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Israel, and then the second Man.

Why the Church Is Today's Sabbath

After the old man was created, God rested. After the new man was created, there must be a new and better Sabbath.

The New Man

Created in Christ to express and represent God, as the first man was. A new creation brings a new rest.

God's Habitation

Canaan was rest because God put His name there (). The church is His dwelling in our spirit.

God's Kingdom Today

Born of the Spirit, we entered the kingdom (). Church and kingdom are used interchangeably ().

Evidence the Kingdom Is HereVerse
Regeneration brings us into the kingdom
"I will build My church… the keys of the kingdom"
The kingdom is righteousness, peace, and joy
We are receiving an unshakable kingdom
John, a companion "in the kingdom," writes to the churches

Entering and Staying

Hebrews 2 shows the church where the Son praises the Father (); Hebrews 3 shows the house the Son cares for (); Hebrews 4 urges us to enter the rest (). The "wherefore" of 3:7 ties them together.

A Warning from Barnabas

Barnabas brought Paul into the ministry and labored with him, then separated over Mark and is not mentioned again in Acts. Paul warns of divisions that cause falling away (). Once we have entered the rest, we should never leave it.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is rest fundamentally a matter of satisfaction rather than ceasing from work?What satisfies God's heart from Genesis to Revelation?

Desire Fulfilled: We cannot rest until our desire is satisfied. God's desire is man expressing Him in His image and representing Him with His dominion.

Working in Rest: When God's desire is met, we rest even while laboring. The New Jerusalem, bearing His appearance with His throne at the center, will be His full rest ().

If Christ is our rest, why must the church also be our rest?Consider the Head and the Body, the individual and the corporate Christ.

Christ Enlarged: The church is the Body of Christ, His enlargement. If the individual Christ was God's Sabbath, how much more the corporate Christ.

Where He Is: Christ walks among the lampstands and speaks to the churches. To take Him as rest, we come where He is: in the church, our home and God's house ().

How do we know the kingdom of God is present today, not only in the future?Look at John 3:5 and Matthew 16:18–19.

Born into It: As an animal is born into the animal kingdom, we born of the Spirit are in God's kingdom ().

Church and Kingdom: The Lord spoke of building His church and giving the keys of the kingdom in one breath (). It has not come in fullness, but it has come.

What does it mean to be "diligent to enter that rest" today?Read Hebrews 3:6–7 and 4:11 together.

Hold Fast in the House: We are God's house if we hold fast (). "Wherefore… Today, if you will hear His voice" ().

Not Drifting Out: Many saved ones remain in the wilderness. Diligence means entering the church life and remaining, not being drawn away by divisions ().
