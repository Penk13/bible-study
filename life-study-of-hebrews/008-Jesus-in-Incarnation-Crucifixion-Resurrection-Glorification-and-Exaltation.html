Hebrews · Message 8

Jesus in Incarnation, Crucifixion, Resurrection, Glorification, and Exaltation

No other short portion of the Word puts all five steps of Christ's process together as Hebrews 2 does, and it presents each one in a high way: He shared our blood and flesh, tasted death for everything, brought forth many brothers, entered glory, and was set over all things.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Incarnation: Not merely "God sent His Son to die so we escape hell," but the Creator sharing in the children's blood and flesh (), made like His brothers in all things to sympathize with them ().
  • Crucifixion: He tasted death for everything, not only for man (; ), made propitiation for sins, destroyed the devil, and freed us from the fear of death.
  • Resurrection: The one grain became many grains (). He declared the Father's name to His brothers and praised the Father in the church ().
  • Glorification: As Captain and Pioneer He entered into glory (). Man crowned Him with thorns; God crowned Him with glory and honor.
  • Exaltation: Given the dominion Adam lost and set over all things (). Born in the first man, we are reborn in the second.

Five Steps in One Chapter

Hebrews 1 shows Christ as the Son of God from eternity past to eternity future. Hebrews 2, in the same principle, shows Jesus as the Son of Man through five steps:

Incarnation: The Highest "Philosophy"

The gospel is not low, but it has often been preached in a low way. The thought of Hebrews is deep and purposeful, and it convinces thoughtful people:

🎄

The Low Preaching

"We were sinful, so He came to save us from hell." True, yet shallow, with no purpose or goal, and often tied to Christmas customs that thoughtful people find childish.

🕊️

The High Preaching

God became exactly what we are, sharing our blood and flesh (), to be one with us and to gain His corporation, Christ and the church.

Crucifixion: An All-Inclusive Death

Do not trust feelings over God's Word. What His death accomplished is a fact bequeathed to us in the testament, to be taken by faith:

AccomplishmentVerseMeaning
Tasted death for everything; Not only for man but for every creature, as Noah's ark saved both people and animals. Death is abolished ().
Made propitiationHilaskomai: to appease by satisfying God's righteous demands on us.
Destroyed the devil; Brought to nought in the flesh on the cross. To say he still prevails is a lie.
Released us from slaveryNo more fear of death, and no more bondage under it.

Resurrection: Many Brothers and the Church

Resurrection is everywhere in nature: a seed dies and wheat comes forth. Christ was the one grain falling into the ground (), and we are the many grains.

Brothers

Brought Forth

His death released the divine life; His resurrection imparted it into us. At once He called us "My brethren" ().

The Father's Name

Declared

The Father is the source of life and being. Born of Him, we partake of the divine nature ().

The Church

Praised in the Midst

The first church meeting was the evening of His resurrection, not Pentecost. There He praised the Father among His brothers ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is "sharing in the children's blood and flesh" a higher view of the incarnation than "coming to save us from hell"?Consider purpose: what God wanted to gain, not only what we needed to escape.

Purpose, Not Only Rescue: The common view is correct but centers on our need. Hebrews shows God becoming exactly what we are () to be one with us and to gain Christ and the church.

Sympathy: To sympathize, one must be on the same level. Being made like His brothers in all things (), He can enter our weakness from within.

If the devil has been destroyed and death abolished, why do we still feel their pressure, and how should we respond?Weigh feelings against the accomplished facts in God's Word.

Facts, Not Feelings: Scripture says the devil has been brought to nought () and death abolished (). These are bequests in a testament, not hopes.

Taking the Bequest: Feelings that say otherwise are lies. Faith takes what the Word declares and stands on it, and experience follows.

What does it mean that Christ "declared the Father's name" to His brothers in resurrection?Think of what a father is to a child, and read 2 Peter 1:4.

The Source: The Father's name is simply "Father," the source of life and being. In resurrection we were born of Him (), so the Sanctifier and the sanctified are all of one ().

Our Inheritance: Because He is our Father, we partake of the divine nature and have all things relating to life and godliness ().

Are you living in the first man or in the second Man, and what difference does it make?Compare what Adam lost with what the exalted Jesus regained.

Two Men: Born in Adam, we lost everything with him. Reborn in Christ, we regained everything, for He has been given the dominion Adam lost and set over all things ().

Looking to Him: We do not yet see all things subjected, but we see Jesus crowned (). Living in the second Man means looking away from the failure of the first to Him in glory.
