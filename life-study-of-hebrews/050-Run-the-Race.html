Hebrews · Message 50

Run the Race

The Christian life is a race, and the race is Christ Himself as God's unique way. We must not stand still or even walk in Him, but run, looking away from all things unto the Jesus seated in heaven.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Prize: Not salvation, which is settled, but a reward for those who run and win ().
  • The Race Is Christ: Glorification is the goal, the law of life the process, losing the soul the way to run; the race itself is Christ, the way ().
  • Run, Don't Walk: Walkers look for exits; runners keep their eyes on the goal.
  • Paul the Example: A Hebrew healed of the same disease, he began, continued, and finished the race ().

God's Unique Way

From Genesis to Revelation there is one way, Christ. Man was made in God's image, which is Christ, and set before the tree of life. Satan distracted him, yet God brought him back through redemption and pictured the way in the tabernacle. Judaism then turned that very picture into a religion that distracted people from Christ.

How to Run

Paul says "us," counting himself among the runners. Fleeing into the Holy of Holies where our Forerunner has entered is the same as running the race ().

Looking unto the Heavenly Christ

Paul's other epistles mostly show the Christ in our spirit, set against the flesh. Hebrews points to the Christ seated in heaven, set against earthly religion: Heir, Captain, High Priest, Minister, Surety, Forerunner, Author of faith. Crowned with glory, He is a magnet drawing His seekers away from every distraction.

Saved, Yet a Castaway?

Paul disciplined his body lest he be disapproved (). A saved one cannot perish, but he can fail to be approved for the prize.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What exactly is the race we are running?Goal, process, way.

Not the Goal or Process: Glorification is the goal and the law of life is the process.

Christ Himself: Since He is the way, He is the course we run; the way and the race are one.

How can good things like the temple become distractions?Bethany vs. the temple.

Pictures Become Religion: What God ordained to show the way was used by Satan to keep people from Christ.

Where the Way Is: While priests served in the temple, the Lord was enjoying fellowship in a house in Bethany.

Are you walking the way or running the race?Looking for an exit.

Walking: A slow pace leaves time to look around, and sooner or later we walk out.

Running: Eyes fixed on the goal leave no room for distraction.

Why does Hebrews stress the Christ in heaven rather than the Christ in our spirit?Heavenly vs. earthly.

The Contrast: The Hebrews' danger was an earthly religion, so they needed to see a heavenly Christ.

The Supply: Looking to Him there, we receive heaven, life, and strength to run the heavenly race on earth.
