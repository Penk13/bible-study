Hebrews · Message 15

Christ as the Apostle Superior to Moses

The third comparison in Hebrews: Moses, Judaism's great leader, was a faithful part of God's house and a photograph of things to come. Christ is the Apostle sent from God, both the material and the Builder of the house, and the house is us: God's living household.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Four Comparisons: Our God vs. the Jews' concept of God; Christ vs. angels (as God and as man); Christ vs. Moses; Christ vs. Aaron.
  • Apostle and High Priest: "Consider the Apostle and High Priest of our confession, Jesus" (). As Apostle He came from God with God (); as High Priest He went to God with us ().
  • Moses, a Photograph: Sent to form Israel into God's house, faithful in it, yet only a part of it, "a testimony of things to be spoken later" ().
  • Material and Builder: In humanity Christ is the stone (; ); in divinity He is the Builder, God Himself (). So He is worthy of more glory.
  • We Are His House: To Christ the church is the Body; to God it is the house (), a living household, Bethel with Christ as the ladder ().

Apostle and High Priest

Few have heard that Jesus is an Apostle, the first one in the New Testament. "Apostle" means one sent by a higher authority.

Typified by Moses

The Apostle

Came from God, with God, to share God with us, that we might partake of His life, nature, and fullness ().

Typified by Aaron

The High Priest

Went to God, with us, to present us and all our cases to God (; ).

Moses and Christ Compared

Moses was sent to bring Israel out of Egypt and constitute them God's house. The tabernacle was only a symbol; God's real dwelling was the people.

Bethel Today

Where the church is, there is Bethel, the house of God and gate of heaven (), with the Son of Man as the ladder joining earth to heaven (). It is a portable, walking house. An individual Christian is a separated stone and cannot enjoy Christ as the Apostle of the house.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the difference between Christ as Apostle and Christ as High Priest?Notice the direction of each: from God or to God.

Two Directions: As the Apostle, typified by Moses, He came from God and with God to bring God to us (). As the High Priest, typified by Aaron, He goes to God with us to present us to Him ().

One Person: Both offices meet in Jesus (). He brings God into us and brings us to God.

Why is Christ worthy of more glory than Moses?Compare a part of a house with its builder (Hebrews 3:3–4).

Part vs. Builder: Moses had only humanity, good material for the house. Christ has humanity as material and divinity as the Builder, who is God ().

Photograph vs. Person: Moses was a testimony of things to come (), a picture. Christ is the reality the picture showed.

What does it mean that God's house is "living"?Compare a family with the building it lives in.

Household and House: A human family and its house are two things, one living and one physical. God's family is God's house; both are living, composed of His children in His life and reality.

God Within: As Christ indwells every member of the Body, the Father is not a separate member of the household but is in all the children ().

Why is the church life necessary for preaching a high gospel?Think of a salesman with and without a sample.

A Model: People want something they can see now. Without a church life that displays genuine humanity and God's house, the high gospel is only words.

Come and See: When the church is Bethel, the gate of heaven (), we can say, "Come and see." The church life itself becomes the preaching.
