Exodus · Message 18

God's Demand and Pharaoh's Resistance (4)

The whole creation stands only under Christ's redemption. Egypt, rejecting it, lay exposed to judgment, while God put a redemption between His people and Pharaoh's: flies, pestilence, and boils fell on Egypt but not on Goshen.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Ten: The number of completeness in human living; ten plagues expose fallen human life fully.
  • Under Redemption: The universe should have collapsed at Adam's fall, but God views it under the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world ().
  • A Redemption Between: God set a redemption between Israel and Egypt (, lit.); Goshen was spared.
  • Implication: Egypt's cattle were judged with their owners (); those who love the Lord bring blessing to all around them.
  • The Ashes: Even the remainder of what has been burned must be dealt with ().

Why the World Still Stands

When the head of creation sinned, all creation came under the curse. God neither tolerated the sin nor gave up His purpose; He clothed Adam with skins () and reconciled all things through Christ's death (). Under redemption He may righteously preserve the earth or judge it.

Rejecting Redemption

Egypt

Water becomes blood, fish become frogs, dust becomes lice, the air brings flies.

Under Redemption

Goshen

Water stays water, the earth yields grain; the plagues do not touch God's people.

The Second Group: The Air

The first group judged the water and the earth; the second judged the air and what depended on it. God dealt with Egypt finely, item by item, to educate both Egypt and Israel.

PlagueWhat Was JudgedMeaning Today

PestilenceCattle for travel and for foodThe world's transportation and eating, judged by implication
BoilsAshes of the furnaceThe remainder of what we think is already dealt with

The Ashes

After water, earth, air, and cattle had been judged, the ashes remained, and God would not tolerate even them. A former passion may be dealt with, yet its remains can still cause boils, even by how we testify of it.

The Magicians Fall Silent

In the second group the magicians could do nothing. Philosophy cannot save from moral and spiritual pollution; only God's salvation can.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How can a righteous God tolerate a sinful world?The Lamb before the foundation.

Redemption in View: God sees creation through Christ's redemption, accomplished in His eyes before the world began.

Mercy to All: Unbelievers enjoy its benefit unknowingly, so that they may have time to repent and receive it.

Is your "water" water or blood?The same supply, two conditions.

Under Christ: The same food and supply are a blessing when they are under His redemption.

Apart from Christ: Outside it, they turn to blood, frogs, and lice. The difference is not the thing but the covering.

What does "implication" mean for your life?The cattle of Egypt and of Israel.

Negative: Egypt's cattle suffered for their owners' stubbornness. Stay away from involvement in sin.

Positive: One who loves the Lord becomes a factor of blessing to household, companions, and society.

What ashes still remain in you?The remainder after dealing.

Not Finished: We may boast that something is dealt with, while its remains are still kept and treasured.

Covered: Pray to be under the prevailing blood, and let the anointing keep us from spreading ashes into the air.
