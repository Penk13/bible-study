Exodus · Message 50

Brought into the Presence of God and into the Knowledge of Him

Salvation, provision, victory over the flesh, and the kingdom lead to Sinai, where God's people first enter fellowship with Him. In His presence they hear His voice and come to know His grace and holiness, becoming His treasure, His priests, and a holy nation.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A New Section: Chapters 19–40 bring the saved people into fellowship with God for building His dwelling.
  • Serving at the Mountain: God had promised that the people would serve Him on this mountain (); a three-day trip took over two months.
  • Into His Presence: At Sinai, the place of God's speaking and vision, they came sanctified and in resurrection, on the third day.
  • Grace and Holiness: God carried them on eagles' wings (), yet set bounds around His holy mountain.
  • What We Become: His peculiar treasure, a kingdom of priests, and a holy nation ().

Brought into His Presence

Until chapter 19 the people heard about God through Moses; now God came down and spoke to them directly. Many Christians likewise have heard about God without hearing Him. Our experience matches Israel's: saved, supplied, victorious over the flesh by Christ above and the Spirit within, and then brought into the kingdom and the presence of God. Sinai was not a place of miracles but of speaking and vision, as were the mountains of Matthew 5, 17, and 24 and John's mountain in Revelation.

Brought into the Knowledge of God

Fellowship brings knowledge. Before Sinai the people knew nothing of the tabernacle, its furniture, or the God whom the law would describe. In His presence they first knew His grace: He bore them on eagles' wings. Those who wait on Him mount up like eagles (), and by grace we are what we are (). They then knew His holiness. Only Moses and Aaron could pass the boundary, and when we cross the boundary of His holiness, fellowship is lost.

What Fellowship Makes Us

The thunder and smoke were a warning to those careless of holiness, not to Moses and Aaron, whose portion was God's presence. What Israel had as a picture, believers have in reality ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did a three-day journey take more than two months?Wandering.

Our Pattern: We could enter fellowship soon after salvation, but most of us wander, still under God's care.

No Excuses: Saying "I am weak" keeps us from the mountain; Christ interceding and the Spirit fighting are not weak.

What is the difference between hearing about God and hearing God?Sinai.

Secondhand: Teaching about God, even accurate, is not His direct speaking.

In His Presence: Meetings should be a mountain of God where His speaking and vision are present.

How would I prepare for "the third day"?Resurrection.

No Idle Talk: Instead of gossip and complaint, pray for the Lord to show anything between Him and us.

New Position: Drop one thing after another until the old has passed away.

Why do grace and holiness come together in fellowship?Eagles' wings and the boundary.

Grace First: In His presence we see that He has carried us through everything.

Then Holiness: To remain with Him we must keep within the boundary of His holiness.

What makes God's people a holy nation?Mutual enjoyment.

Treasure and Priests: He treasures us, and as priests we enjoy Him.

Separated: Nothing separates us unto God like this two-way enjoyment.
