Exodus · Message 29

Israel's Crossing of the Red Sea

The Red Sea is a type of baptism: the separating line that buries Satan and the world and brings God's people into a realm of freedom, with God's habitation and kingdom as the goal.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Separating Line: Without the sea, Israel could still return to Egypt; baptism makes salvation secure ().
  • Type of Baptism: Israel was baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea ().
  • By Faith: Faith came when God spoke, and they walked through as on dry land ().
  • Saved Through Water: As with Noah, the water saved them out of the old world into a realm for God's purpose ().
  • The Song's Goal: Moses praised not only the victory but God's habitation and eternal reign ().

The Blood and the Water

Israel had two problems: God's judgment and Pharaoh's tyranny. The Passover dealt with the first but not the second. God had prepared the Red Sea as a baptistry and led His people to it so that turning back would be impossible. Most Christians today know the blood but have not experienced the saving, separating water.

The Passover Blood

Saved them from God's righteous judgment.

The Red Sea Water

Saved and separated them from Egypt's slavery, burying Pharaoh's army ().

Baptized unto Moses, in the Cloud and the Sea

The people did not have faith at first; they complained. Faith came when the Lord gave Moses a word, and they stepped between the walls of water. Baptism is likewise through faith in the operation of God (), both for those baptizing and those being baptized.

From Salvation to Habitation

On the shore Moses sang a song the overcomers sing again by the glassy sea (). It speaks of God's habitation in the perfect tense, as already done, though the tabernacle was not yet built. Baptism leads to the church life. Miriam and the women followed with praise of the victory, though her song does not mention the habitation.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why was the Passover not enough to free Israel?Two problems.

Judgment and Tyranny: The blood answered God's judgment, but Pharaoh's usurpation needed the exodus and the sea.

No Return: The sea was a line that made going back to Egypt impossible. Baptism seals our salvation in the same way.

What is the difference between ritual baptism and the experience of the Red Sea?Saving, separating water.

A Form: Many have been baptized as a ceremony without being separated from the world.

A Reality: In Christ's death, the world's power is buried; in the Spirit, we are led on. Both are received by faith.

How did Israel move from complaint to faith?God's word.

Not Rebuked: The Lord was not angry at their unbelief; He gave Moses a word.

Faith Comes: When the word came, faith came, and the people followed Moses into the sea.

What should we tell new believers is the goal of their baptism?Habitation and kingdom.

Not Only Rescue: God's aim is more than freedom from bondage; it is a dwelling place among a separated people.

The Church Life: Baptism brings them to the mountain of God's inheritance, where the church is built as His house and kingdom.
