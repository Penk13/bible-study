Exodus · Message 15

God's Further Training of Moses

Defeated in the first conflict, Moses needed more than a call; he needed experience. God trained him to look away from the people, Pharaoh, and his own lips to God's name, covenant, assuring word, ordination, and strong hand.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Five and Three: Positively, God's name, covenant, word of assurance, ordination, and strong hand; negatively, the people's unbelief, Pharaoh's stubbornness, and Moses' natural concept.
  • Assurance, Not Environment: God would deliver Israel, take them as His people, and make them His armies (). The environment changes; His word does not.
  • Uncircumcised Lips: Moses expected God to make his speaking powerful (). God left his lips as they were and gave him Aaron.
  • Ordained, Commissioned, Representing: The genealogy shows God's selection; Moses was sent to represent God, and Aaron represented him ().
  • The Strong Hand: Words without a hand are vain; God's hand backs His word ().

Graduating Through Experience

Moses' call in chapters three and four was the most complete in the Bible, yet his first attempt ended in apparent failure, and he complained (). God did not send him back until He had trained him further.

Forget Your Lips

The children of Israel did not hearken, Pharaoh did not hearken, and even Moses did not hearken: after a long word of assurance he still pleaded his uncircumcised lips. The environment should have exposed his shortage and turned him to trust; instead he complained.

Moses' Concept

Change My Lips

"Unless You make my speaking powerful, I cannot go." Cleaving to his natural faculty, he neglected God's word.

God's Way

Same Lips, Sent One

God made him God to Pharaoh and gave Aaron as his prophet. The vessel stays earthen; the treasure is within ().

Ordination and the Strong Hand

The genealogy names only Reuben, Simeon, and Levi, tracing the line of God's choosing down to Moses and Aaron. They were not presumptuous; they were chosen before birth. Such a sent one is backed by God's hand.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did God not believe the situation that Moses described?The environment versus His word.

His Basis: God believed in Himself, His covenant, His word of assurance, and His hand. What Moses reported was true, but temporary.

The Outcome: The same unbelieving people later marched around Jericho as His army. Today "I will build My church" () stands above every discouraging report.

What are your "uncircumcised lips"?Waiting to be changed before obeying.

The Hidden Demand: Moses was not merely confessing like Isaiah (); he was complaining that God had not improved him.

The Lesson: Success depends on being sent and on what God is, not on our skill. The thorn bush burns without becoming something else, and the glory goes to God.

How can the environment speak to us without ruling us?Two sides of the same unbelief.

Not to Be Believed: Circumstances fluctuate; they are not the final word.

To Be Heeded: Yet opposition can expose our shortage and press us to trust God more, rather than to come to Him with a complaining spirit.

What is the difference between representation and presentation?Authority.

Presentation: Merely displays something; it carries no authority.

Representation: Comes with commission, so authority is present. We should not assume it, but simply be sure we are chosen and sent, and let authority follow.
