Exodus · Message 26

Israel's Exodus from Egypt (1)

Every believer has the Passover, but few have the exodus. Getting out of the world requires God's strong hand subduing our environment, and a watchful people who march out as His army.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • By Strength of Hand: The blood saved Israel from judgment; God's hand saved them from Pharaoh by subduing the whole environment ().
  • Driven Out: The Egyptians urged them to leave in haste; the world itself eventually wants us gone ().
  • No Time for Leaven: God arranges our situation so that no time remains for sinful things ().
  • Plundering Egypt: They left with silver, gold, and clothing, later given for the tabernacle ().
  • Watching and Marching: A night of observation ended the 430 years of sojourning, and Israel went out in martial array ().

Saved by the Blood and by the Hand

Israel could not leave Egypt by their own power. Twelve negotiations and ten plagues were needed before Pharaoh and the Egyptians were subdued. Many believers have the Passover but not the exodus because some part of their environment has not yet been subdued. Rather than give up, we cooperate with the Lord, who alone can do it.

🩸

The Blood

Saves us from God's righteous judgment.

✋

The Hand

Saves us from Satan's usurpation and from the world.

Leaving in a Pure Way

Leavened bread takes time to prepare, and so does sin. Hurried out of Egypt, Israel baked only unleavened cakes. God may use a sudden move, a pressing situation, or even physical weakness to separate us from our leaven, so that we leave the world purely.

The World Pushes Us Out

As long as we remain in the world, the world has no peace. Under the Lord's hand, those around us end up urging us to go.

Plundering Egypt for the Tabernacle

Unlike religions that teach taking nothing from the world, God told His people to ask for Egypt's wealth. Like Paul, we may be poor yet make many rich (). We work diligently in the world, but not for the world.

Righteous

Back Wages

Not robbery, but payment owed for years of forced labor.

Not for Self

No Private Gain

The aim was never to enrich God's people.

God's Testimony

The Tabernacle

The riches were offered to build God's dwelling, Christ with the church.

Watching and Marching Out

Israel left absolutely, with children, flocks, and herds, so prevailing that a mixed multitude followed. The exodus ended 430 years of sojourning in a strange land; until we leave the world, we are not dwelling in Christ as our good land.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why do many genuine believers have the Passover but not the exodus?The environment.

Not Yet Subdued: The exodus needs a fitting environment, and only God's hand can subdue it.

Cooperate: Giving up is not ours to decide either; we stay open to the Lord as He works on our situation.

How have circumstances left you no time for leaven?Haste, moves, weakness.

Sin Takes Time: Like leavened bread, sinful things need time to prepare. God can remove that time.

A Pure Exodus: What exhortation could not accomplish, a changed environment often does, and we can thank Him for it.

What is the difference between working in the world and working for the world?Where the gain goes.

Diligent Labor: We should study and work well, earning a proper living.

For His Testimony: The gain is not for the world or merely for ourselves, but offered for God's building. That is plundering Egypt.

Why must leaving the world be a night of watching?Not superficial.

Not Simple: Even God was watching that night. A light view of “do not love the world” leaves us asleep in Egypt.

Ready to March: Those who watch unto the Lord know when to go out and march as His army.
