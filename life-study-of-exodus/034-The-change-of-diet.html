Life-Study / Exodus 034

Exodus · Message 34

The Change of Diet

Redeemed and delivered, Israel was still Egyptian in constitution and could not be material for God's dwelling. By changing their diet to manna, God reconstitutes His people with Christ so that they can build and become His habitation.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Be Nothing, Do Nothing: God wants to be everything to us and do everything for us, so that we may become His dwelling place.
  • Reconstitution: Exodus 12 gives redemption and life; chapter 16 gives what both lack, a new constitution.
  • Exposed by Hunger: God waited until the Egyptian food ran out, exposing an Egyptian appetite ().
  • Regulated by Manna: Quails came without rules; manna came with regulations that make us correspond to God ().
  • Nothing but Christ: Israel had "nothing at all, besides this manna" (). Christ is the food that does not perish.

Out of Egypt, but Still Egyptian

Raised on cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic, the people's very tissue was Egyptian. God does not build His house with redeemed Egyptians. Our Egyptian diet today is whatever we feed on for satisfaction: television, sports, shopping, money, position. The issue is deeper than loving the world; what we hunger for is what constitutes us.

Egyptian Diet

Makes People Egyptian

Fits the lust of the flesh, provokes God's holy anger, and ends in death ().

Heavenly Diet

Makes People Heavenly

Fulfills God's purpose, tests us concerning His will, and keeps us alive for Him ().

The Regulations of Manna

Grace has more regulations than law, because life regulates, just as proper table manners help us enjoy a meal. To eat manna we must cooperate with God's ways, and in so doing we become one with Him. The more manna we eat, the more our living matches what He is.

Eating Christ and Caring for the Church

Within months of leaving Egypt, a people fed on manna began building the tabernacle; only those reconstituted with Christ can build the church, and they become God's dwelling. For forty years Israel did little but gather manna and care for the tabernacle (). We, too, are here simply to eat Christ and practice the church life.

Food That Endures

The world labors for food that perishes. Christ alone is the food that abides to eternal life (). We need not only a new diet but a new appetite.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can redeemed people still be unfit material for God's building?Constitution.

Same Fibers: Redemption, deliverance, and supply do not by themselves change what we are made of.

Reconstituted: Only as Christ replaces the Egyptian element do we become fit for, and then become, God's dwelling.

What is on your Egyptian diet today?What you feed on for satisfaction.

Not Only Sin: Entertainment, news, fashion, or advancement can all be food we live on.

Turning: We may use necessary things, but our appetite should be for Christ. When something else becomes our food, we can confess and turn back to Him.

How can grace carry more regulation than law?Life regulates.

Wild Flesh: Quails came without restraint, matching an unrestrained appetite.

Divine Life: Manna's rules on timing, measure, and rest train us to be one with God, not to bind us outwardly.

Is eating Christ only because there is nothing else the same as a changed appetite?Diet vs. appetite.

Forced Diet: Israel may have eaten manna simply because they had nothing else, while longing for Egypt.

True Hunger: The Lord wants us to love, breathe, drink, and eat Him because we desire Him.
