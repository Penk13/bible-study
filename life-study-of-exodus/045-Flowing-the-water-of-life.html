Exodus · Message 45

Flowing the Water of Life

Drinking and flowing are two sides of one thing. Without an outflow our drinking stops and our thirst dies; by speaking, and by being one with the smitten Christ, the water wells up into eternal life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • One Thing: The water we drink becomes a spring welling up within us ().
  • Outflow Keeps Inflow: Like a hose, how much flows out determines how much can flow in; no flowing nullifies drinking.
  • Flow by Speaking: Calling, praying, singing, praising, and speaking to and for the Lord release the water.
  • Into Eternal Life: The flowing brings us and others into a situation flooded with life.
  • The Smitten Rock: Identified with the crucified Christ, our natural life is smitten and the flow is pure ().

Why Flowing Matters More

For believers, the main problem is not starting to drink but continuing to flow. It is the outflow that keeps the inner current moving. You cannot see water entering a hose, but anyone can see whether it flows out.

Flowing by Speaking

The flowing is like flushing a pipe with a sudden rush of water. We should be bubbling, not dumb: speaking to the Lord, for Him, and with Him, even to the walls of our room if no one is there. Scripture describes believers continually speaking, singing, and giving thanks (; ).

In the Meetings

Function, Don't Wait

Those who complain of poor meetings may have made them poor by their silence. Take every chance to pray, praise, and thank.

Against Negatives

The Best Repellent

Silence invites swarms of negative thoughts and temper; speaking drives them away like a repellent.

The Result

Welling Up

A spring always flows; speaking brings a visit or a meeting into the fullness of life and of the Spirit.

Identified with the Smitten Christ

The rock had to be smitten for water to flow. We need not smite ourselves; by being one with the crucified Christ, our natural life is put to death. Then our prayers and testimonies are not natural but the pure flow of the divine life, which carries the supply of the tree of life () and becomes rivers ().

🗣️

Natural Speaking

Praise and prayer mixed with the self; even good words carry no life.

🪨

Pure Flow

The natural life smitten with Christ; what flows out is God's eternal life without mixture.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can't we keep drinking without flowing?The hose.

Proportion: The amount of outflow sets the amount of inflow.

Current: Only the outflow keeps the inner current moving; blocked, the drinking ceases and thirst fades.

How can I flow in a practical way today?Speak.

Speak: Call, pray, sing, and talk to the Lord aloud, even with no one around.

Seize Moments: Preach the gospel, speak truth to believers, and function in every meeting.

What does it mean for water to well up into eternal life now?Flooded with life.

Experience: When the water flows, we are brought into a situation filled and flooded with life.

Contrast: A dry, silent visit drags both people down into death; a flowing one lifts both into life.

Why must the flow come through the smitten Christ?No mixture.

Natural Speaking: Speaking alone can be merely natural, even in praise.

Pure Life: Identified with His death, our natural life is smitten and only the divine life flows out.
