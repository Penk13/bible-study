Exodus · Message 35

The Heavenly Diet — Manna (1)

Eating is a basic concept from Genesis to Revelation. Manna is a type of Christ, the same as the tree of life: the one food for God's people, taken into us as the subjective Spirit so that we live by Him moment by moment.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • First Mention: After creating man, God spoke about eating. Man fell by eating wrongly and is recovered by eating rightly ().
  • A Type of Christ: The Father gives the true bread out of heaven; whoever eats Him lives because of Him ().
  • Manna Is the Tree of Life: One God, one Christ, one Spirit, one food, from the garden to the New Jerusalem.
  • Christ Subjective: As the life-giving Spirit in our spirit, He can be eaten and assimilated; we are one spirit with Him ().
  • Unique Food, One Ministry: There is one manna and, in the New Testament, one ministry of Christ as life ().

Eating Through the Whole Bible

Man was made in God's image, an outward likeness, and then placed before the tree of life to take God in as his content. Eating is central in redemption too: the Passover lamb was eaten, and in the wilderness God fed His people manna. What we eat determines how we live, even affecting our temper; the heavenly food both nourishes us and eliminates negative things.

Living by Christ, Moment by Moment

Whatever satisfies, strengthens, and sustains us is our food. We may need education and other useful things, but we must not live on them. Christ can be our food because He is subjective, the processed God as the all-inclusive Spirit in our spirit. Being one spirit with Him is like breathing: not once for all, but moment by moment. An unkind look is enough to cut us off, so we watch and pray (), confess, and turn back.

Many Sources

Today's Egyptians

Living on education, sport, amusement, and religious or psychological supermarkets.

One Diet

Christ Alone

Our unique satisfaction, strength, and sustenance, eaten all day long ().

One Food, One Ministry

The twelve were allotted a portion of "this ministry" (), and Paul spoke of having "this ministry." The four Gospels differ in angle but minister one Person. Many ministries in Christianity produce many kinds of food and so cause division; one ministry of Christ as life keeps God's people in oneness. One ministry does not mean one speaker: many speak the same thing for the same goal.

No Choice of Menu

Israel could not choose food to suit personal taste. In the New Jerusalem there is one river and one tree of life.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is eating, rather than behavior, the focus of Exodus 16?We live by what we eat.

Organic Change: God did not want to adjust His people outwardly but to change them inwardly by their food.

Fall and Recovery: Man fell by eating the wrong tree; he is saved and recovered by eating the tree of life.

What does it mean that manna and the tree of life are interchangeable?One food.

Same Christ: The manna of Exodus 16 and John 6 is the tree of Genesis 2 and Revelation 22.

No Other Tree: God has no second diet, no tree of doctrine or prophecy alongside the tree of life.

How can knowing "manna is Christ" remain only doctrine?Twenty-four hours a day.

Occasional: We may feed on Him in a meeting yet live on other things the rest of the day.

Practical: Each morning ask for grace to be one spirit with Him; through the day, notice when the switch goes off and come back.

How does one food relate to oneness among believers?One ministry.

Many Foods, Many Divisions: Different ministries supplying different things divide God's people.

One Christ Ministered: When all speak Christ as life with one tone and goal, the church is preserved in oneness.
