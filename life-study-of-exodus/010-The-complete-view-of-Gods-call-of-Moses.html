Exodus · Message 10

The Complete View of God's Call of Moses

From the burning thorn-bush to Zipporah's cutting, God's call is one complete whole. What Moses experienced in type, Paul experienced in reality, and it must all be our experience today.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Burning Bush: The Triune God burning within a redeemed humanity, the vision of the divine economy. Once seen, it never lets us go.
  • The One Who Is: God is "I AM" () and the God of resurrection. Know Him by what He is, not by miracles.
  • Three Signs: The called one must know the Devil, the flesh, and the world, as Paul did.
  • Matching and Cutting: Aaron restricted and humbled Moses; Zipporah made him a "bloody husband." Only then was the call complete.

From Thorn-Bush to New Jerusalem

Forty years later Moses still spoke of God as the One "who dwelt in the bush" (). The line of God's dwelling runs from the bush to the tabernacle, the temple, Christ Himself (; ), the church (), and the New Jerusalem.

Exodus 3

A Redeemed Thorn-Bush

Once cursed thorns, now burning with God yet not consumed.

Exodus 40

The Tabernacle

Acacia wood overlaid with gold, linen worked with gold: humanity transformed by divinity.

Knowing Who God Is

He is the One "who is and who was and who is to come" (). Weakness, strength, riches, and poverty pass away; God remains. Paul, facing martyrdom, did not ask for an open prison door. He knew God as the One who is, and he would know Him afterward as the God of resurrection.

Not by Miracles

Whether God does something for us or not, He is. Every death situation is an opportunity for Him to be the God of resurrection.

The Signs and the Helps

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why would Moses rather recall the thorn-bush than the tabernacle?A sweet memory.

Grace: That God would dwell in thorns under the curse is marvelous. The bush shows where He started with us.

Process: The church may still have thorns, but it is being transformed toward gold, pearl, and precious stone.

What does it mean that the vision will not let us go?Captured, not just informed.

Burned In: The vision of the Triune God dispensing Himself into the church is wrought into our being. Even if we are weak, it holds us.

Steadfast: Paul could not be turned by prison or martyrdom because he had seen the heavenly vision.

How is knowing God as "the One who is" deeper than knowing Him as almighty?When nothing seems to happen.

Beyond Acts: To us He may not appear able or mighty in a given situation, yet He still is. Faith rests on His being, not on His deeds.

Experience: In poverty or turmoil, everything outward may change; God does not.

Where do you need to let others do what you are able to do?The safeguard of matching.

Restriction: Aaron was not more capable than Moses, yet God arranged that Aaron speak. Being restricted humbles us.

Protection: The more we are matched with others, the more our spiritual life is safeguarded.
