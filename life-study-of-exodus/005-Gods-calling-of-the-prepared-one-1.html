Exodus · Message 5

God's Calling of the Prepared One (1)

The calling of Moses is the fullest record of God's calling in the Bible and the pattern for all His servants. God waited eighty years for one prepared vessel, then called him at the back side of the desert, from a burning thorn-bush, as the great I Am.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Motivation: Israel's cry. God saw their affliction, heard their cry, and knew their sorrows ().
  • The Time: God was able and eager, yet waited about eighty years. The delay was on man's side: there was no prepared one. The Lord's return waits in the same way for a ready people.
  • The Place: The back side of the desert, the mountain of God, holy soil, the midst of a thorn-bush, a flame of fire.
  • The Calling One: The Angel of Jehovah, Jehovah, God, the God of the fathers, I Am, and the God of the Hebrews.
  • The Standard Servant: Moses was the first fully perfected servant of God, so his calling is the model for ours.

God's Patience and Our Readiness

Moses at forty was educated and "mighty in words and deeds" (), but still natural. God set him aside until he had lost confidence, future, and goal, and was content to tend another man's flock. Then, at the right time, God came.

The Problem Is Never on God's Side

The people's cry was desperate, God's heart was eager, but Moses' growth was slow. Instead of complaining about the situation, give yourself to grow in life so that the Lord has prepared ones to come to.

The Place of the Calling

Outwardly it was a remote corner of the wilderness; in reality it was God's mountain. Each layer brings us closer to the One who called.

The Calling One

The one called must know the name of the One who calls. Exodus 3 unveils God's name more fully than almost any other portion.

Add What You Need

"I Am" is an unfinished sentence: tired, He is rest; dying, He is life (). Not that everything is God, but God is the reality of every positive thing.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did God wait eighty years to deliver Israel?Not lack of power.

No Vessel: God was able and willing, but there was no one prepared. At forty Moses was capable but natural.

Today: The Lord's return is delayed for the same reason. The answer to a slow situation is not complaint but growth in life.

What is the difference between a genuine calling and a religious "calling"?Consider the place.

Religious: Consecration, Bible school, ordination after graduation. Such a process means nothing in God's eyes by itself.

Genuine: Meeting God at the back side of our life, on holy soil free of human interference, with Him speaking from within us as a holy fire.

Why must the calling One be the Angel of Jehovah?Only the Sent can send.

Qualified by Experience: Christ is God's sent One, yet He is Jehovah Himself. Having been sent, He knows how to send others.

Our Commission: "As the Father has sent Me, I also send you" ().

What does it mean to you that God's name is "I Am"?He is; we are not.

Our Nothingness: Only He has the reality of being (). Apart from Him we have no reality.

Our Supply: Before Abraham was, He is (). Whatever we lack, He is. The One who calls is the One who sustains a lifetime of service.
