Exodus · Message 23

The Passover (1)

Exodus 12 is the fullest type of Christ's redemption in the Bible. Christ is not only the lamb but the whole Passover, including the house in which we are covered.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Christ Our Passover: Not merely our lamb; He is the lamb, the bread, the herbs, and the house ().
  • In Christ, Not Under the Blood: The New Testament says we are in Christ (); redemption is based on union with Him.
  • A New Calendar: The Passover month became the first month (); our sacred age begins at our new birth.
  • A Lamb for a House: The unit of salvation is the household, and the lamb is never too small ().
  • Unbroken Bones: Christ's legs were not broken (), signifying His indestructible, life-imparting life.

From Skins to the House

Redemption is revealed progressively, then fully developed in Exodus 12. The ark becomes the house, a type of Christ, covering those who eat within it.

The Time and the Lamb

The month Abib means sprouting, the budding of a new life. The lamb was taken on the tenth day and examined until the fourteenth, the end of two weeks, signifying that Christ's death ends our old life. The Lord was tested by priests and rulers, found faultless three times by Pilate, and killed on the very Passover day.

Eating the Flesh

The blood redeems; the flesh supplies life (). How we take Christ matters ().

Raw

An Example

Christ only as a model to imitate.

Boiled

A Martyr

His death as persecution, not redemption.

Roasted

The Redeemer

Judged under the fire of God's holy wrath for us; eaten whole, head, legs, and inwards, with nothing left.

Unleavened bread means purging everything sinful; bitter herbs mean regret and repentance. The life we receive is sensitive to sin.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why does Paul call Christ our Passover rather than our lamb?The whole feast.

All-Inclusive: Every item, the lamb, the bread, the herbs, and the house, points to Christ.

Covering: Before He can be our Passover, He must be our house. We are in Him, and judgment passes over us there.

What does the lamb's sufficiency for every house teach?Exodus 12:4.

Never Too Small: A house can be too small for the lamb, but the lamb is never too small for a house.

Our Capacity: Christ meets every need according to the number of souls and each one's eating.

Are you taking Christ raw, boiled, or roasted?Example, martyr, Redeemer.

Wrong Ways: Imitating Him or admiring His suffering leaves us unredeemed.

God's Way: Receive Him as the One judged by God's fire in our place, and take all of Him.

What does your sacred calendar show?The month Abib.

New Beginning: A believer has two births and two ages. The spiritual one began the day we believed.

Budding Life: Since then something within has sprouted and keeps producing.
