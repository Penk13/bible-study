Exodus · Message 38

The Heavenly Diet — Manna (4)

An omer of manna was kept in a golden pot inside the ark, the hidden center of God's dwelling. The Christ we eat, digest, and assimilate becomes our center, an eternal memorial, and the hidden manna that makes us functioning priests.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Focal Point: Beneath five layers (court, tabernacle, Holy of Holies, ark, pot) lay the manna. The center of God's building today is the Christ eaten by His people.
  • One Omer: Whether greedy or lazy, each gathered and ate one omer, and one omer was preserved: we keep only as much Christ as we eat ().
  • A Memorial: The Christ who constitutes us is the only thing worth remembering in eternity, and He is the supply for God's dwelling.
  • Hidden Manna: Manna not eaten stays open; manna eaten becomes hidden within our spirit, the Holy of Holies ().
  • The Priestly Tenth: An omer is a tenth of an ephah, the priests' portion. Eating Christ moves us from the congregation into the functioning priesthood.

Manna at the Center

Nowhere else did God ask His people to keep food before Him. The golden pot of manna sat in the ark with the tables of the covenant (). As the church, we are God's tabernacle today, and our center should be the Christ we have eaten.

Living One Day at a Time

Manna kept overnight bred worms and stank. Natural saving for tomorrow is not God's way; the Lord tells us not to worry about tomorrow (). Gathering each morning, neither greedy nor lazy, and eating the day's portion gives a life of peace and rest. What God preserves is the very amount we eat.

Memorial

Our Constitution

The Christ we enjoy reconstitutes us; nothing of ourselves is worth remembering.

Memorial

Supply for the Building

Fed with manna, Israel built the tabernacle; Christ supplies us to become God's dwelling.

From Open to Hidden Manna

Manna on the ground was public; the omer in the ark was hidden. In experience, open manna is Christ not yet eaten; whatever we eat becomes hidden within us. Since a tenth part was the priests' portion, the hidden manna is not for the general congregation but for serving priests.

🌾

Open Manna

Known about but not eaten; we remain in the general congregation, without function.

🏺

Hidden Manna

Eaten and assimilated; our special portion, making us priests who function in the church.

No Greed: However much we collect, by God's measure we have what we can eat.

Eat to Preserve: What we preserve of Christ equals what we have actually eaten of Him.

What will we remember in eternity?A memorial.

Christ Enjoyed: Only Christ, who became our constitution, is worthy of remembrance.

Built Together: We will recall how we took Him as supply to become God's dwelling place.

How does eating Christ turn us into priests?The tenth part.

The Priestly Portion: The omer, a tenth, belonged to the priesthood; hidden manna is their portion.

Function: The more Christ we eat, the more we function, moving from the outside into the Holy of Holies.
