Life-Study / Exodus 003

Exodus · Message 3

Preparation of the Savior

In times of crisis God uses the female life, the life dependent on Him. Through four kinds of females Moses was born, raised, and trained. Then forty years in Midian stripped him of his natural strength, so that God Himself could burn in him.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Female Life: In chapter 1 the midwives preserved God's people; in chapter 2 females prepared their savior. The female pictures the life wholly dependent on God, and all believers should be such.
  • Mother Only: Hebrews says his parents hid him (), but Exodus names only the mother (), and leaves the females unnamed, to impress us with the female life.
  • Built Up, Then Torn Down: Forty years made Moses mighty in words and deeds (); forty more made him "slow of speech" ().
  • Raised by Females, Rejected by Males: His brothers asked, "Who made you a prince and a judge over us?" (). The Lord Jesus too was crucified by the male life and appreciated by the female.
  • Received and Perfected: The daughters of Midian received him, and the priest of Midian, a mature male life, perfected him.

Four Kinds of Female Life

Several women gather around one small ark of papyrus, the same Hebrew word as Noah's ark: a vessel preserving life through water. Each has a function the Lord still needs today.

Natural Preparation, Spiritual Preparation

Moses was "well pleasing to God" (), and his parents saw it. But knowledge must not remain raw; it must be processed. Forty signifies trial and testing.

First 40 Years

In Pharaoh's Palace

Trained in all the wisdom of the Egyptians (). Confident, he killed an Egyptian, supposing his brothers would understand ().

Next 40 Years

Shepherding in Midian

God's "graduate school" (). Then the bush that burned but was not consumed (): you are the vessel, I am the fuel.

By Faith

He refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter, chose affliction with God's people over the passing pleasures of sin, and looked to the reward (). Outwardly he feared (); inwardly he endured as seeing the invisible One ().

Rejected by the Brothers, Received by the Heathen

Rejected because he was bold, Moses was received because he helped the weak. When shepherds drove away the seven daughters, he stood up and watered their flock ().

Immature Male Life

The Rejecting Hebrews

Inexperienced; they could not receive the vessel God had raised up.

Female Life

The Daughters of Midian

Received him and spoke well of him, as sisters comfort the persecuted (Peter at Mary's house).

Mature Male Life

The Priest of Midian

Received Moses, gave him Zipporah, and perfected him over forty years.

Meanwhile Israel groaned under bondage; God heard, remembered His covenant, and took knowledge of them (; ). All of chapter 2 was for preparing the savior.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is the "female life," and why must brothers also be females?Dependent versus independent.

Definition: Not gender but the life that stands with God and depends utterly on Him. In crises only this life is useful to Him.

The Danger: Anyone who presumes to be a male, independent of God, becomes a Pharaoh. In the eyes of God all believers should be females.

Was Moses mighty in words or slow of speech?Acts 7:22 and Exodus 4:10.

Both, in Sequence: At forty he was eloquent and bold, but his strength was natural. After forty years of shepherding he knew he had nothing.

The Burning Bush: God does not use our natural eloquence, talent, or energy. We are the bush; He is the fire ().

Why do we need to be rejected as well as raised up?Raw versus processed.

Processing: If you have never been rejected, you are still raw. The stronger and more frank we are, the more we shall be rejected, and this rejection is what our disposition deserves.

The Pattern: Whatever God raises up through the female life is rejected by the immature male life, as Moses was () and as Christ was.

In the church today, which life do you show: the receiving one or the rejecting one?The priest of Midian versus the Hebrew brothers.

Receiving: We need the various female functions to raise something up and the mature male life to perfect what God has raised up. Do not be dissenting, cold, or neutral, but receptive and comforting.

Long Training: Forty years pass quickly. With a heart for the Lord and a standing with His people, we can accept His training however long it lasts.
