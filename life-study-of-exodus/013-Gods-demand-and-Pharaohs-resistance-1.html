Exodus · Message 13

God's Demand and Pharaoh's Resistance (1)

Jehovah, the God of Israel, demands that His people go into the wilderness to feast and sacrifice to Him. Pharaoh, Satan and our usurped self, resists by denying God and increasing the labor.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • God's Title: Jehovah, the self-existing One; the God of Israel, a transformed, victorious, kingly people ().
  • God's Demand: Let My people go to hold a feast and sacrifice to Me, three days' journey into the wilderness ().
  • Pharaoh's Resistance: "Who is the LORD?" (). No straw, same bricks, accused of idleness.
  • The Issue: More suffering, a discouraged Moses (), and a people short of spirit ().

Jehovah, the God of Israel

Though His people were still slaves, God did not call Himself the God of Jacob before Pharaoh. In His eyes they were already Israel, and Hebrews, river-crossers. Do not hold to your feeling about yourself; believe His word.

How We See Ourselves

Jacob

A heel-holder in bondage, pitiful in our condition.

How God Sees Us

Israel

Transformed into victors and kings, already brought into the good land.

A Feast That Is a Sacrifice

The feast stands against slavery and is "to Me" (): God is happy when His people eat and rejoice. What they sacrifice to Him becomes their feast, as with the Passover lamb (). This is dispensational worship, God dispensed into us for our enjoyment ().

A Corporate Matter

No one can feast alone. When we miss a church meeting, we lose the enjoyment, but God loses even more: He has no feast and no sacrifice.

Pharaoh's Tactics, Then and Now

Pharaoh is Satan objectively and our Satan-usurped self subjectively. We can be a Pharaoh to ourselves, to a spouse, to children, or to roommates whenever we keep anyone from feasting to the Lord.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is worship according to God, not natural concept?Feasting, not prostrating.

Natural: Bowing and kneeling before God.

Divine: Enjoying God as our provision and resting in what we enjoy. The more we drink of the Spirit, the more worship the Father receives.

In what ways are you a Pharaoh to yourself?The subjective Pharaoh.

Excuses: Too tired for the meeting, yet energetic for the telephone. The self, usurped by Satan, denies the Lord's demand.

Others: Parents who press study over every meeting, or brothers who hold others back, act as Pharaohs too.

What kind of "idleness" does God want?Existence versus purpose.

Limited Existence: Anything beyond our need becomes Egyptian. Even good cleaning becomes worldly when it crowds out the feast.

Proper Idleness: At times we must tell Satan, "Enough," and spend time feasting with the saints.

Why did more suffering follow God's demand?A positive sign.

Negotiation Underway: Increased suffering shows God is dealing with the enemy on our behalf.

Our Need: God reconfirmed His name and covenant to Moses. We must pray never to be short of spirit.
