Life-Study / Exodus 007

Exodus · Message 7

The Corporate Thorn-Bush

The burning bush is not only Moses but all God's people. Through redemption the fire that once shut fallen man out now dwells in the cursed thorn, and the God of resurrection makes a corporate thorn-bush His dwelling place, the church today.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Him Who Dwelt in the Bush: At the end of his life Moses called God the One who dwelt in the bush (). The bush was God's house.
  • Genesis 3 to Exodus 3: The thorn of the curse and the excluding flame become one: the fire now visits and indwells the bush.
  • Twofold Consciousness: A man of God knows he is still a thorn-bush, and knows God's glory burns within him.
  • Speak as God Sees: God beheld no iniquity in Jacob (). Speaking well of the church brings blessing; speaking ill brings a curse.
  • In Resurrection: The God at the bush is the God of the living (), so His dwelling is altogether in resurrection.

From Excluding Fire to Indwelling Fire

Sin brought the curse, signified by thorns (), and the curse brought the flaming sword (). Had the Bible ended there, we would be hopeless. But Christ became a curse for us () so that we might receive the Spirit (), the fire He came to cast on the earth ().

Genesis 3

Thorn and Flame Apart

Cursed man is kept away from God as the tree of life.

Exodus 3

Thorn and Flame One

Through redemption, the holy fire visits, stays, and dwells in the once-cursed bush.

Individual and Corporate

Both Moses and Israel went through the same process. We are not just "sinners saved by grace"; we are a thorn-bush burning with God's glory.

Transformed Without Changing

The bush in Exodus 3 stayed a bush, yet the fire burned within it. The church still has thorns, perhaps more than others, but the mark of the Lord's recovery is that this bush is burning.

Do Not See Deeper Than God

Hired to curse, Balaam could only bless: "How lovely are your tents, O Jacob" (). In a Shanghai prayer meeting Brother Nee used this word to praise the Lord that the church is never low. To call the church dead or poor is to repeat the enemy's lie and to fall under a curse.

Natural Sight

Defects

Quick to spot shortcomings, especially in the leading ones.

God's Sight

His Dwelling Place

"I find no fault in them; I dwell among them." In eternity His word will prove right.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How did the fire of Genesis 3 become the fire of Exodus 3?Redemption in between.

The Curse Removed: Christ became a curse on the cross, foreshadowed by Abel's lamb.

The Fire Given: The Spirit came as tongues of fire. The holiness that excluded sinners now visits and dwells in them.

What is the "twofold consciousness" of a man of God?Bush and fire together.

The Thorns: Even our natural virtues, kindness and patience, are still thorns. Do not think too highly of yourself or of those you admire.

The Glory: Yet God's fire burns within, transforming us into men of God.

Why does speaking negatively about the church bring a curse?Whose word is true?

God's Estimation: He does not behold iniquity in His people. To claim to see deeper than He does is to side with the accuser.

Experience: Words against the church cost us our peace; declaring the church lovely keeps us under blessing.

Do you dare to declare that you are in resurrection?The God of the living.

The Ground: The God in the bush is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, the God of resurrection ().

The Practice: In ourselves we are in the flesh; in Him we are in resurrection. Say it in faith, and it becomes our experience.
