Exodus · Message 11

The Purpose of God's Calling

God calls us, as He called Moses and Paul, not merely to get people out of the world, but to bring them all the way into Christ as the good land, for God's kingdom and God's dwelling.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Called Like Paul: Paul was one member of the Body; what he was called to, every member is called to.
  • Out of Tyranny: Pharaoh typifies Satan, the ruler of this world (); the world is not enjoyment but tyranny.
  • Wilderness and Mountain: Separation in resurrection, then an elevated place to see God's economy and the tabernacle ().
  • Into the Good Land: The ultimate goal is Christ in His all-inclusiveness (), for the kingdom and the temple.
  • Living by Christ: For a believer, not to live by Christ is sin, even if behavior is good.

Five Aspects of the Purpose

Preaching is easy; bringing people out of Satan's hand and on to the goal requires the impact of a called one.

Three Stages of Enjoying Christ

Most Christians know the Passover and the manna, but few know the good land as a type of Christ. Canaan is not heaven; heaven has no enemies to defeat. When Israel entered the land, the manna ceased and they ate its produce ().

In Egypt

The Lamb

Small, for escaping judgment and leaving the world.

In the Wilderness

Manna & Water

Daily supply for the journey and the tabernacle.

In Canaan

The Land's Produce

Spacious and rich; strength to defeat the powers in the heavenlies.

The Greatest Lack: Living Christ

We can sing that Christ lives in us (; ) yet live by ourselves. Seeking holiness, victory, or spirituality instead of Christ Himself hinders His being lived out.

The Unbeliever

Not Believing

The unique sin is not to believe in the Son ().

The Believer

Not Living Christ

Good behavior without Christ is still rebellion against God's economy. Without Christ, the church is a social organization.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why should we not regard Moses and Paul as far above us?Members of one Body.

Same Calling: God's call of us is the same in principle. Paul was a representative member; what he did, all members can be perfected to do.

Type and Reality: Moses had it in type, Paul in reality, and both should be our experience.

What forms of Pharaoh's tyranny hold you today?The world as bondage.

Subtle Slavery: The freeway, fear of losing a job, fashion, even the newspaper can take the time we would spend with the Lord.

Wilderness: A proper salvation brings us out to a place where there is no Egyptian element.

Why is bringing people into the good land harder than bringing them out of Egypt?The lamb versus the land.

Depth of Christ: The lamb is small; the land is vast, with hills, valleys, wheat, barley, and minerals. Few experience Christ in this way.

Warfare: In the land the enemies are the principalities and powers, which must be defeated to enjoy Christ.

Have you ever repented for not living by Christ?Beyond good behavior.

A New Confession: We regret losing our temper, but rarely regret living a whole day by ourselves. God wants Christ, not our zeal or ethics.

Pray: "Lord, forgive me for living by my natural life. Be my person and my life today."
