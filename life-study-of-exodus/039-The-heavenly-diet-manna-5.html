Exodus · Message 39

The Heavenly Diet — Manna (5)

The Christ we eat is preserved in the divine nature (the golden pot), in Christ (the ark), in our spirit (the Holy of Holies). Placed before the testimony, this hidden manna answers the inner law of life and makes us overcomers and stones for God's building.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Golden Pot: Gold is the divine nature we partake of (); only it, not our mind or emotion, can preserve the Christ we enjoy.
  • In the Ark, in the Spirit: The divine nature is in Christ, and Christ is in our spirit, where God dwells as the church ().
  • Before the Testimony: The pot sat before the tablets of the law, so the hidden manna matches the inner law of life and fulfills its demands, bringing peace ().
  • For Overcomers: The hidden manna constitutes priests and overcomers and transforms them into white stones for God's dwelling ().
  • Miracle upon Miracle: Kept manna rotted, the Sabbath portion and the pot's omer did not; the measure is God's, not ours.

Where the Hidden Manna Is

Objective teachers make the Holy of Holies heaven, placing the hidden manna far away. Read subjectively, the picture maps our experience: our life is hidden with Christ in God (), and what we enjoy of Him is kept within us as an eternal memorial.

Manna Answering the Testimony

The "Testimony" before which the pot was laid () is not the ark but the tablets inside it, since Hebrews places the pot in the ark. The law testifies that God is holy, righteous, light, and love. Within us we have this testimony as the law of life (), and our natural life cannot meet it. The Christ we eat and assimilate does.

Food, Testimony, Correspondence

The inner law demands; the hidden manna answers. With both in the church as God's tabernacle, inward strife ends in peace, our service is accepted, and God speaks.

Regulated by God

Nothing about manna fits natural concepts. Manna stored by greed bred worms, yet the double Sabbath portion stayed fresh and the omer in the pot neither spoiled nor melted. Whether we gather more or less, God measures us to one omer; we must not be lazy, yet we cannot adjust ourselves. Even our hunger for Christ, when others with the same teaching have none, is a miracle of mercy.

🐛

Our Way

Hoarding for tomorrow or trying to balance ourselves: religious effort that spoils.

🤍

God's Way

Gathering daily by His word; what He preserves lasts forever and builds us as white stones.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can only the divine nature preserve Christ in us?A golden pot.

Not the Soul: Memory and feeling cannot hold Christ; they change.

Regenerated Nature: The divine nature received at regeneration is the gold that keeps Him forever.

How does the hidden manna relate to the law of life?Before the testimony.

Demand and Supply: The inner law requires holiness, righteousness, light, and love; nothing natural meets it.

Peace: The assimilated Christ corresponds to that law, so we are no longer torn within.

Why read the tabernacle subjectively?Heaven or our spirit?

Objective Only: Making the Holy of Holies heaven keeps the manna remote.

In Experience: Seeing our spirit as the Holy of Holies shows us where Christ is enjoyed and kept today.

If God sets the measure, what is our part?Neither greedy nor lazy.

Gather: We still go out each morning; without that, there is no mercy to claim.

Leave the Rest: We stop trying to adjust ourselves and let the heavenly measure balance us.
