Exodus · Message 24

The Passover (2)

Applying the Passover means eating as an army ready to march, entering the house through the blood by a little faith, and remaining in the house, abiding in Christ, until we are filled and equipped.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • A Memorial: The detailed record is so that we would remember Christ's redemption specifically, not vaguely ().
  • Eat as an Army: Loins girded, shoes on, staff in hand, and in haste ().
  • Hyssop and Basin: The blood is applied by a small faith from a small vessel; applying Christ is easy ().
  • Stay in the House: No one was to go out until morning; we must abide in Christ ().
  • Feasting Equips: Eating the whole lamb in the house filled them and made them ready to march out of Egypt.

Eating Like Soldiers

Israel came out of Egypt by their armies. The gospel often stops at believing, but Exodus 12 adds preparation for a journey of war. Many of us sensed at our salvation that a new journey had begun.

Girded Loins

No Longer Loose

Our being is gathered up, ready to fight.

Shoes & Staff

Ready to Travel

Equipped for a long journey out of Egypt.

In Haste

Battle Ahead

Soldiers eat quickly; the slow are the first attacked from the rear.

The Blood on the Entrance

The blood was put on the door, not the roof. The same blood opens the way into Christ as the house and shuts out the destroyer (). Hyssop, among the smallest of plants, signifies faith; even a little faith applies the blood ().

🐑

The Lamb

The means of redemption; whatever He redeems He keeps.

🏠

The House

The means of preservation; we are in Christ by God ().

Remaining in the House

Redemption rests on identification. Like those in Noah's ark, we share the destiny of the One we are in. Many entered the house at conversion but stepped out by trying to please God in their own energy, like the Galatians. Apart from Him we can do nothing (). The house is wherever the standing of the redeeming blood is kept.

Not Only Passed Over

The blood ushers us in and keeps judgment away, but it does not equip us. Feasting on the whole lamb inside the house does, and while we feast, God enjoys it even more than we do.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What is missing from a gospel that stops at “believe and be saved”?Girdle, shoes, staff.

An Army: God redeems a people to march out and fight, not to be carried to heaven in a palanquin.

Preparation: Being girded, shod, and holding a staff belong to applying the Passover properly.

Why are hyssop and a basin encouraging?Small and easy.

Little Faith: God does not ask for faith like a cedar. A simple “Lord, thank You” applies the blood.

Whole Passover: Through that small faith we enter the house, and everything in it becomes ours.

How can a believer come out of the house without realizing it?The Galatians.

Self-Effort: Endeavoring in natural energy to please God leaves the standing of redemption and puts us outside Christ in experience.

Way Back: Return through the blood-sprinkled door, confess that we are nothing and He is everything, and abide.

How does feasting on Christ equip you for the journey?Filled with the lamb.

Filled: The more we eat every part of Christ, the more we are qualified and formed into an army.

His Joy: Our feasting is a feast to the Lord; He is satisfied, and the enemy is defeated.
