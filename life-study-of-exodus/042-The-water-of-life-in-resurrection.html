Exodus · Message 42

The Water of Life in Resurrection

The water from the smitten rock is spiritual and flows in resurrection. It is the Triune God, who passed through incarnation, human living, and death, flowing out as the life-giving Spirit for us to drink.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Spiritual and Mysterious: Paul calls both the drink and the following rock spiritual (); they can be enjoyed but not fully explained.
  • Water in Resurrection: A rock implies incarnation, human living, and death; the water flowed only after these, once Jesus was glorified ().
  • Resurrection Is God: "I am the resurrection" was spoken by the Word who is God (); He entered death to prove it.
  • One Flow: Rock, pierced side, and throne show one water, flowing from the throne from eternity through the manger, the cross, and resurrection.
  • The Triune God Flowing: Father as source, Son as course, Spirit as flow (), for the Body and the bride.

What Resurrection Means

Resurrection is life coming forth from what has passed through death. Christ's resurrection was not only leaving the tomb but the issue of thirty-three and a half years in which no hardship could hold Him down. Because the water is in resurrection, it is triumphant; drinking it lifts us out of our natural condition and over every negative thing. He offered this living water even to the Samaritan woman ().

Three Pictures, One Water

In biblical order the rock comes first, then the body of Jesus, then the throne. In reality the throne is first: the river proceeds from it (), and the Bible is the story of God flowing. The water of life is the Spirit, the Spirit is the Triune God, and the Triune God is the water, best known by drinking rather than debate.

From the Throne

Authority and Riches

Drinking subdues us and brings the riches of the tree of life growing in the river.

Through the Smitten Christ

His Humanity

We taste His human living and death only by this inner flow.

In Resurrection

Body and Bride

With power, ascension, and enthronement; forming one Body and preparing the bride who calls, "Come" ().

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why did God bring water out of a rock rather than the ground?What a rock implies.

Christ Processed: For God to be a rock implies incarnation and human living; for it to be smitten implies death.

Water After Death: The flow comes only from One who has passed through death into resurrection.

How can resurrection be God Himself?Resurrection before life.

The Speaker: The One saying "I am the resurrection" is the Word who was God.

Proved by Death: He became man to enter death and show that nothing can hold Him.

What does drinking water "in resurrection" do for us in hardships?Triumphant water.

Its Ingredients: The water carries His incarnation, human living, and death, working inwardly.

Its Effect: The more we drink, the more we transcend our natural state and difficulties.

Why is enjoying the Triune God better than analyzing Him?Drink, don't debate.

Loss by Analysis: Debating the Trinity deprives us of the supply available to drink.

Gain by Drinking: Daily enjoyment of the Father, Son, and Spirit sustains us and builds the Body.
