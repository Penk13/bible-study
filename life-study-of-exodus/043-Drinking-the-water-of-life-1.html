Exodus · Message 43

Drinking the Water of Life (1)

Eating and drinking are God's way of working Himself into us. They are facts, not metaphors, and the Bible's final call puts drinking first, so we need not only to drink but to know how to drink.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Beginning and End: The Bible opens with the tree of life and a river () and closes with the water of life and the tree of life.
  • God Taken In: By eating and drinking, God becomes one with us organically, as food becomes our very tissue ().
  • Facts, Not Figures: Paul does not say the rock typified Christ; he says the rock was Christ ().
  • Drinking First: The last call is to the thirsty, and the tree grows in the river ().
  • Two Ministries: Paul's completing ministry and John's mending ministry both stress eating and drinking.

Facts, Not Metaphors

Many Christians, including the speaker in his early years, preached about living water without knowing how to drink it. When he began speaking in 1958 on eating Jesus, a professor called it crude; John 6:57 answered him. Calling these words mere metaphors weakens them. Christ came from heaven as real food, and whoever thirsts may come to Him and drink ().

📖

Only a Figure

Eating and drinking Christ treated as a parable; the rock merely a symbol.

💧

A Divine Fact

Christ is truly our food and drink, taken in daily, even hourly, and assimilated into our being.

Why Drinking Comes First

The Bible's early order is food then water: Genesis 2, Exodus 16–17, Revelation 2:7 before 7:17. Its final word reverses this. The river flows from the throne and carries the tree, so eating is included in drinking. Physically too we survive longer without food than without water. Even the psalmist drank of God's river of pleasures (), and the Lord promises the fountain of the water of life to the thirsty ().

The Science of Drinking

Past saints drank as people once took vitamins without knowing of them. We need to drink knowingly and purposely.

Completing and Mending

Paul was commissioned to complete the word of God (); John, writing later, mended what had been broken, especially faith in Christ's deity. Both emphasize eating and drinking.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why are eating and drinking so crucial in God's relationship with man?Organic union.

God's Intention: He wants to work Himself into us as our life and everything.

His Way: What we eat and drink becomes one with us in substance; so does God when we take Him in.

What is lost when we call eating and drinking Christ a metaphor?The rock was Christ.

Weakened Word: Turning a fact into a figure strips Paul's and the Lord's words of their force.

No Practice: A metaphor can be admired; only a fact can be eaten and drunk daily.

Why does the Bible's last word put drinking before eating?River carries tree.

More Basic: The tree grows in the river; the water conveys the food.

Decisive: The concluding call of Scripture is to the thirsty.

Is it enough to drink without knowing how?Vitamins.

Unknowing Drinking: Earlier believers did drink, as people took vitamins before they were discovered.

Knowing Drinking: Learning the way lets us drink purposely and consistently, as proper Christians.
