Exodus · Message 8

The Three Signs

The burning bush was an objective sign; the three signs of Exodus 4 are subjective. A rod that becomes a serpent, a hand that becomes leprous, and water that becomes blood show that a sent one must know how to deal with Satan, the flesh, and the world.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Why the Signs: God's calling was complete on His side, but Moses still feared no one would believe him (). The signs are proof of a genuine sent one.
  • The Serpent: Whatever we lean on is a rod, and Satan hides in it. Cast it down, then take it up by the tail.
  • Leprosy: Our bosom, our flesh, holds nothing good (); we are cleansed by keeping the Lord's word.
  • Blood: The water of the Nile, the world's supply and enjoyment, is death once poured out on the land.
  • Against the Triune God: Satan opposes the Son, the flesh opposes the Spirit, and the world opposes the Father.

Three Signs, Three Enemies

Three is the number of the Triune God's economy. These signs expose the three things that frustrate it.

Taking the Serpent by the Tail

Moses' rod was his support. God did not tell him to throw it away but to cast it down so its true nature would show (). Job, spouse, education, savings: anything we lean on can hide the usurper.

Leprosy and Blood

Moses may have expected a treasure in his bosom; he drew out a leprous hand (). Like Isaiah, anyone who meets the Lord sees his own uncleanness (). At the Lord's word Moses put his hand back, and it was restored ().

✋

The Flesh Exposed

Whoever boasts of his goodness is not a called one. Obeying the Lord's word brings His cleansing.

🩸

The World Exposed

Kept in a vessel, the Nile's water looks like supply. Poured on the dry land, it is blood. Lead people out to drink from the cleft rock instead.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the burning bush not enough proof that someone is sent?Objective versus subjective.

Objective Vision: Moses saw the bush and heard God, yet was still not moved.

Subjective Evidence: A sent one must know by experience how to deal with Satan, the flesh, and the world. Without these, a claim to be sent should not be believed.

What does it mean to take something "by the tail"?Not thrown away, not held as before.

Two Easy Ways: Keep the thing in the common way, or give it up altogether.

God's Way: Hold it for the Lord and by grace. The serpent then hangs limp, and the rod becomes authority. Brothers who let their wives become the head lost their usefulness.

How is a leprous hand cleansed?Back into the bosom, at His word.

The Root of Sin: Disobedience is the basic element of sin.

The Cure: Keeping the Lord's word brings His cleansing power with it.

What "rod" or "Nile water" in your life needs to be exposed?Cast it down; pour it out.

The Rod: Ask what you lean on besides the Lord: a job, a person, a bank account. Take your hands off and see what comes out.

The Water: Sports and entertainment can be enjoyment in the world's eyes and death in God's. Pray, "Lord, let me see the world as You see it."
