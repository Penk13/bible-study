Exodus · Message 40

The Living Water out of the Smitten Rock (1)

After manna comes living water. Christ, the rock God prepared, was smitten by the authority of God's law on the cross, and the Spirit flowed out as the ultimate issue of the Triune God for us to drink.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Manna, Then Water: Exodus 16–17 and John 6–7 share the same sovereign order: the word first, then the Spirit.
  • Led to a Dry Place: Following the pillar, Israel came to a place without water, where a rock God had prepared was waiting.
  • Drinking Is Vital: Thirst kills sooner than hunger, and water is needed to digest food; the word is digested by the Spirit.
  • Smitten by the Law: Moses' rod is the authority of God's law; the cleft rock is Christ judged on the cross, His side pierced ().
  • The Following Rock: The rock was Christ and it followed Israel from Horeb to Kadesh ().

The Tree and the River

Eden had the tree of life and a river; Revelation shows the tree growing in the river (). At the start of salvation we receive the word and then the Spirit flows, as the fatness of God's house comes before the river of His pleasures (). As we go on, the flowing Spirit brings us the word.

Beginning

Word, Then Spirit

Tree before river (Genesis 2); manna before water (Exodus 16–17).

Ongoing

Spirit Bringing the Word

The tree of life grows in the river of water of life (Revelation 22).

Seven Experiences, Then Water

Israel journeyed at the Lord's command, silently given by the pillar of cloud and fire. In a short time they had passed through seven basic experiences, much like our own spiritual history. Yet even after enjoying manna they still needed to drink.

The Rock Smitten

Grinding manna hints at Christ's death; cleaving a great rock portrays it fully. During the last three hours on the cross, Christ was smitten not by men but by God's righteous law. Scripture also calls God the rock who begot us, our salvation, strength, refuge, and shade in a weary land. When this rock was cleft, living water came forth, the Spirit whom believers would receive ().

The Bible's Own Interpretation

Rock = Christ; rod of Moses = the law's authority; smiting = the crucifixion; flowing water = the Spirit, the ultimate issue of the Triune God.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why do we still need water after eating manna?Digestion.

More Urgent: Thirst is more serious than hunger; we cannot live without drinking.

Assimilating the Word: The word received needs the Spirit to be digested into life.

How does the order of word and Spirit change as we grow?Tree and river.

At First: We hear the word, and then the Spirit begins to flow.

Later: The flowing Spirit itself supplies us with the word, as the tree grows in the river.

What does it mean that Christ was smitten by the rod of Moses?The law's authority.

God's Judgment: In God's eyes, Christ was put to death by the power of His righteous law, not merely by the Jews.

Released Life: That smiting opened the way for the Spirit to flow to us.

How can we follow the Lord's leading as Israel followed the pillar?Silent command.

Not Our Choice: Israel moved only when the pillar moved, without knowing the destination.

Into Supply: Even a dry place they were led to was where the rock was waiting.
