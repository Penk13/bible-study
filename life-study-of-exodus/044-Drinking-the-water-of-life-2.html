Exodus · Message 44

Drinking the Water of Life (2)

The Bible tells us to drink but hides the way. The secret is to call on the name of the Lord: our calling is our drinking, and drinking God in spirit is the real worship He seeks.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Positioned: Baptism has brought us to the fountain; we were all given to drink one Spirit ().
  • Thirst and Coming: We need a thirst for the Lord and must come to Him again and again ().
  • Calling Is Drinking: Saying "Lord Jesus" puts us in the Spirit (), and so we drink.
  • Receiving by Faith: The more we call, the more we believe, receive, and take the water freely ().
  • Drinking Is Worship: God wants to be drunk, not merely revered as a distant object ().

The Way to Drink

As with believing, Scripture commands drinking without spelling out the method; we learn to drink by drinking. Yet after decades of seeking, the speaker presents a simple sequence for believers. Each step leads to the next, and calling is where the water actually flows in.

Calling Anytime, Anywhere

To call in the biblical sense is to cry out and invoke a person audibly by name; "on" adds the thought of looking to Him in prayer. Unceasing calling makes unceasing prayer possible (). Calling needs no proper place, dress, or posture. In anger, sorrow, burden, or an argument, calling brings in the living water; refusing to call brings dryness and death.

🎩

Formal Reverence

Waiting for the right time and place; like an ambassador so busy with table manners he never enjoys the meal.

💧

Spontaneous Drinking

Calling "Lord Jesus" while dressing or driving; like a child who simply eats and enjoys.

Drinking as True Worship

God's kernel intention is to work Himself into us as life, and He does so as we eat and drink Him. So worship is not prostrating before a distant object, even in spirit, but drinking Him in our spirit. At the Lord's table, too, we remember and worship Him by eating and drinking.

Where Was God?

While priests worshipped formally in the temple, God was at a Samaritan well, receiving real worship from a woman who drank Him as living water.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is calling on the Lord the practical secret of drinking?1 Cor. 12:3, 13.

Same Chapter: We were made to drink one Spirit, and no one says "Lord Jesus" except in the Spirit.

Experience: To call from the spirit is to be in the Spirit touching the Lord; the calling itself is the drinking.

How does calling help us pray without ceasing?Not a long prayer.

Possible: We cannot keep up formal prayer all day, but we can keep calling His name in any situation.

Practical: Waking, washing, driving, or under a burden, calling keeps us drinking and looking to Him.

How can religious reverence keep us from drinking?Table manners.

Forms Kill: Care for posture, place, and solemnity can replace the actual taking in of the Lord.

His Desire: He would rather be drunk than formally revered; drinking Him is how we honor Him.

What happens when we stop calling because of an opinion or offense?Dryness.

Cut Off: Argumentative or offended ones seldom call, so there is no drinking and no supply.

Remedy: The Lord has not offended us; calling Him in that very moment brings the living water and dissolves opinions.

What is the worship God seeks?John 4.

Not an Object: He does not want merely to be bowed to as the transcendent One.

Drunk in Spirit: The processed God as the life-giving Spirit is worshipped when we drink Him with our spirit, as the Samaritan woman did.
