Exodus · Message 12

The Three Stations in Fulfilling God's Purpose

Exodus 3 directs God's people to three stations: the wilderness, the mountain, and the good land. The first two are temporary; the goal is Christ as the good land, entered through Christ with the church life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • The Wilderness: Here a place of separation, not of trial, reached through the Passover and the Red Sea ().
  • The Mountain: Revelation of what God is and of the tabernacle, reached by Christ as our supply.
  • The Good Land: Entered by the ark with the tabernacle, through burial in the Jordan and circumcision.
  • Not Too Long: Israel stayed about nine months at the mountain; after it, the wilderness became a place of testing.
  • The Land Within: The good land is the processed Triune God as the all-inclusive Spirit in our spirit ().

Three Stations and Their Factors

The whole history of Israel is a portrait of a believer's full salvation, not only its beginning.

Two Waters, Two Dealings

Worldliness and sin are dealt with at the first two stations. What keeps us from the all-inclusive Christ is the self and the flesh. The ark went into the Jordan first, and twelve stones were buried there (); then came circumcision.

The Red Sea

The World Buried

Pharaoh's army drowned; we are separated from the world.

The Jordan

The Self Buried

The old self laid down, the flesh cut off; then the manna ceased and the land's riches began.

Walking in Christ as the Land

Only the land's produce strengthened Israel to fight the Canaanites, who typify the powers in the air. When we deal with the self and the flesh, those powers come out to war, and we fight supplied by Christ.

The Consummate Commandment

"As you therefore have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him" (). Walk by the Spirit (; ). Not walking in Christ is a believer's primary sin.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why is the wilderness positive in Exodus 3 but negative later?Before and after the mountain.

Before: It is simply separation from Egypt, where God's people can sacrifice to Him.

After: Because of unbelief, it became a place of test and trial. Only Joshua and Caleb entered the land.

How is the tabernacle different from the temple?Two stages of the church life.

Tabernacle: Movable, of wood, with no foundation. A temporary dwelling, good but not final.

Temple: Solid, built of stones () in the land. It comes by walking in Christ as the good land.

Why is the self, not the world, the main hindrance at this stage?The Jordan.

Exposed: We may proclaim "To me to live is Christ" in the meeting, yet live in the self afterward.

The Factor: Christ with the church life goes in first, so that the self can be buried and the flesh cut off.

Where is the good land for you today?Not far away.

Within: The all-inclusive Spirit dwells in our spirit. We need not wander for years to reach it.

Like Caleb: Declare that we are well able to possess it (), and turn to the spirit to walk in Him.
