Life-Study / Exodus 004

Exodus · Message 4

The Life Useful to God

To build God's dwelling and fight for His interests, God uses only the "female" life: the life that depends on Him. A "male" in this sense is one who is independent, and independence of God is rebellion. God alone is the unique Husband; all His people, brothers and sisters, are His wife.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Building and Fighting: Exodus ends with the tabernacle, and Israel came out as an army. To be useful to God is to build His dwelling and fight for His interests.
  • The Unique Male: "Your Maker is your husband" (). Married brothers are husbands in shadow; before the Lord they too are females.
  • Life and Knowledge: The tree of life makes us dependent; the tree of knowledge makes us independent (). Apart from the vine we can do nothing ().
  • Moses Trained: An independent male at forty, he learned to be a female in Midian, but once acted as a male, striking the rock, and lost the good land ().
  • Only Dependent Life Perfects: Independent workers produce independent people, and the end is Babylon, not the New Jerusalem.

The Order of Headship

Christ is not the head of every man in Himself, but under God's headship; man is head of the woman only as he is under Christ (). The Son did nothing from Himself (; ), yet He corrected Peter whenever Peter acted as the head.

God's People as a Female

From the Old Testament to eternity, God's people are His spouse. Even King David was part of the corporate wife. Whenever Israel rebelled, it took the husband's place.

Moses, Luther, Paul

Moses never assumed he was the leader; when Israel rebelled he took it as rebellion against God and brought it to Him. Even the tabernacle was not his idea, but built according to the pattern shown on the mountain ().

The "Male" Life

Independent of God

Moses at forty striking the Egyptian. Even preaching or helping the churches can be rebellion if done apart from Him.

The "Female" Life

Dependent on God

Paul "in weakness, in fear, and in much trembling" (), afraid to do anything in himself.

No Graduation from Life

We never graduate from eating, drinking, and breathing. God puts us where we must depend on Him. The issue is not how much we can do, but how much we depend on Him.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

What does "male" and "female" mean in this message, beyond gender?Independence and dependence.

Male: Headship and an independent life. In the positive sense this belongs only to God; to claim it is blasphemy.

Female: The life that depends on God and does nothing apart from Him. A woman who lives independently becomes a real "male"; a man who depends on God is a real "female."

How can Christ be both dependent and the Head?Look upward and downward.

Upward: The Son can do nothing from Himself ().

Downward: He is Head of every man and dealt strictly with Peter's independence. Married brothers must likewise be under Christ to be heads at home ().

How can good works become rebellion?Not wrong, but independent.

The Source Matters: Preaching, helping saints, or caring for churches done apart from the Lord is independence, and independence is rebellion.

Healthy Fear: We refrain from some things not because they are wrong but because doing them would be independent of God. Every problem in married life and church life comes from this one source.

In your daily living, do you act from the tree of knowledge or the tree of life?Knowledge makes proud; life makes dependent.

Knowledge: Once a child learns something, he no longer depends on you. Experience can make us independent of the Lord too.

Life: Abide in the vine () and pray, "Lord, apart from You I can do nothing. I want to live a female life that always depends on You."
