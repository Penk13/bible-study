Life-Study / Exodus 002

Exodus · Message 2

Israel under Slavery

Genesis ends with a dead man in a coffin in Egypt; Exodus ends with the ark in a tabernacle filled with glory. In between, God's people are enslaved by the world of enjoyment, and Satan kills the life for God's purpose. Yet God is sovereign, and He uses the midwives to save the male life.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Poor End, Glorious End: Genesis closes with Joseph in a coffin in Egypt (); Exodus closes with God's glory filling the tabernacle ().
  • Active in Death: Like those dead in sins who still walk according to the age of this world (), Israel was dead yet laboring, and daily being killed.
  • Enslaved for a Living: Famine drove Israel to Egypt, the world of enjoyment (). People are enslaved mainly by the need to make a living.
  • Kill the Males, Keep the Females: Satan kills the life for God's purpose and keeps the life for man's pleasure (). It is easier to gossip than to pray.
  • God Is Sovereign: The more Israel was afflicted, the more they multiplied (), and God made the God-fearing midwives households ().

Egypt: The World of Enjoyment

The Bible shows at least three aspects of the world. Abraham was called out of one; Israel made their exodus out of another.

🗼

Babel

Rebellion and idolatry, the worship of anything other than God.

🔥

Sodom

The sinful world.

🌾

Egypt

Rich supply and physical pleasure, watered by the Nile; it usurps God's people from His purpose.

Pharaoh's Enslavement

Pharaoh typifies Satan, the ruler of this world (). He dealt "wisely" to usurp, occupy, and enslave ().

In EgyptMeaning Today
Served with rigorNo liberty, no rights, no rest

Pithom, "mouth of integrity"The world boasting of its goodness
Raamses, "thunder of the standard"Every nation proud of its cultural attainment

The Lord Jesus

No boasting, but words of grace (); no thundering, for He would not strive nor cry out ().

The Male Life and the Female Life

Every believer has both lives. Without grace, we become a Pharaoh to ourselves, alive to amusements yet deadened to the Lord's day.

Satan's Way

Eve Used to Deaden

At the beginning of the Old Testament, Satan visited a woman and brought in the fall. Pharaoh tried the midwives the same way.

God's Way

Mary and the Midwives

At the beginning of the New Testament, God visited a virgin to bring forth the Savior (). The midwives feared God and saved the boys.

A principle in history: In high times Satan comes to the weaker vessel to damage the situation; in times of degradation God uses the female life to rescue it. The Hebrew women were "lively" (), and that too was God's sovereignty.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How can people already dead be "daily killed"?Ephesians 2: dead in sins yet walking and doing.

Dead Works: The spiritually dead are not inactive; they walk according to this age and fulfill the desires of the flesh (), but all their works are works of death.

Enslaved, Then Deadened: The world first enslaves people, then deadens them. Even saved ones, outside the church life, can be enslaved again; we must be on the alert.

In what way are you tempted to serve Egypt for your livelihood?Fish, cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic (Num. 11:5).

Usurped: Egypt gave Israel a good living and made them strong, yet kept them from the purpose for which God chose them. Many today serve mammon, earning hard and spending it on pleasure.

Thanksgiving: The Lord has delivered us from the usurping hand of Satan. Our education and work need not be bricks for Pharaoh's treasure cities.

Where today do you kill the male life and keep the female life?Prayer versus gossip; the Lord's day versus amusements.

Pharaoh Within: When the Lord stirs us to pray but we chat instead, we are one with Satan in killing the life for God's purpose ().

Be a Midwife: Refuse to stand with Pharaoh. Turn the life for man's pleasure into the life for God's purpose.

How should God's sovereignty shape the way we labor in the church?Neither trusting our work nor being idle.

Trust His Blessing: The church becomes strong and lively by God's sovereignty, not our skill (). With His blessing even mistakes turn for good; without it, nothing is gained.

Still Do Our Duty: We labor faithfully, knowing the result is His. To those who fear Him, God gives households that produce life for His purpose ().
