Life-Study / Exodus 014

Exodus · Message 14

Jehovah God's Name and His Covenant

To a discouraged Moses God came not as El Shaddai, the promising God, but as Jehovah, the existing and fulfilling God, standing behind a covenant sworn with uplifted hand.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • Name Means Person: God's name is not an empty title but a definition of what He is.
  • Knowing a Name: The patriarchs knew the name Jehovah, but did not know God by it (); they died without the fulfillment ().
  • Promise to Fulfillment: El Shaddai promises; Jehovah, the One who is, fulfills.
  • The Covenant: A promise confirmed by an oath. God lifted His hand to the fathers (); Christ was lifted up to the cross and the throne.
  • The Recovery: "I will build My church" () is covenant, not imagination.

El Shaddai and Jehovah

Four hundred years after the promise to Abraham, the time of fulfillment had come. So God answered Moses' complaint () with "I am Jehovah."

To the Patriarchs

El Shaddai

The mighty, all-sufficient One, like a nourishing breast: the supplying and promising God ().

To Moses

Jehovah

The self-existing One: the existing and fulfilling God (; ).

Jesus: Jehovah the Savior

The Lord Jesus said, "Before Abraham was, I AM" (). His name means Jehovah the Savior (), not El Shaddai the Savior. He came not to promise but to fulfill. Many know His name without experiencing Him by it.

A New Way to Pray

Not only "Lord, You are able," but "Lord, here is a problem, but You are." My troubles will end; He will be forever.

A Covenant Sworn by Uplifting

God did not destroy Pharaoh with one blow; He even hardened his heart. Through the long conflict He trained Moses and Israel to know His name and His covenant.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

How can one know a name and not know God by that name?Doctrine versus experience.

Known Name: Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob used the name Jehovah, just as many today speak of Jesus.

Experienced Person: To know Him by the name is to experience what it means: the Savior who saves, the I Am who fulfills.

Why must the fulfilling God be the existing God?Four thousand years.

Time: A promise to Abraham awaits his resurrection. Only One who always is can keep such a word.

Contrast: I may fail to keep my word because I may cease to be. Jehovah cannot.

Are you living in promise or in fulfillment?El Shaddai or Jehovah.

Short of Experience: We sing of Christ's riches yet live in poverty. Praising His sufficiency is easier than experiencing the I Am.

Settled: One night of taking Him as the fulfilling God can settle a life's direction.

How do His name and covenant meet discouragement?Moses after the first conflict.

His Name: He is, but Pharaoh is not. Opposition may delay, but cannot prevail.

His Covenant: Christ on the throne is the sure sign that what He swore He will bring us into.
