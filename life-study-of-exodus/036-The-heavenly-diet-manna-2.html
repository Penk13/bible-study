Exodus · Message 36

The Heavenly Diet — Manna (2)

Manna came from heaven, with the dew, in the morning. Christ is a heavenly, godly food beyond analysis, received through fresh daily grace and a willingness for a new beginning, not through tradition or doctrine.

Key verses

Rapid recall

  • From Heaven: Manna cannot be analyzed, yet it sustained millions for forty years. Christ is the bread out of heaven and the bread of God ().
  • With the Dew: Quails came by wind; manna came with dew, signifying the Lord's fresh grace each day ().
  • In the Morning: Manna was gathered every morning, giving a daily new beginning ().
  • Not Tradition: Knowing that manna typifies Christ, without gathering it, is a prescription without the medicine ().

Heavenly and Godly Food

No one can say what manna was made of, yet it held everything the body needed. Christ is likewise invisible and beyond analysis, but real and substantial. Since the Roman Empire, rulers have tried to destroy His Body and failed, because a heavenly element within the church sustains it. He is the living bread; whoever eats Him lives forever ().

🌬️

Quails by the Wind

Sent in the evening, from the old creation, to satisfy lust.

💧

Manna with the Dew

Sent in the morning, from heaven, on a base of gentle grace ().

The Dew and the Morning

Dew is softer than rain and warmer than frost. Like the dew of Hermon descending on Zion (), it is grace from the heavens: God reaching us in mercy, new every morning (). A morning watch without dew leaves the Word as black and white letters; where the dew is, the manna is too. And manna comes to those ready for a new start, not to those wanting to relive yesterday.

Beyond Tradition

Many read the Bible daily and gather nothing, using it for ethics or treating it like the classics. The religionists searched the Scriptures yet would not come to Christ for life. Paul received light because he checked his experience with the Word under the Spirit, not under inherited tradition. Even being one spirit with the Lord () can become mere doctrine. The deep point of Exodus 16 is not "don't love the world" but a changed diet.

First Thing in the Morning

Before the day's affairs take over the mind, come to the Lord in the Word, turn to the spirit, and feed on Him.

Reflect

Think each question through first, then open it to compare.

Why can't the church be destroyed?A heavenly element.

Sustained from Above: As manna kept a nation alive in a barren land, Christ imparts a heavenly element into His Body.

Not Earthly: Its supply has no earthly source, so no earthly power can cut it off.

What does the dew add to our understanding of the morning watch?Grace first.

Not Just a Practice: Rising early and reading can still be dry if the Lord does not visit us.

Grace Brings Food: When His fresh mercy waters us, the Word becomes manna.

Why does manna depend on a willingness for a new beginning?Daily, not yearly.

A Daily Turn: God gives each day as a fresh start; manna matches that rhythm.

Our Part: Telling the Lord we want to be different from yesterday opens us to receive Him.

How can a true revelation become tradition among us?Prescription vs. medicine.

Knowledge Only: Knowing the right teaching about manna or the mingled spirit does not feed us.

The Living Person: Seeking the Lord Himself as we read keeps fresh light coming.
